# Equipment slots

Each ship have 0~5 equipment slots where you can equip them.

## Equipment type and suited ships

[Implemented in Equipment::canEquip and FleetView::FleetView#equip_slots]

In this game all equipment slots are equal in terms of equipment that can be mounted. Disabling some types of equipment for specific slots (other than the extra slot) is not supported.

## Planes

[Implemented in ShipEquip] Unlike in KC where each slot position have a fixed number of plane slots, in this game you can freely assign planes to each slots. Different plane equipment still require different slots.

## Extra equipment slots

[Implemented in Equipment::canEquipEx and FleetView::FleetView#equip_slots] Automatically enabled above level 30
