# Labor condition

It is not beneficial to use the same ships 24/7.

### Condition loss

[Implemented in Server::conditionDrop] Each ship have a condition capped at 480 that will change by -5 per battle node (further +1 for S-victory, 0 for A-victory (and further -1 for each rank worse), -1 for night battle for day-battle-start nodes or day battle for night-battle-start nodes) when you send them on sortie/expedition.

### Condition gain

[Implemented in Server::minutePulse#recover_condition] If done by sortie(not expedition), the condition will only start to recover after 15 minutes (scaled to 60 if 0% HP) of rest. They recover by 1 per 3 minutes. No flagship or MVP condition bonus is planned.

[Implemented in ShipDisplay::setContent#display_cond:] Condition above 144 is displayed green, above 36 and below 144 is yellow, and below 36 is red.

[Implemented in Server::minutePulse#negative_condition_penalty] If the condition drops to below 0, ALL your ships will start losing experience as they hate you for overworking their brethren. This only applies to sortie though; if anyone in an expedition fleet have their condition drop to 0 the fleet will stop.
