# Labor condition

It is not beneficial to use the same ships 24/7.

Each ship have a condition capped at 480 that will drop by 1 per minute when you send them on sortie/expedition. The condition will only start to recover after 15 minutes (30 minutes if lightly damaged/45 if moderately damaged/60 if critically damaged) of rest. They recover by 1 per 2 minutes.

Condition above 60 is displayed green, above 15 and below 60 is yellow, and below 15 is red.

If the condition drops to below 0, ALL your kanmusu will start losing experience as they hate you for overworking their brethren.
