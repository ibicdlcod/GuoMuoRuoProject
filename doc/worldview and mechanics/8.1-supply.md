# Supply

### Normal supply[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED]

Each ship have a maximum oil (fuel consumption) or explosives (ammo consumption) cost which will affect the below calculations.

Each battle node have a specific percentage of oil and explosives cost which will be imposed on your ships during sortie. Whirlpool may cause losses as well, but its stochastic.

You must resupply ships (ideally after each sortie) in order to avoid disadvantage. Unlike in KC, this process CAN be made automatic (you don't have to).

Ships with either 100% oil or explosives loss cannot be sortied.

### Supply chain and attrition[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED]

If your sortie map is far from any [home port](5.2-homeport.md) then upon end of sortie your oil and explosives will suffer further losses.
$$
a=\prod_{i=1}^{n}s_i^{-1}-1
$$
where $s_i$ is (naval supremacy / expected supremacy) at any node on the route between any home port and the sortie map. Expected supremacy is the same as the supremacy that clearing the chosen difficulty will grant.

Any ship that sortied said map (including expedition) will AUTOMATICALLY lose oil and explosives at $a$ times the maximum.   If this may cause your resources to drop below 0, you will not be allowed to sortie said map beforehand. Beware that this does not prevent you from overlooking a bad but theoretically viable supply line and thus lose a lot of resources!

### Maintenance of planes[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED]

Since the game have no means to differentiate planes while your ship is in port because you can change planes on equip slots freely, your resources will automatically decrease proportional to your plane's maintenance cost (different from the develop cost, as that cost is very demanding for advanced equipment) and planes that were shoot down OR need maintenance (which have a chance to happen, more advanced ones need higher).

The reason that planes need maintenance is because highly advanced planes are more difficult to shoot down but their cost in resources should still outnumber the earlier, simpler ones.

If this may cause your resources to drop below 0 (calculated by 100% plane loss/maintenance, no matter what actually will happen), you will not be allowed to sortie said map beforehand.
