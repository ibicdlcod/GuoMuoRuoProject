# Experience and modernization

## Experience

### Ship level

[Implemented in Ship::getLevel]
$$
\text{Level}=\lfloor\frac{1+\sqrt{1+\frac{8x}{a}}}{2}\rfloor
$$
$a$: [rule/shipexpscacle](settings.md)

$x$: experience points

### Cap

Normal level is capped at 100 without decoration (TBD). That equates to 495000 exp when a is 100.

### Gain

[Implemented in Server::processExpGain#ship]

Via sortie (each sortie node have an individual fixed value)

| Battle Result | Experience gain relative to base |
| :-----------: | :------------------------------: |
|    Rank S     |                1x                |
|    Rank A     |               0.8x               |
|    Rank B     |               0.6x               |
|    Rank C     |               0.4x               |
|    Rank D     |               0.2x               |
|    Rank E     |                0                 |

[Implemented in Server::processExpGain#flagship_bonus] Flagship in a fleet have 2x this bonus (note this bonus will not apply if you don't put your first ship at position 0)

### Loss

[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED] Loss: Violating Labor conditions

### Effects

[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED] At Level 0 the ship has 50% of its total stats, at Level 100 the ship has 100%. Maximum stats (without modernization) is 120.71% of the base stats.
$$
\text{Efficiency} = \frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cdot\frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\space+(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}})m
$$

$x$: 100

$y$: actual level

$m$: modernization factor

total stats therefore cannot exceed 150%.

## Modernization

[Implemented in Server::modernize] Any ship can be modernized with ship blueprints of that same ship that otherwise will be wasted due to you already have said ship. A blueprint of later remodel level provides extra modernization points.

### Effects[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED]

$$
m=\frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}
$$

$m$: modernization factor

$y$: accumulated modernization points

$x$: [rule/equipmentstandardstar](settings.md)
