# Naval Supermacy

Each map have a naval supermacy value which ranges from 0% to 300% (it's -1% when you have not unlocked them)

Successfully defeating the BOSS node in a normal map sets its naval supermacy value to at least 100%/200%/300% (Early-war/Mid-war/Late-war difficulties S rank, A * 0.8, B * 0.6).

Naval supermacy will decay by [rule/navalsupermacydecay](settings.md) multiplied by your current naval supermacy each minute.

You are not allowed to play Mid-war/Late-war difficulty unless you already have 60%/120% naval supermacy there by playing Early-war/Mid-war difficulty.

### Resource gain

[This image](../map/map_resources.svg) describes the relation between land resources. For each land resource node, the average naval supermacy in related naval map will grant you appropriate percentage of resources in that land.

## Normal Expedition

As you are only sortieing one map at the same time, it's not practical to maintain naval supermacy by normal sortieing. Instead you can send a weaker expedition fleet to automatically fight for you, 15 minutes per node.

Expedition fleet does not have to fight BOSS node to maintain naval supermacy. Successfully completing the end node in a normal map sets its naval supermacy value to at least $a/2+50\%$/$a/2+100\%$/$a/2+150\%$ (Early-war/Mid-war/Late-war difficulties) where $a$ is your current naval supermacy.
