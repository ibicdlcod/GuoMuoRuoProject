# Gauge

In all maps you need to defeat the enemy flagship (position = 0) at boss node to clear it. This differs from KC in that non-gauge maps take only B-victory to clear.

[Implemented in User::isGaugeFinished] Most map have gauge bar of 0, meaning one battle with enemy flagship down is enough. Some maps and all DLC maps have non-zero gauge bar. The bar will decrease whenever boss flagship is damaged (differs from KC that non-event maps require flagship is sunk to decrease gauge), and when gauge bar is at or below 0, sinking enemy flagship clears the map.

[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED] The gauge bar can fall below 0 (minimum is -2147483647) due to either failing to defeat the flagship or repetition. Which will make this map easier and easier.
