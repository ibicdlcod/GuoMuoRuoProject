# Map

There are 86(TODO: add a Caspian Sea map for 87) normal maps in the base game, spanning all over the globe. Unlike in KC, maps are not grouped into sea zones.

Normal maps have three difficulty tiers: Early-war("C"), Mid-war("B") and Late-war("A").

## Battle nodes

Each map have a network of battle nodes with branching rules similar to KC. Unlike in KC there are no resource nodes, as clearing map itself will grant regeneration of resources. 

### Battle node types

[Implemented in KP::NodeType and lua/maps.lua#maps.Battle_type]

|               Type                |                          Properties                          |
| :-------------------------------: | :----------------------------------------------------------: |
|             Starting              |               Displayed as a rudder, no battle               |
|              Normal               |                        Normal battle                         |
|               Boss                | Boss battle (does not affect whether sortie ends; that is controlled by returning 0 in next node information) |
|               Empty               |                          no battle                           |
| Natural disaster<br />(whirlpool) | Have a probability (increased by LOS) of nothing happens; otherwise cause fuel and ammo loss |
|           Night-opener            | Unlike in KC, can always chase into day phase<br />Low LOS may mean certain high-level enemies may attack without fear of retaliation |
|            Night-Boss             |              Same as above, but is a boss node               |
|                Air                | Can order non-deployment of attack planes, which becomes anti-air battle; the boss node enemy will get reinforcements due to non-loss of this node though.<br />Enemy submarine may attack, and can be acttacked |
|             Transport             |             For unloading of transport material.             |



## Map relations

[Implemented in Server::clearMap]

[This image](../map/map.svg) describes the relation between maps. An edge means when you pass a map, you unlock the map at the other side when your fleet have ships of appropriate nationality (see below).
