## Ranking and Industrial Plant [NOTYETIMPLEMENTED]

You gain industrial points every month by ranking. It is calculated as following:
$$
ln(a)+(x-1)ln(x-1)-xlnx+1
$$

$a$ is total number of players in server

$x$ is your ranking (0*ln0 is 0).

This means all players gain on average 1.0 industrial points per month.

Industrial points can be used to purchase highly advanced equipment, irrespective of its tech requirements. If you try to gain equipment this way, maximum possessing limit in this case is global [rule/normalproductionstockpile](settings.md) rather than this equipment's specific attribute. This has the benefit of further increasing tech level and mitigate skill point loss from damaged equipment than normal means can achieve.
