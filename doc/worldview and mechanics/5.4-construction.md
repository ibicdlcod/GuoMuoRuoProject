# Construction

Unlike in KC when you specify resources and may get various ship depending on many factors, in this game you must specify an ship definition and construct for that specifically.

Each ship, in addition to its blueprint, have 1~5 initial equipment that must be provided when construct it. Unlike other equipment, carrier planes will not be consumed, only require you to possess it.

### Possess limit

Each ship (counting all remodel levels) can only have one present without using Cloning Vats.

### Success rate

100%

### Resource cost

$$
\text{Cost} = a\text{Round}(c(t+1))
$$

$a$: base cost, varies by ship type [Implemented in Shiptype::consResBase()]

$c$: [rule/devresscale](settings.md)

$t$: ship's tech level

### Construct time

[Implemented in Equipment::devTimeInSec]


$$
\text{Time(second)} = b\text{Round}(c(t+1))
$$
$b$: [rule/devtimebase](settings.md)
