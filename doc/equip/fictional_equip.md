# Fictional Equipment and Real-World Counterparts

Equipment items in this game that have no direct Wikidata identifier but are based on,
inspired by, or functionally related to real-world weapons and aircraft.

## Format

Each section lists fictional equipment and the real-world item it is derived from or
closely related to.  Where the real-world item has a Wikidata identifier it is noted.
