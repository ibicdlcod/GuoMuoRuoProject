# Game Balance Report

Generated by `CFServer --testbattle` on 2026-05-23.
All tests use lv=100 ships, identical player-supplied equipment (12cm main gun + 53cm torpedo for DDs; default equipment for CL/CA/BB), no skillpoint effects, no air power.

| 島風 | 1943 | 318 | 466 | 66 | 1008 | 902 | 249.2 | 71.1% | 15.5 | 302.5 / 318 |

Enemy hit rate: 14-16% (enemies die before firing many attacks).

**Assessment:**
- Tech progression is functional — 1943 ships deal ~50% more damage than 1922 ships.
- 島風 is the clear standout: highest speed (41 kt), evasion (1008), torpedo (902), and damage output (249).
- Marginal gains between 吹雪 (1928) and 夕雲 (1941) are small (~±10 Dmg, ±2% hit rate) — these classes feel interchangeable.
- No torpedo damage appears because extraBattle=false skips the disengaging-phase torpedo salvo.
- Gun cut-ins are essentially 0 (神風 got 5 in 500 runs; all others got 0).

**Suggestions:**
- Run with `extraBattle = true` to exercise torpedo mechanics.
- Differentiate mid-era DDs via equipment upgrades in default equip (better guns, sonar).
- Increase small-caliber gun primary stats or reduce the cut-in divisor from `/100` → `/50` to make DD cut-ins viable.

- Enemy amnesiac ships should have their own equipment that matches their tech level rather than relying solely on default equipment stats.
- Secondary gun accuracy base (currently 1000.0 multiplier, same as main gun at night) could use its own tuning — the doc doesn't differentiate secondary gun accuracy from main gun.

## 3. Cut-In Trigger Analysis

Formula (plain gunshot): triggerChance = a₁ × a₂
where aᵢ = max(0, (f+1)/2) × (p / hypot(1, p))
and p = primaryStat / divisor (100 for main gun, 250 for big-caliber, 50 for secondary gun)

With formation efficiency f ≈ 0.3, base = (0.3+1)/2 = 0.65.

| Gun type | Primary stat | p | aᵢ | Trigger per pair |
|----------|-------------|---|-----|-----------------|
| 12cm main | 15 DPM | 0.15 | 0.65×0.148=0.096 | 0.92% |
| 20.3cm CA | ~50 DPM | 0.50 | 0.65×0.447=0.290 | 8.4% |
| 35.6cm BB | ~100 DPM | 0.40 (÷250) | 0.65×0.371=0.241 | 5.8% |

Observations:
- DD cut-in trigger (0.92%) matches the observed ~0% cut-in rate across 500 runs.
- CA cut-in (~8%) is close to 古鷹's observed 3.6% + gun-torp rate.
- BB cut-in (~6%) under-predicts 扶桑's observed 16.8%. The difference likely comes from multiple main-gun pairs in 扶桑's default equipment (she has default equip 7 + 11 + secondary guns).

**Suggestions:**
- Halve the divisor for small-caliber guns (100 → 50) to make DD cut-ins trigger at ~3.7%, enough to be noticeable.
- The `/2` in the base formula `(f+1)/2` was recently changed from `/4`. Consider reverting to `/4` for big-caliber to avoid BB cut-ins being too frequent (扶桑's 16.8% may be too high for balance).

## 4. Armor Imbalance

Enemy amnesiac ships have disproportionately low armor compared to player ships of the same type:

| Ship | Player Armor | Amnesiac Armor | Ratio |
|------|-------------|----------------|-------|
| DD   | 55-70       | 43             | 1.3-1.6× |
| CL   | 72          | 42             | 1.7× |
| BB   | 186         | 139            | 1.3× |

This contributes to enemies dying quickly (avg final HP = 0 for DD and CL enemies).

**Suggestions:**
- Scale amnesiac armor to ~90% of equivalent player ship armor:
  - 駆逐ㄅ級Regular: 43 → 55
  - 軽巡ㄉ級Base: 42 → 65
  - 戦艦ㄐ級Base: 139 → 170
- Or increase amnesiac HP by +30-50%.

## 5. Formation Efficiency

Both test suites show friend formation efficiency ~0.28-0.34 and enemy ~-0.37 to -0.46.
The formulas rely on LOS, speed, and log-based terms.
With no planes, LOS is purely from ship stats — very low (5-50 range).
The `log(los/max(los, exp(-10)))` clamping introduced to fix NaN when LOS=0 keeps values stable.

No immediate issues identified. Formation efficiency values in the ±0.3-0.5 range are reasonable.

## 6. Overall Recommendations

### Battle Mechanics
1. **Enemy HP and armor need +30-50% buffs** — amnesiac ships die too fast across all ship types.
2. **DD cut-in divisor**: reduce from 100 → 50 for small-caliber guns to give DDs a visible cut-in mechanic.
3. **Secondary gun accuracy**: review whether the `1000.0` base multiplier is appropriate — secondary guns firing 2-5× faster than main guns should have lower per-shot accuracy.
4. **Evasion formula `2^{-b/c}` is working** — produces 66-89% hit rates for player ships vs appropriate evasion at lv=100. The formula produces lower hit rates than the old `e^{(a-e)/1000}`, which is intentional.
5. **Run torpedo tests with `extraBattle=true`** to validate torpedo damage formula and torpedo cut-in rates.

### Ship/Equipment Stats
1. **Mid-era DDs (吹雪 ← → 夕雲) are too similar** — consider equipment upgrades in default loadout to differentiate them.
2. **Amnesiac equipment may need Firingspeed values** — amnesiac guns lack Firingspeed, which affects reload timing.
3. **島風 is a well-balanced outlier** — high speed (41), evasion (1008), and torpedo (902) make her noticeably different from the pack. Other standout ships (秋月-class AA DD, 綾波改二等) could follow this pattern.

### Test Infrastructure
1. The `--repeat N --report out.md` pipeline works well for balance testing.
2. Multi-ship fleets in a single test file give clean per-ship comparisons.
3. Future tests should include: torpedo validation (extraBattle=true), heavy cruiser vs light cruiser comparison, AA cut-in validation, skillpoint effect validation.
