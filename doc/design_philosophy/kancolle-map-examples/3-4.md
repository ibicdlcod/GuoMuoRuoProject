# Kantai Collection Map 3-4 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 3‑4: 北方海域艦隊決戦 (Northern Waters Fleet Decisive Battle)** – A 9‑star difficulty map that serves as the capstone of World 3, featuring complex routing with multiple viable paths, heavy enemy battleship‑carrier combinations, and strategic whirlpool management.

### Purpose
- **Fleet Composition Mastery**: Tests players' ability to build fleets that balance combat power with routing requirements
- **Multi‑Route Strategy**: Offers 4+ distinct route strategies with different risk‑reward tradeoffs (south 2‑battle, north 3‑battle, 4‑battle, 6‑battle routes)
- **Whirlpool Risk Management**: Features both normal and "strong" whirlpools with potential 150% fuel loss, forcing radar consideration
- **Boss‑Access Precision**: Requires careful fleet building to avoid being diverted into dead‑end or excessive‑battle routes
- **Resource Economy**: Provides bauxite and furniture‑box farming opportunities on diversion routes
- **Northern Waters Finale**: Culmination of World 3 with the most powerful enemy fleet compositions yet

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (9 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 70‑90 ships (well‑equipped carriers, battleships, heavy cruisers)
- **Resource Cost**: Very high fuel/ammo consumption due to multiple combat nodes and potential whirlpool penalties
- **Repair Cost**: High risk of damage at nodes C (battleship vanguard), G (carrier task force), and P (boss)
- **EXP Range**: 160‑440 (boss node 420‑440)
- **HQ Level Scaling**: Not explicitly mentioned

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear 3‑3 ("アルフォンシーノ方面進出")
- **Note**: Often considered a "wall" for new players due to its difficulty and complex routing

   - **Type**: Furniture box node

   - **Strategic Value**: Side reward on diversion routes

#### Whirlpool Nodes
1. **Node D: うずしお (Whirlpool)**
   - **Fuel Loss**: Base 30% (actual 30%, maximum loss unconfirmed)
   - **Radar Reduction**: Each radar reduces loss (0 radars: ‑30%, 1 radar: ‑22.5%, 2 radars: ‑18%, 3 radars: ‑15%, 4 radars: ‑13.5%, 5 radars: ‑12.6%, 6 radars: ‑12%)
   - **Strategic Impact**: Only on south route; manageable with radars

2. **Node F: 強うずしお (Strong Whirlpool)**
   - **Fuel Loss**: Two patterns – 40% base (actual 24%) or **150%** base (actual 90%)
   - **Radar Reduction**: Same formulas applied to actual loss percentage
   - **Maximum Loss**: 70 fuel for strong whirlpool pattern
   - **Strategic Impact**: Can drain fuel to near‑zero if strong whirlpool triggers; major risk on north routes

### Route Strategies (from Player Guides)

1. **South 2‑Battle Route (DHLJP) – Recommended for Clearing**:
   - **Conditions**: (軽巡+駆逐+海防) ≥1, (戦艦級+空母系) =0, 駆逐 ≥3
   - **Combat Nodes**: D (whirlpool), H (heavy torpedo squadron), P (boss)
   - **Advantages**: Minimal combat (2 battles + whirlpool), no heavy enemy battleships/carriers in route
   - **Disadvantages**: Limited firepower (no battleships/carriers allowed), node H has dangerous torpedo cruisers

2. **North 3‑Battle + Whirlpool Route (ACFJMP) – Carrier‑Heavy**:
   - **Conditions**: (戦艦級+空母系) =2, 軽巡 ≥1, (駆逐+海防) ≥2, 高速+以上統一
   - **Combat Nodes**: A (patrol), C (battleship vanguard), F (strong whirlpool), J (fog), P (boss)
   - **Advantages**: Carrier firepower available, predictable routing with speed requirement
   - **Disadvantages**: Risk of strong whirlpool (150% fuel loss), battleship enemy at node C

3. **North 4‑Battle + Whirlpool Route (ACEGJP) – Balanced**:
   - **Conditions**: (戦艦級+空母系) =3‑4, (水母+補給艦) ≥1, 正規空母 ≤2
   - **Combat Nodes**: A, C, E (bauxite node), G (carrier task force), J, P
   - **Advantages**: Avoids strong whirlpool, gains bauxite resource, heavier fleet possible
   - **Disadvantages**: Extra combat node (G), requires seaplane tender/supply ship

4. **4‑Battle No‑Whirlpool Route (BHGJP) – Single Carrier**:
   - **Conditions**: (戦艦級+空母系) =1, (軽巡+駆逐) =1
   - **Combat Nodes**: B (patrol), H (heavy torpedo), G (carrier task force), J, P
   - **Advantages**: No whirlpool, can bring one carrier for air support
   - **Disadvantages**: Torpedo cruiser threat at H, carrier‑battleship enemy at G

5. **6‑Battle Route (ACBHGJP) – Avoid (Excessive Combat)**:
   - **Conditions**: (軽巡+駆逐+海防) =0 **or** 正規空母 ≥3 **or** (戦艦級+空母系) ≥5
   - **Combat Nodes**: A, C, B, H, G, J, P
   - **Advantages**: Maximum fleet freedom (any composition)
   - **Disadvantages**: 6 combat nodes with resource penalties; not viable for clearing

6. **"Pseudo‑6‑Carrier" Farming Route (ACEGJP) – Ranking Meta**:
   - **Composition**: Seaplane tender ×1, standard carriers ×2, light carriers ×2, light cruiser ×1
   - **Purpose**: High‑speed boss farming for ranking points; relies on opening airstrike to eliminate enemies before they act
   - **Requirements**: High‑level equipment (友永天山, 二式艦偵, etc.), extensive modernization

## Enemy Analysis

### Node A: 敵北方艦隊 哨戒部隊 (Enemy Northern Fleet Patrol Unit)
- **EXP**: 160‑190
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, escalating with more heavy cruisers and flagship variants
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 heavy cruiser Ri‑class flagship, 1‑2 light cruiser To/Ho‑class elite, 2‑3 destroyer Ro/I‑class
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Trapezoid (梯形)
- **Air Power**: None
- **Threat Level**: Low‑moderate – heavy cruiser flagships can deal significant damage if not neutralized

### Node B: 敵北方艦隊 哨戒部隊 (Enemy Northern Fleet Patrol Unit)
- **EXP**: 160‑190 (same as node A)
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, similar to node A
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 heavy cruiser Ri‑class flagship, 1‑2 light cruisers, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Trapezoid (梯形)
- **Air Power**: None
- **Threat Level**: Low‑moderate

### Node C: 敵北方艦隊 前衛部隊 (Enemy Northern Fleet Vanguard Unit)
- **EXP**: 180‑220
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, introducing battleship Ru‑class and light carrier Nu‑class
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 battleship Ru‑class (flagship/elite), 1 light carrier Nu‑class (elite/flagship), 1 light cruiser flagship, 2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Diamond (複縦)
- **Air Power**: Moderate (light carrier provides some air power)
- **Threat Level**: High – battleship Ru‑class can cripple ships; first appearance of combined arms

### Node G: 敵北方艦隊 機動部隊 (Enemy Northern Mobile Force)
- **EXP**: 300‑320
- **Patterns**: 2 variations, includes standard carrier Wo‑class flagship and multiple battleship Ru‑class
- **Typical Composition**: 1 standard carrier Wo‑class flagship, 1‑2 battleship Ru‑class (flagship/elite), 1 light cruiser flagship, 2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Diamond (複縦), Circular (輪形)
- **Air Power**: 28‑32 (enemy) / 42‑48 (required for air superiority) / 84‑96 (required for air supremacy)
- **Threat Level**: Very high – combined carrier‑battleship force; can destroy unprepared fleets

### Node H: 敵北方艦隊 重水雷戦隊 (Enemy Northern Heavy Torpedo Squadron)
- **EXP**: 200‑230
- **Patterns**: 4 variations, centered around torpedo cruiser Chi‑class
- **Typical Composition**: 1 light cruiser He‑class flagship, 3‑4 torpedo cruiser Chi‑class (flagship/elite), 1‑2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Diamond (複縦)
- **Air Power**: None
- **Threat Level**: High – torpedo cruisers deal massive opening torpedo damage; can cause instant retreats

### Node M: 敵北方艦隊 泊地防衛部隊 (Enemy Northern Anchorage Defense Unit)
- **EXP**: 250‑300
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, includes light carrier Nu‑class flagships
- **Typical Composition**: 1 heavy cruiser Ri‑class flagship **or** 1 battleship Ru‑class flagship, 2 light carrier Nu‑class flagship, 1 light cruiser flagship, 2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Diamond (複縦)
- **Air Power**: 46 (enemy) / 69 (required for air superiority) / 138 (required for air supremacy)
- **Threat Level**: High – multiple light carriers provide air power; battleship variant is particularly dangerous

### Node N: 敵北方艦隊 泊地防衛部隊 (Enemy Northern Anchorage Defense Unit)
- **EXP**: 250‑280
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, similar to node M
- **Typical Composition**: 1 heavy cruiser Ri‑class flagship **or** 1 battleship Ru‑class flagship, 2 light carrier Nu‑class flagship, 1 light cruiser flagship, 2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Diamond (複縦)
- **Air Power**: 46 (enemy) / 69 (required for air superiority) / 138 (required for air supremacy)
- **Threat Level**: High

### Node P (BOSS): ボス深海棲艦 北方艦隊中枢 (Boss Deep‑Sea Northern Fleet Core)
- **EXP**: 420‑440
- **Patterns**: 5 variations, the most powerful fleet in World 3
- **Typical Composition**: 2 standard carrier Wo‑class flagship, 1‑3 battleship Ru‑class flagship, 1‑2 torpedo cruiser Chi‑class flagship **or** 1 light cruiser He‑class flagship, 1‑2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦)
- **Air Power**: 28‑56 (enemy) / 42‑84 (required for air superiority) / 84‑168 (required for air supremacy)
- **Threat Level**: Extreme – double carrier Wo‑class flagships provide heavy air strikes; multiple battleship Ru‑class flagships deal devastating shelling
- **Objective**: Sink carriers and battleships for S‑rank; flagship is typically Wo‑class or Ru‑class

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. Multi‑Route Viability
- **Clear Strategic Options**: 5 distinct route strategies with different fleet requirements (south 2‑battle, north 3‑battle, 4‑battle, etc.)
- **Player‑Choice Emphasis**: Allows players to choose based on their fleet strengths (carrier‑heavy vs. torpedo‑focused vs. balanced)
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Routes range from "easy but restrictive" (south) to "hard but flexible" (north)

### 2. Whirlpool as Strategic Gatekeeper
- **Risk‑Reward Choice**: South route has predictable whirlpool (D); north routes risk catastrophic strong whirlpool (F)
- **Radar Investment**: Players must decide between bringing radars (reducing fuel loss) vs. combat equipment
- **Fuel Management**: Strong whirlpool can reduce fuel to near‑zero, crippling evasion and making boss fight extremely difficult

### 3. Composition Precision
- **Exact Counts Critical**: Many conditions require exact ship counts (e.g., "(戦艦級+空母系) =2", "軽巡 =1")
- **Educational Intent**: Reinforces careful fleet building and understanding of ship‑class categories
- **Anti‑Spam Design**: Prevents players from simply bringing 6 battleships/carriers (routes them into 6‑battle dead‑end)

### 4. Enemy Type Specialization
- **Node Specialization**: Different nodes feature specialized enemy types (torpedo cruisers at H, carriers at G, battleships at C)
- **Fleet Adaptation Required**: Players must prepare for different threat types depending on chosen route
- **Progressive Enemy Introduction**: Builds on enemy types introduced in earlier World 3 maps

### 5. Resource Farming Integration
- **Bauxite Recovery**: Node E provides bauxite to offset carrier‑heavy fleet consumption
- **Furniture‑Box Farming**: Nodes K and O provide currency rewards
- **Economic Viability**: Makes the map worth revisiting after clearance for resource gathering

### 6. "Wall" Map Design
- **Significant Difficulty Spike**: 9‑star difficulty represents a major jump from 3‑3 (7‑star)
- **Comprehensive Test**: Requires mastery of routing, composition, equipment, and resource management
- **Gatekeeping Function**: Separates mid‑game from late‑game players

### 7. Meta‑Strategy Emergence
- **"Pseudo‑6‑Carrier" Farming**: High‑level players developed optimized farming composition for ranking points
- **Community Knowledge Sharing**: Complex routing led to extensive community guides and strategy discussions
- **Equipment‑Dependent**: Success heavily depends on having proper equipment (radars, high‑end planes, etc.)

## Implementation Considerations for FleetMemories

### Core Mechanics to Replicate
1. **Multi‑Route Strategy**: Maintain 4‑5 viable route options with distinct fleet requirements
2. **Whirlpool Risk‑Reward**: Keep strong whirlpool with catastrophic potential but radar mitigation
3. **Composition Precision**: Exact ship counts should matter for routing but consider simplifying extreme cases
4. **Enemy Specialization**: Different node types should require different fleet preparations
5. **Resource Farming**: Include bauxite/furniture‑box nodes as side rewards
6. **Boss Difficulty Spike**: Boss should be significantly harder than previous World 3 maps

### Adaptation Suggestions
1. **Simplify Routing Complexity**: Consider reducing from 5 routes to 3‑4 primary routes while preserving strategic choice
2. **Adjust Whirlpool Severity**: Strong whirlpool 150% loss may be too punishing; consider 100% maximum
3. **Modify Exact Count Requirements**: Allow ranges (e.g., "1‑2 carriers" instead of "exactly 2 carriers") where possible
4. **Preserve Strategic Options**: Ensure both carrier‑heavy and carrier‑light routes remain viable
5. **Balance Enemy Strength**: Node G (carrier‑battleship) should be threatening but not impossible
6. **Maintain Farming Viability**: Keep resource nodes worthwhile but not essential for progression

### Balance Considerations
- **Difficulty Curve**: Position as significant step up from 3‑3 but not insurmountable
- **Resource Economy**: Whirlpool penalties should be meaningful but not game‑ending
- **Route Viability**: All primary routes should offer reasonable clear chances for intended fleet types
- **Equipment Requirements**: High‑end equipment should provide advantage but not be mandatory
- **Time Investment**: Clearing should require multiple attempts but not excessive grinding

### Learning Objectives to Preserve
1. **Fleet Composition Mastery**: Players must understand ship classes and their routing effects
2. **Risk‑Reward Evaluation**: Choosing between safe vs. risky routes with different rewards
3. **Resource Management**: Fuel/ammo conservation and radar usage decisions
4. **Enemy Type Adaptation**: Preparing fleets for different specialized enemy compositions
5. **Persistence Through Failure**: Map may require multiple attempts and adjustments
