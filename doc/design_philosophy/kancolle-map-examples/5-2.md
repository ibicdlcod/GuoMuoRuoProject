# Kantai Collection Map 5-2 Design Philosophy
## 珊瑚諸島沖海戦 (Coral Sea Naval Battle)

### Map Overview
**Purpose:** First carrier‑vs‑carrier battle map, introducing aerial combat and air raid mechanics in Southern Waters.

**Difficulty:** ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (9 stars) – high difficulty, requires strong air power and fleet composition planning.

**Unlock Requirements:**
- Clear map 4‑4 (カスガダマ島) and 5‑1 (南方海域前面)
- Teaches progression from Western Waters to Southern Waters carrier combat

**Notable Features:**
- **HP Gauge:** Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by 25% (4 sinks required to clear)
- **New combat types:** Introduces 航空戦マス (aerial combat nodes) and 空襲戦マス (air raid nodes)
- **Carrier‑centric design:** Enemy fleets feature strong carrier groups with extreme air power
- **Resource farming routes:** Specialized fleets can farm ammunition and bauxite via G/J nodes
- **Historical theme:** Based on the Battle of the Coral Sea, first carrier‑vs‑carrier naval battle

**BGM Changes:**
- Map: "艦隊の再集結"
- Route daytime: "飛龍の反撃"
- Boss daytime (before 2020‑03): "暁の水平線に（インストVer.）"
- Boss daytime (after 2020‑03): "鶴墜ちる海"

### Node Layout
Map features **15 combat nodes** (A‑O) plus start point, with complex branching based on fleet composition and detection.

**Key Node Types:**
- **A** – **Whirlpool** (うずしお) – fuel drain
- **B** – No combat (戦闘回避)
- **C** – **空襲戦** (Air Raid) – enemy carrier task force (機動部隊)
- **D** – Surface combat (敵任務部隊 随伴部隊) – transport ships and escorts
- **E** – Surface combat (敵任務部隊 機動部隊) – carrier‑battleship mixed force
- **F** – Surface combat (敵任務部隊 機動部隊) – carrier‑heavy force
- **G** – **Resource (Ammo)** +20‑50 ammunition, bonus with drum cans/landing craft
- **H** – No combat (敵影を見ず)
- **I** – **航空戦** (Aerial Combat) – enemy carrier main force (機動部隊本隊)
- **J** – **Resource (Bauxite)** +40‑80 bauxite, bonus with drum cans/landing craft
- **K** – Surface combat (敵任務部隊 随伴護衛戦隊) – escort squadron
- **L** – **空襲戦** (Air Raid) – enemy carrier task force
- **M** – **Strong Whirlpool** (強うずしお) – heavy fuel drain
- **N** – No combat (敵影を見ず)
- **O** – **Boss** (敵任務部隊 機動部隊本隊) – carrier main force

**Route Structure:**
Multiple routes with key decision points:
- **Start → A/B:** Based on battleship/carrier counts, submarine presence
- **C → D/E:** Based on carrier, heavy cruiser, destroyer counts, specific ship requirements
- **D/E → F/G:** Complex conditions involving祥鳳 (Shōhō) and夕張 (Yūbari)
- **F → H/I/O:** Detection score‑based branching with battleship/carrier count influences
- **I → J/O:** Random with祥鳳/夕張 influence
- **K → M/N:** Detection score and speed‑based branching
- **L → N:** Random (requires特定 conditions for N routing)

### Routing Rules (ルート分岐法則)
**Updated:** 2026‑01‑28

**Key Branching Conditions:**

1. **Start → A/B:**
   - **A if:** (戦艦級 + 空母系) ≥5 **OR** 戦艦級 ≥4 **OR** 正規空母 ≥3 **OR** contains潜水艦
   - **Otherwise B**

2. **C → D/E:**
   - **D if:** (空母系2隻 **AND** 重巡級2隻 **AND** 駆逐2隻)
   - **D if:** 夕張を含む **AND** (軽空母 + 重巡級 + 駆逐 + 補給艦) =5
   - **D if:** 祥鳳を含む **AND** (重巡級 + 軽巡 + 練巡 + 駆逐 + 補給艦) =5
   - **E if:** contains速力:低速 **OR** (戦艦級 **OR** 正規空母)を含む
   - **D if:** (翔鶴と瑞鶴の両方を含む **AND** 駆逐 ≥2) **OR** (軽空母2隻 **AND** 駆逐 ≥2) **OR** (軽空母1隻 **AND** 重巡級 ≥1 **AND** 駆逐 ≥2)
   - **Otherwise E**

3. **D/E → F/G:**
   - **G if:** 祥鳳と夕張の両方を含む **AND** other 4 ships meet: (駆逐2隻 **AND** (重巡2隻 **OR** 補給2隻))
   - **G if:** 祥鳳を含む **AND** other 5 ships meet: (駆逐3隻 **AND** 重巡1隻 **AND** (軽巡1隻 **OR** 補給1隻)) **OR** (駆逐3隻 **AND** 補給2隻)
   - **G if:** 夕張を含む **AND** other 5 ships meet: (駆逐5隻) **OR** (駆逐4隻 **AND** 補給1隻) **OR** (駆逐3隻 **AND** 補給2隻) **OR** (駆逐2隻 **AND** 補給2隻 **AND** 重巡1隻) **OR** (駆逐2隻 **AND** 補給1隻 **AND** 重巡2隻)
   - **Otherwise F**

4. **F → H/I/O:**
   - **H if:** 索敵スコア <63? (detection score)
   - **H/I random if:** 63? ≤ 索敵スコア <70 **AND** ((戦艦級 + 正規空母) ≥5 **OR** 戦艦級 ≥3 **OR** 空母系 ≥3)
   - **H/O random if:** 63? ≤ 索敵スコア <70 **AND** otherwise
   - **I if:** (戦艦級 + 正規空母) ≥5
   - **I/O random if:** 戦艦級 ≥3 **OR** 空母系 ≥3
   - **O if:** 索敵スコア ≥70

5. **I → J/O:**
   - **J‑biased random (55%?) if:** 祥鳳と夕張の両方を含む
   - **J‑biased random (85%?) if:** otherwise
   - **Note:** Flagging祥鳳 or 夕張 increases J probability by ~5%?; high‑speed+ unified adds ~5%?, fastest unified adds ~10%?

6. **K → M/N:**
   - **K/N random if:** 祥鳳と夕張のいずれも含まない **AND** (高速+以上統一 **OR** 索敵スコア ≥62)
   - **M/N random if:** 祥鳳と夕張のいずれも含まない **AND** 索敵スコア <60
   - **K/M random if:** 祥鳳と夕張のいずれも含まない **AND** 60 ≤ 索敵スコア <62
   - **K if:** 高速+以上統一 **OR** 索敵スコア ≥62
   - **M if:** 索敵スコア <60
   - **K/M random if:** 60 ≤ 索敵スコア <62

**Detection Score Notes:**
- **Branching coefficient:** 2 for this map
- **Detection score formula:** Standard detection calculation
- **Values shown:** For HQ level 120; lower HQ levels need +1 margin

**Speed Notes:**
- **Fastest unified:** All ships at fastest speed
- **High‑speed+ unified:** All ships at high‑speed+ or faster

### Design Patterns Observed

#### 1. **Carrier‑vs‑Carrier Battle Focus**
- First map centered entirely on carrier combat
- Introduces **aerial combat nodes** (航空戦マス) – shelling phase skipped, only air battles
- **Air raid nodes** (空襲戦マス) – player fleet cannot attack, only defend
- Teaches importance of fighter coverage and air defense

#### 2. **HP Gauge Mechanic**
- **Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by 25%** (4 sinks required to clear)
- Gauge does not reduce from damage – only flagship sink counts
- Encourages repeated engagement and boss‑focused strategies
- Prepares players for more complex gauge mechanics in later maps

#### 3. **Historical Ship‑Specific Routing**
- **祥鳳 (Shōhō)** and **夕張 (Yūbari)** enable special routing for resource farming
- **翔鶴 (Shōkaku)** and **瑞鶴 (Zuikaku)** together allow battleship inclusion while taking optimal route
- Teaches historical fleet composition significance
- Provides alternative strategies for different player ship collections

#### 4. **Detection‑Based Branching**
- **Detection score thresholds** (63?, 70) determine boss access
- **Branching coefficient 2** – detection calculation differs from standard maps
- Requires players to optimize detection equipment
- Introduces detection as critical routing factor

#### 5. **Resource Farming Route Design**
- Specialized fleets with祥鳳/夕張 can farm ammunition (G) and bauxite (J)
- **Low‑combat route:** C → D → G (→ J) with minimal enemy resistance
- Encourages players to build resource‑gathering fleets
- Provides alternative gameplay beyond boss clearing

#### 6. **Complex Conditional Branching**
- **Multiple overlapping conditions** at each branching point
- **Ship‑specific requirements** combined with class/count restrictions
- **Speed and detection interplay** for optimal routing
- Teaches advanced fleet composition planning

#### 7. **Enemy Progression Design**
- **Air raid nodes** (C, L) – moderate threat, teach air defense
- **Surface nodes** (D, E, F, K) – escalate from transports to carrier‑battleship groups
- **Aerial combat node** (I) – very high threat, pure air battle
- **Boss node** (O) – carrier‑focused with pattern escalation
- **Logical difficulty progression** through the map

#### 8. **Anti‑Submarine Considerations**
- **Submarine inclusion at start forces A route** – teaches submarine routing impact
- **D node includes submarine pattern** – requires ASW capability for certain routes
- **No submarine‑only nodes** – integrated into mixed fleets

### Conclusion
Map 5‑2 serves as the definitive carrier‑vs‑carrier battle map, introducing key mechanics like aerial combat nodes, air raid nodes, and HP gauges. The complex routing based on historical ships, detection scores, and fleet composition teaches advanced strategic planning. The resource farming routes provide alternative gameplay, while the escalating enemy difficulty prepares players for later Southern Waters challenges.

For FleetMemories, this map provides a template for carrier‑focused combat, complex conditional branching, historical ship integration, and alternative resource‑gathering gameplay. The aerial combat and air raid mechanics add tactical depth, while the HP gauge system introduces progression‑based map clearing.
