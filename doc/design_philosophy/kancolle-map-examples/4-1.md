# Kantai Collection Map 4-1 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 4‑1: ジャム島攻略作戦 (Jam Island Capture Operation)** – A 5‑star difficulty Western Waters introductory map focusing on anti‑submarine warfare, with branching based on battleship/carrier counts and destroyer/coastal defense ship composition.

### Purpose
- **Western Waters Introduction**: First map of World 4 (Western Waters), introducing new enemy types and mechanics
- **Anti‑Submarine Emphasis**: Features submarine combat nodes with explicit anti‑submarine warfare requirements
- **Composition‑Based Routing**: Clear branching based on battleship/carrier counts vs. destroyer/coastal defense ship composition
- **Multiple Viable Strategies**: Offers light fleet (destroyer‑heavy) and heavy fleet (battleship/carrier) route options
- **Resource Farming**: Contains resource nodes for fuel collection with drum can bonuses
- **Beginner‑Friendly Complexity**: Moderate difficulty (5 stars) with clear routing rules for new players

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆ (5 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 30‑50 ships with basic anti‑submarine equipment
- **Resource Cost**: Moderate fuel/ammo consumption
- **Repair Cost**: Low‑moderate risk of damage at nodes with elite enemy types
- **EXP Range**: 140‑380 (boss node 330‑380)
- **Enemy Threat Level**: Moderate, with elite torpedo cruisers and heavy cruisers as primary threats

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear 鎮守府海域 (World 1), 南西諸島海域 (World 2), and 北方海域3‑1 (Northern Waters 3‑1)
- **Progression**: Part of Western Waters operation sequence

## Routing Rules

### Ship‑Class Definitions (as of 2020/10/03)
- **戦艦級 (Battleship‑class)**: 戦艦 (battleships), 高速戦艦 (fast battleships), 航空戦艦 (aviation battleships)
  - Includes Гангут (Gangut) as fast battleship
  - "低速戦艦" (slow battleships) counted separately in internal data but grouped as 戦艦級 for routing
- **空母系 (Carrier‑class)**: 正規空母 (standard carriers), 装甲空母 (armored carriers), 軽空母 (light carriers)
  - 特設護衛空母 (special escort carriers) treated as 補給艦 (supply ships)
- **重巡級 (Heavy Cruiser‑class)**: 重巡洋艦 (heavy cruisers), 航空巡洋艦 (aviation cruisers)
- **軽巡級 (Light Cruiser‑class)**: 軽巡洋艦 (light cruisers), 練習巡洋艦 (training cruisers), **重雷装巡洋艦 (torpedo cruisers)**
- **海防艦 (Coastal Defense Ships)**: Includes 海防戦艦 (coastal defense battleships)
- **潜水艦 (Submarines)**: 潜水艦, 潜水空母 (submarine aircraft carriers)
- **補給艦 (Supply Ships)**: 補給艦, 特務艦, 灯台補給船, 南極観測船, 特設護衛空母, etc.

### Route Strategy Analysis (from Wiki Guide)
The wiki outlines three primary fleet compositions:

1. **Light Fleet Route (CFHJ or ABDHJ)**
   - Composition: (戦艦+空母)=0, 駆逐=4, (軽巡=1 OR 重巡級=0)
   - Path: Shortest route with 2 combat nodes (C→F→H→J) if starting at C
   - Risks: Vulnerable to torpedo accidents at C (雷巡チ級) and artillery accidents at H (重巡リ級flagship)

2. **Mixed Fleet Route (CFDHJ or ABDHJ)**  
   - Composition: (戦艦+空母)≤1, 重巡級=1, 軽巡=1, 駆逐=2
   - Path: Avoids G node (battleshipタ級 pair) but takes 3 combat nodes
   - Strategy: Use aviation battleships/cruisers with seaplanes for air superiority at boss

3. **Heavy Fleet Route (CFDGJ or ABDGJ)**
   - Composition: (戦艦+空母)=2, 駆逐≤1 plus others
   - Path: Confronts G node battleships with heavy firepower
   - Strategy: Use carrier opening strikes + torpedo cruiser pre‑emptive torpedoes to neutralizeタ級 before shelling

**Critical Notes:**
- (戦艦+空母)≥3 risks E node dead‑end (resource farming only, no boss access)
- (戦艦+空母)≥5 guarantees D→H→I loop (no boss access)
- Submarines strongly bias toward H node from D and cause random routing at H→I
- Destroyer/coastal defense ship count critical for D→G vs D→H branching

## Enemy Analysis

### Node A: 敵水雷戦隊 (Enemy Torpedo Squadron)
- **Purpose**: Light fleet combat introduction, torpedo cruiser threat training
- **Pattern 1 (140 EXP)**: 軽巡ホ級flagship, 雷巡チ級elite×2, 駆逐ロ級後期型, 駆逐イ級×2
- **Pattern 2 (150 EXP)**: 軽巡ホ級flagship, 雷巡チ級elite×3, 駆逐ロ級後期型×2  
- **Pattern 3 (160 EXP)**: 軽巡ホ級flagship, 雷巡チ級flagship, 雷巡チ級elite×2, 駆逐ロ級後期型×2
- **Threat Level**: Moderate‑High (torpedo cruiser pre‑emptive strikes dangerous for light fleets)

### Node C: 敵哨戒艦隊 (Enemy Patrol Squadron)
- **Purpose**: Basic surface combat, introduction to 重巡リ級flagship
- **Pattern 1 (150 EXP)**: 軽巡ヘ級flagship, 軽巡ヘ級elite×2, 駆逐ロ級後期型, 駆逐イ級×2
- **Pattern 2 (170 EXP)**: 軽巡ヘ級flagship, 軽巡ヘ級elite×2, 駆逐ロ級後期型, 駆逐イ級後期型×2
- **Pattern 3 (180 EXP)**: 重巡リ級flagship, 軽巡ヘ級elite×2, 駆逐ロ級後期型, 駆逐イ級後期型×2
- **Threat Level**: Moderate (リ級flagship artillery dangerous for under‑armored ships)

### Node D: 対潜戦 (Anti‑Submarine Combat)
- **Purpose**: Mandatory anti‑submarine warfare training
- **Resource Cost**: Normal consumption (not reduced like some ASW nodes)
- **Pattern 1‑6 (160‑190 EXP)**: 潜水カ級 (elite/standard) formations with light cruiser/destroyer escorts
- **最高 Pattern (190 EXP)**: 潜水カ級elite, 潜水カ級×3, 軽巡ホ級elite, 駆逐ロ級後期型
- **Threat Level**: Low‑Moderate (カ級elite torpedoes can damage un‑equipped ships)

### Node E: 敵増援強襲輸送船団 (Enemy Reinforcement Assault Transport Convoy)
- **Purpose**: Transport ship farming node (dead‑end for heavy fleets)
- **Pattern 1‑5 (300‑330 EXP)**: 輸送ワ級 (flagship/elite) with increasing battleshipル級 escort
- **最高 Pattern (330 EXP)**: 輸送ワ級flagship×2, 戦艦ル級flagship×2, 駆逐イ級後期型×2
- **Threat Level**: Low‑Moderate (battleship escorts dangerous but transports primary target)

### Node G: 東方艦隊 先遣戦艦部隊 (Eastern Fleet Advance Battleship Unit)
- **Purpose**: Heavy combat check for battleship/carrier fleets
- **Pattern 1‑3 (250‑300 EXP)**: 戦艦タ級 (elite/standard) with destroyer escorts
- **最高 Pattern (300 EXP)**: 戦艦タ級elite×2, 駆逐ロ級後期型×4
- **Threat Level**: High (タ級elite artillery can critically damage fleet)

### Node H: 敵深海連合巡洋艦戦隊 (Enemy Deep Sea Combined Cruiser Squadron)
- **Purpose**: Intermediate cruiser combat before boss
- **Pattern 1‑4 (200‑230 EXP)**: 重巡リ級flagship (1‑2), 軽巡ヘ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級 (flagship/elite), destroyers
- **最高 Pattern (230 EXP)**: 重巡リ級flagship×2, 軽巡ヘ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級flagship, 駆逐ロ級後期型×2
- **Threat Level**: Moderate‑High (concentrated cruiser firepower)

### Node J (BOSS): 敵深海連合部隊 司令部艦隊
- **Purpose**: Final boss of Western Waters 4‑1
- **Air Superiority Values**: 24/36/72 (advantage/air superiority/air supremacy)
- **Pattern 1‑6 (330‑380 EXP)**: 重巡リ級flagship为核心, escalating with 軽母ヌ級 (light carrier) additions
- **最高 Pattern (380 EXP)**: 重巡リ級flagship×2, 軽母ヌ級flagship, 軽巡ヘ級flagship, 駆逐ロ級後期型×2
- **Air Power**: Light carriers provide modest air power (24‑69 fighter power)
- **Threat Level**: High (flagship heavy cruisers + light carrier air strikes)

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. Western Waters Introduction Philosophy
- **Anti‑Submarine Emphasis**: World 4 begins with mandatory ASW node (D), teaching players to equip sonar/depth charges
- **New Enemy Types**: Introduces Western Waters‑specific enemies (タ級 battleships, リ級 heavy cruisers)
- **Moderate Difficulty Ramp**: 5‑star difficulty provides gentle introduction after World 3's Northern Waters challenges

### 2. Composition‑Based Branching Design
- **Clear Thresholds**: Battleship/carrier count (0, 1‑2, 3‑4, 5+) produces predictable routing
- **Destroyer‑Centric Light Route**: Rewards destroyer‑heavy compositions with shortest path
- **Anti‑Heavy Fleet Mechanics**: Heavy fleets (≥3 BB/CV) get dead‑ended at E (transport farming) or looped (≥5 BB/CV)
- **Submarine Routing Influence**: Submarines strongly bias toward H node and create randomness

### 3. Strategic Depth Through Multiple Viable Paths
- **Three Primary Strategies**: Light fleet (speed), mixed fleet (balance), heavy fleet (power)
- **Risk‑Reward Tradeoffs**: 
  - Light fleet: Fewer battles but vulnerable to torpedo accidents
  - Heavy fleet: More firepower but faces toughest enemy (タ級 battleships)
- **Resource Farming Integration**: Heavy fleets can intentionally route to E for transport farming

### 4. Teaching Advanced Mechanics
- **Air Superiority Management**: Boss node introduces light carrier air power requiring seaplane fighters
- **Combined Arms**: Node D teaches ASW with surface escort combat
- **Route Planning**: Clear composition rules teach players to plan fleet for specific paths

### 5. Progression Gates
- **Prerequisite Knowledge**: Requires completion of World 3‑1, ensuring players understand basic mechanics
- **Equipment Checks**: ASW node requires proper equipment, boss requires air power management
- **Fleet Building**: Encourages players to develop balanced fleets rather than relying on single ship type
