# Kantai Collection Map 7-5 Design Philosophy
**Operation Surabaya Naval Battle & Batavia Naval Battle** (スラバヤ沖海戦・バタビア沖海戦)

## Map Overview
- **Purpose**: **Triple‑gauge monthly Extra Operation** serving as a **tutorial for event‑map mechanics** – introduces **historical‑ship damage bonuses**, **multi‑stage gauge progression**, **PT‑boat/land‑based boss combinations**, and **gimmick‑based third‑gauge unlock**. Tests fleet versatility across anti‑air, anti‑submarine, surface combat, and anti‑ground/PT specialties.
- **Difficulty**: ☆☆(☆×11) (**13 stars total** – highest difficulty in normal海域)
- **Unlock Requirements**: **7‑4 clear** (once cleared, accessible monthly without re‑clearing 7‑4)
- **HP Gauges & Progression**:
  1. **First Gauge (K node)**: Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by **½ (50%)**; **2 sinks destroy gauge**, unlocking **L–Q nodes**
  2. **Second Gauge (Q node)**: **Land‑type boss flagship destruction** reduces gauge by **⅓ (33.3%)**; **3 sinks destroy gauge**
  3. **Third Gauge (T node)**: Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by **⅓ (33.3%)**; **3 sinks clear map**
- **Gimmick**: **M node S‑Rank victory once** unlocks **R–T nodes** (third‑gauge route)
- **Monthly Rewards** (time‑limited):
  - **Medal ×1**: Must clear before **month‑end 23:59**
  - **Special War Results +170**: Must clear before **month‑end 21:59**
  - After time limit, rewards unavailable; gauge resets on login refresh
- **Restrictions**: **No Land‑Based Air Support (LBAS)**, **no Support Expeditions** – pure fleet strength required
- **BGM**:
  - Map: 「ダバオ沖の夕雲型」
  - Route combat: 「水雷戦隊の反撃」
  - Boss battle: 「激闘！第三次ソロモン海戦」

## Node Layout
```
Three‑phase progression:
Phase 1 (first gauge): Start → A → (B/C) → (D/E) → (F) → (G/H) → (K: boss1)
Phase 2 (second gauge): After first‑gauge clear, L–Q nodes open → (L/M/N) → (O) → (Q: boss2)
Phase 3 (third gauge): After M‑node S‑victory gimmick, R–T nodes open → (R/S) → (T: boss3)
```
**Key Routes**:
- **Shortest route (all phases)**: A‑B‑D‑F‑G‑H‑K (first gauge) → A‑B‑D‑F‑J‑O‑Q (second gauge) → A‑B‑D‑F‑J‑O‑P‑T (third gauge)
- **Speed‑based routing**: **Fastest+ unified** (最速統一) allows any composition to reach all bosses shortest; **Fast+ unified** (高速+統一) may block third‑gauge shortest
- **Gimmick route**: Must pass **M node** (軽巡ヘ級改flagship) for S‑victory to unlock third gauge

**Node Types**:
- **A**: **Air Raid** – Deep‑Sea Base Air Wing Java Deployment (飛行場姫 variants, air power 48–335)
- **B**: **Anti‑Submarine** – Macassar Strait Deep‑Sea Patrol Group (潜水カ級elite/flagship, 3–5 subs)
- **C**: **Surface Combat** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Destroyer Division (駆逐イ級後期型/ロ級後期型elite)
- **D**: **Surface Combat** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Vanguard (軽巡ホ級/ヘ級flagship + destroyers)
- **E**: **PT‑Boat Combat** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Patrol Boat Unit (駆逐イ級 + PT小鬼群 A–D)
- **F**: **Active Branching** (能動分岐) – no combat
- **G**: **Surface Combat** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Alert Unit (軽巡ヘ級flagship + destroyers)
- **H**: **Active Branching** – no combat
- **I**: **No Combat** – “敵影を見ず”
- **J**: **Surface Combat** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Java Defense Line (重巡リ級flagship + 軽巡ヘ級flagship + 駆逐ロ級後期型elite, later patterns add 軽母ヌ級elite)
- **K (Boss1)**: **Light Cruiser T‑class Flagship** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Main Force (軽巡ト級elite/flagship + 重巡リ級elite/flagship + 軽巡ツ級 + destroyers)
- **L**: **Surface+Sub** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Guerrilla Force (駆逐イ級後期型 + 潜水カ級elite)
- **M (Gimmick)**: **Surface Combat** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Escape Remnant (軽巡ヘ級改flagship A/B + destroyers)

- **Q (Boss2)**: **Land‑Based + PT Boss** – Deep‑Sea Combined Fleet Kragan Fortress (砲台小鬼 + 飛行場姫 + 集積地棲姫II + PT小鬼群 + 輸送ワ級)

## Routing Rules
**Key Branching Conditions** (speed‑based, composition, LOS):

### Start → B/C
- **→C if**: **Fast+ unified or faster** (高速+以上統一); OR **Regular Carriers ≥2**; OR **Slow Battleships ≥2**; OR **contains Submarine**; OR **Light Cruisers =0**; OR **(Destroyer + Escort) ≤1**; OR **Regular Carrier ≥1**; OR **Light Carriers ≥2**; OR **Battleship‑class ≥3**; OR **Heavy‑Cruiser‑class ≥3**
- **→B otherwise**

### C → D/E
- **→E if**: **Fastest unified** (最速統一); OR **Regular Carriers ≥2**; OR **Carrier‑class ≥3**; OR **(Battleship‑class + Regular Carrier + Heavy‑Cruiser‑class) ≥3**; OR **contains Submarine**; OR **(Light Cruiser + Destroyer) =0**
- **→F if**: **Fast+ unified** (高速+統一); OR **Battleship‑class =2 AND** (Fast unified AND (Destroyer + Escort) ≥2) OR ((Destroyer + Escort) ≥3); OR **(Light Cruiser + Destroyer + Escort) ≥2**
- **Otherwise →E**

### F → G/H
- Always **→G**

### G → H/J
- **Active branching** → **J**

### H → I/K
- **Active branching** → **K**

### I → L
- **→L if**: **LOS score <52**
- **→L or M (random) if**: **LOS 53–58**
- **→M if**: **LOS ≥59** (branch coefficient =4)

### J → N/O
- **→O if**: **Light Carrier =1 AND Heavy‑Cruiser‑class =2 AND Light Cruiser =1 AND (Destroyer + Escort) =2 exactly**; OR **Fast+ unified or faster** (高速+以上統一); OR **(Destroyer + Escort) ≥3**; OR **Fast unified** (高速統一)
- **→N if**: **Regular Carrier ≥1**; OR **Light Carriers ≥3**; OR **Slow Battleships ≥2**; OR **(Battleship‑class + Heavy‑Cruiser‑class) ≥3**; OR **(Destroyer + Escort) ≤1**
- **Otherwise →O**

### O → P/Q
- **Active branching** → **Q**

### P → R/S/T
- **→S fixed if**: **LOS <58**
- **→S random if**: **LOS 58–63**
- **→R or T based on composition if**: **LOS ≥63 OR random gave non‑S**:
  - **→T if**: **Fastest unified** (最速統一)
  - **→R if**: **Regular Carrier ≥1**; OR **(Battleship‑class + Light Carrier) ≥2**; OR **(Battleship‑class + Heavy‑Cruiser‑class) ≥3**; OR **Light Cruisers =0**
  - **Otherwise →T**

**LOS Notes**:
- **Branch coefficient**: **4**
- **LOS scores for HQ Level 120**; for HQ <120, **add +1 margin**
- **Speed categories**: **Fastest unified** (最速統一) > **Fast+ unified** (高速+以上統一) > **Fast unified** (高速統一) > **Slow**

## Enemy Analysis
**Air‑Raid Node (A)**:
- **Patterns 1‑4**: 飛行場姫 variants (陸爆中, 鳥黒弱/強) – air power 48–335, radius 5
- **High air power but low damage** – anti‑air cut‑in recommended but not essential

**Submarine Nodes (B, N)**:
- **B**: 3‑5 潜水カ級elite/flagship (EXP 120–150)
- **N**: 3‑7 潜水カ級elite/flagship (EXP ?–150)
- **Requires multi‑ship ASW** (1‑slot pre‑emptive ASW sufficient)

**Surface Nodes (C, D, G, J)**:
- **C**: 駆逐イ級/ロ級後期型elite (6‑ship destroyer divisions)
- **D**: 軽巡ホ級/ヘ級flagship + destroyers
- **G**: 軽巡ヘ級flagship + destroyers
- **J**: 重巡リ級flagship + 軽巡ヘ級flagship + 駆逐ロ級後期型elite (later patterns add 軽母ヌ級elite)

**PT‑Boat Node (E)**:
- **Patterns 1‑4**: 駆逐イ級 + PT小鬼群 (A–D)
- **Requires anti‑PT equipment** (small‑caliber guns, armed landing craft, machine guns)

**Boss1 (K) – Light Cruiser T‑class**:
- **Patterns 1‑2**: 軽巡ト級elite/flagship + 重巡リ級elite/flagship + 軽巡ツ級 + destroyers
- **Final Forms (3‑4)**: upgraded versions with 軽巡ツ級elite/駆逐ロ級後期型
- **High anti‑air firepower** (can trigger anti‑air cut‑in), **high accuracy**
- **Airpower zero** – one seaplane fighter secures air supremacy

**Boss2 (Q) – Land‑Based + PT**:
- **Patterns 1‑4**: 砲台小鬼 + 飛行場姫(陸爆中) + 集積地棲姫II(B/壊) + PT小鬼群(C/D) + 輸送ワ級(elite)
- **Final Forms (5‑6)**: upgraded land installations
- **Requires anti‑ground and anti‑PT equipment**; **no LBAS support**
- **Fixed轮形 formation**, land‑type bosses not protected by escort

**Boss3 (T) – Batavia Anchorage Princess**:
- **Patterns 1‑2**: バタビア沖棲姫(A/B) + 軽巡ヘ級flagship + 軽巡ツ級 + destroyers
- **Final Forms (3‑4)**: バタビア沖棲姫‑壊(A/B) + 軽巡ツ級elite/駆逐ロ級後期型elite
- **Post‑clear patterns (5‑6)**: reduced escort count (4‑ship fleet)
- **High armor** (233 pre‑final, 244 final at HQ120), **treated as battleship internally** (day‑double‑attack possible without battleships)
- **Airpower zero** – seaplane fighter suffices

## Design Patterns Observed
1. **Triple‑Gauge Monthly EO**:
   - **Three distinct bosses** (surface cruiser, land+PT, heavy princess) test **different fleet specializations**
   - **Gimmick‑based unlock** (M‑node S‑victory) mimics event‑map mechanics
   - **Monthly time‑limited rewards** (Medal + Special War Results) encourage early‑month completion

2. **Historical‑Ship‑Bonus Tutorial**:
   - **Extensive list of historically‑relevant ships** with **node‑specific damage multipliers**
   - **Teaches event‑map concept of "historical routing/damage bonus"** in a controlled environment
   - **Bonus multipliers modest** (1.06–1.15) – noticeable but not mandatory

3. **Speed‑Based Routing Complexity**:
   - **Four speed categories** (最速統一, 高速+以上統一, 高速統一, 低速) with **strict routing consequences**
   - **Fastest unified allows any composition shortest route** – rewards investment in high‑speed ships
   - **Fast+ unified blocks third‑gauge shortest** – forces trade‑offs between fleet power and routing

4. **Multi‑Role Fleet Requirement**:
   - **Anti‑air** (node A), **ASW** (B, N), **surface combat** (C, D, G, J), **anti‑PT** (E), **anti‑ground** (Q), **high‑armor boss killing** (T)
   - **No LBAS/support** – pure fleet strength and versatility tested
   - **Progressive gauge design** allows fleet adjustment between phases

5. **PT‑Boat & Land‑Based Boss Integration**:
   - **Boss2 combines land installations + PT boats** – requires **dual‑purpose anti‑ground/PT equipment**
   - **No LBAS support** forces **on‑fleet anti‑ground solutions**
   - **Fixed轮形 formation** prevents escort protection – land bosses vulnerable to direct attacks

6. **High‑Armor Final Boss with Escort Reduction**:
   - **Batavia Anchorage Princess** has **233–244 armor** (event‑level difficulty)
   - **Post‑clear patterns reduce escort count** (4‑ship) for farming
   - **Battleship‑internal classification** allows day‑double‑attack without battleships

7. **Tutorial‑Level Simplicity in Complex Framework**:
   - **Short gauge lengths** (2/3/3 sinks) – less repetitive than other EOs
   - **Single gimmick node** (M) – simple unlock mechanic
   - **Predictable routing** with clear speed/composition rules – learnable patterns

## Implementation Notes for FleetMemories
- **Triple‑Gauge Progression**: Implement **sequential gauge unlock** with **intermediate node unlocks** (L–Q after first gauge, R–T after gimmick)
- **Historical Ship Bonuses**: Add **node‑specific damage multipliers** for historically‑relevant ships based on real‑world battle participation
- **Speed‑Category Routing**: Use **four‑tier speed classification** (Fastest, Fast+, Fast, Slow) with **branching penalties for lower categories**
- **Land‑Based + PT Boss**: Design **combined land/PT bosses** requiring **anti‑ground and anti‑PT equipment**, with **fixed formation preventing escort protection**
- **Gimmick‑Based Unlock**: Include **single‑node S‑victory requirement** to unlock final phase – simple but meaningful gate
- **Monthly Time‑Limited Rewards**: Offer **Medal + Special War Results** with **strict monthly deadlines** to drive early‑month engagement
- **No LBAS/Support Restrictions**: Create **pure‑fleet challenge maps** where external support is disabled, testing fleet self‑sufficiency
- **Post‑Clear Escort Reduction**: After gauge clear, **reduce boss escort count** for farming efficiency while maintaining core challenge
