# Kantai Collection Map 2-5 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 2-5: 沖ノ島沖戦闘哨戒 (Okinoshima Offshore Combat Patrol)** - An 8-star difficulty high‑level map focused on cruiser‑centric fleet composition, complex LOS‑based routing, and strategic resource management with time‑limited rewards.

### Purpose
- **High‑Difficulty Patrol Mission**: Tests advanced fleet composition skills with cruiser‑focused requirements
- **Resource Farming**: Primary source of fuel and steel via resource nodes
- **Time‑Limited Rewards**: Special‑time rewards (特別戦果) and medals with monthly deadlines
- **LOS‑Based Routing Education**: Teaches importance of LOS (索敵スコア) in route selection
- **Cruiser Fleet Proficiency**: Emphasizes cruiser‑type ships over battleship‑carrier heavy fleets

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (8 stars, "advanced cruiser challenge")
- **Recommended Level**: Level 70‑90 ships with well‑developed equipment
- **Resource Cost**: Moderate‑high fuel/ammo consumption, with whirlpool losses
- **Repair Cost**: Significant damage expected at combat nodes
- **EXP Range**: 120‑220 (boss node 200‑220)
- **Time Limits**: 
  - Special rewards: Until month‑end 21:59
  - Medals: Until month‑end 23:59
  - After deadlines: Map resets on relogin, no rewards

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear World 2‑4
- **No special requirements**: Open after completing 2‑4

## Route Branching Rules (ルート分岐法則)

### Key Class Definitions
- **戦艦級 (Battleship‑class)**: Fast Battleships, (Slow) Battleships, Aviation Battleships
- **空母系 (Carrier‑type)**: Standard Carriers, Armored Carriers, Light Carriers
- **軽巡級 (Light Cruiser‑class)**: Light Cruisers, Training Cruisers, **Torpedo Cruisers** (updated 2022/11/13)
- **重巡級 (Heavy Cruiser‑class)**: Heavy Cruisers, Aviation Cruisers
- **水母 (Seaplane Tender)**: Seaplane Tenders
- **潜水母艦 (Submarine Tender)**: Separate from submarines
- **補給艦 (Replenishment Ships)**: Fleet Oilers, Special Duty Ships, etc.
- **海防艦 (Coastal Defense Ships)**: Coastal Defense Ships, includes 海防戦艦

### Detailed Branching Conditions (Priority: Higher conditions take precedence)

**Start → B/C**
- **Submarine ≥4** → B
- **Submarine ≥1 AND Battleship‑class ≤3** → Random (carrier‑type incl. → random; seaplane tender ≥2 → random)
- **Carrier‑type included** → C
- **Seaplane tender ≥2** → C
- **Drum‑canister carrying ships ≥2** → B
- **(Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≥4** → B
- **Light Cruiser ≥1 AND (Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≥3** → B
- **Battleship‑class included** → C
- **(Light Cruiser + Torpedo Cruiser) ≥1 AND Aviation Cruiser ≥2** → C
- **(Light Cruiser + Torpedo Cruiser) ≥1 AND Aviation Cruiser ≥1 AND (Heavy Cruiser‑class + Light Cruiser + Torpedo Cruiser) ≥5** → C
- **6‑ship fleet** → Random with 80% bias toward B
- **Otherwise** → Random with strong bias toward C

**B → A/F**
- **Submarine ≥3** → A
- **Otherwise** → F

**C → D/E**
- **Carrier‑type ≥3** → D
- **Battleship‑class ≥3** → D
- **Light Cruiser ≥1 AND Destroyer ≥2** → E
- **Aviation Cruiser ≥2 AND Destroyer ≥2** → E
- **Otherwise** → Random with 70% bias toward E

**E → G/I**
- **Battleship‑class included** → G
- **Light Cruiser ≥1 AND (Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≥4** → I
- **Includes slow speed (低速)** → G
- **(Standard Carrier + Heavy Cruiser‑class) ≥2** → G
- **Light Cruiser ≥1 AND Destroyer ≥3** → I
- **Otherwise** → G

**F → E/J**
- **Includes slow speed (低速)** → J
- **Destroyer ≥3** → E
- **Light Cruiser ≥1 AND Destroyer ≥2** → E
- ~~Battleship‑class ≥3 → J~~ (crossed out)
- **Otherwise** → Random with 65% bias toward J

**G → I/K/L** (CRITICAL LOS‑BASED BRANCHING)
- **(Battleship‑class + Carrier‑type) ≤1 AND (Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≥4** → I
- **(Battleship‑class + Carrier‑type) =0 AND Light Cruiser ≥1 AND Destroyer ≥3** → I
- **LOS score <37?** → K
- **LOS score 37?‑41?** → Random between K/L
- **LOS score ≥41?** → L

**I → O/H** (LOS‑BASED)
- **LOS score <31** → H
- **LOS score 31‑34** → Random
- **LOS score ≥34** → O

**J → H/M/O** (COMPLEX LOS & HEAVY SHIP ROUTING)
- **LOS score <42?** → H
- **LOS score 42?‑49?**:
  - **(Battleship‑class + Carrier‑type) ≥4** → Random
  - **Otherwise** → Random between H/O
- **(Battleship‑class + Carrier‑type) ≥4** → Random between M/O
- **LOS score ≥49?** → O

**L → N/O**
- **Includes Light Cruiser AND Destroyer ≥2** → O
- **(Battleship‑class + Carrier‑type) =0** → Random with 60% bias toward O
- **Otherwise** → Random with 60% bias toward N

### LOS (索敵スコア) Mechanics
- **Branching coefficient**: 1 for this map
- **LOS score calculation**: Use standard LOS formula (索敵スコア計算式)
- **Critical thresholds**:
  - **34**: I→O branch (safe node access)
  - **37?**: G→K/L branch
  - **41?**: G→L branch (fixed)
  - **42?**: J→H branch threshold
  - **49?**: J→O branch (safe node access, high requirement)

### Strategic Implications
**Three Main Route Groups**:
1. **North Route (Submarine/Cruiser Focus)**: Start→B→F→J→...
   - Pros: Avoids carrier‑heavy nodes, uses submarine advantage
   - Cons: High LOS requirements at J (≥49 for safe node)

2. **Central Route (Carrier/Battleship Heavy)**: Start→C→D/E→...
   - Pros: Carrier‑battleship power, fixed routing with heavy ships
   - Cons: Resource consumption, may hit dead‑ends

3. **South Route (Mixed Fleet)**: Various paths with LOS‑dependent branching
   - Pros: Flexible compositions
   - Cons: Unpredictable routing, high LOS requirements

**Critical Design Elements**:
- **LOS‑Based Routing**: Multiple branches depend on LOS score (索敵スコア), teaching equipment optimization
- **Cruiser‑Centric Design**: Encourages cruiser‑based fleets over battleship‑carrier dominance
- **Time‑Limited Rewards**: Monthly deadlines create urgency and farming patterns
- **Whirlpool Management**: Radar equipment reduces losses, teaching utility equipment value

## Design Philosophy Analysis

### Role in Game Progression
**Advanced Cruiser Challenge**: Following the "beginner's wall" of 2‑4, this map tests advanced fleet composition skills with cruiser‑centric requirements and complex LOS‑based routing.

**Resource Management Test**: Introduces time‑limited rewards (monthly deadlines) and significant resource node mechanics (whirlpool with radar mitigation).

**LOS Education**: Multiple routing branches depend on LOS scores, teaching players to optimize equipment for reconnaissance rather than just combat power.

### Strategic Intent
**Cruiser‑Focused Design**: Deliberately discourages battleship‑carrier heavy fleets through routing restrictions, encouraging balanced cruiser‑based compositions.

**Time‑Pressure Mechanics**: Monthly deadlines create urgency and strategic planning around resource farming schedules.

**Equipment Utility Demonstration**: Radar's whirlpool mitigation shows non‑combat equipment value, expanding strategic thinking.

**Complex Routing Mastery**: Requires understanding of multiple simultaneous routing factors: ship type counts, LOS scores, speed classifications.

### Notable Design Features
**LOS‑Based Branching**: One of the most complex LOS‑routing systems in early‑game maps, with multiple threshold‑based branches.

**Monthly Time Limits**: Unique reward structure with hard deadlines, encouraging regular play and strategic timing.

**Whirlpool with Equipment Mitigation**: Advanced version of whirlpool mechanic introduced in 2‑4, with more significant losses and radar‑based reduction.

**Three‑Tier Route System**: Distinct submarine/cruiser, carrier/battleship, and mixed fleet paths accommodate different playstyles and resources.

**Cruiser‑Centric Enemy Compositions**: Enemy fleets feature cruiser‑type enemies (Ri‑class heavy cruisers, Chi‑class torpedo cruisers) matching player fleet expectations.

### Map‑Specific Mechanics
**Route‑Based Trade‑offs**:
- **North Route**: Submarine/cruiser advantage vs. high LOS requirements
- **Central Route**: Carrier/battleship power vs. resource consumption and dead‑end risk
- **South Route**: Flexibility vs. unpredictability and LOS dependence

**LOS Threshold System**: Multiple critical LOS values (31, 34, 37?, 41?, 42?, 49?) create equipment optimization challenges.

**Time‑Limited Farming**: Monthly deadlines create strategic farming patterns and reward timing optimization.

**Radar Utility Demonstration**: Whirlpool mitigation teaches radar's non‑combat value beyond LOS contribution.

### Overall Design Goal
An advanced‑difficulty map that tests cruiser fleet proficiency, LOS optimization, and strategic timing. It serves as a bridge between the "beginner's wall" of 2‑4 and the more complex world 3 maps, focusing on equipment utility and fleet composition balance rather than raw firepower.

The monthly time limits create ongoing engagement while the complex routing teaches advanced game mechanics. The map reinforces that Kantai Collection is as much about strategic preparation and equipment optimization as it is about combat power.

## Implementation Considerations for FleetMemories

### Core Mechanics to Replicate
1. **LOS‑Based Routing**: Implement multiple branching conditions based on LOS scores with coefficient 1
2. **Cruiser‑Centric Design**: Routing restrictions that favor cruiser‑type ships over battleship‑carrier heavy fleets
3. **Time‑Limited Rewards**: Monthly deadlines for special rewards and medals
4. **Whirlpool with Radar Mitigation**: Fuel loss mechanic with equipment‑based reduction
5. **Complex Enemy Variety**: Multiple patterns with increasing difficulty across nodes

### Adaptation Suggestions
1. **Simplify LOS Complexity**: Reduce number of LOS thresholds while maintaining strategic importance
2. **Adjust Time Limits**: Convert monthly deadlines to weekly or event‑based for FleetMemories' pacing
3. **Modify Enemy Types**: Use equivalent enemy types from FleetMemories' roster
4. **Maintain Cruiser Focus**: Keep routing restrictions that encourage cruiser‑based fleets
5. **Scale Resource Values**: Adjust whirlpool losses and resource node yields to FleetMemories' economy

### Balance Considerations
- **Difficulty Curve**: Position as advanced challenge after 2‑4, before world 3 maps
- **LOS Implementation**: Ensure LOS calculation is transparent and understandable to players
- **Time Pressure**: Make deadlines meaningful but not overly punitive
- **Route Viability**: Ensure all three route groups remain viable with different player resources

### Learning Objectives to Preserve
1. **LOS Importance**: Players must learn that reconnaissance affects routing as much as combat power
2. **Equipment Utility**: Radars have both LOS and resource‑protection functions
3. **Cruiser Proficiency**: Balanced cruiser fleets can be more effective than heavy battleship‑carrier fleets
4. **Strategic Timing**: Time‑limited rewards require planning and regular engagement
5. **Complex Routing Mastery**: Multiple simultaneous factors affect route selection
