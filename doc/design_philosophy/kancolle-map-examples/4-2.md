# Kantai Collection Map 4-2 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 4‑2: カレー洋制圧戦 (Curry Ocean Suppression Battle)** – A 6‑star difficulty Western Waters map with dual‑route structure (north/south), requiring 7‑1 clearance for access, featuring resource collection nodes (steel/bauxite) and complex composition‑based routing with destroyer‑count dependent branching.

### Purpose
- **Western Waters Progression**: Second map of World 4, increasing difficulty from 4‑1 with more complex routing
- **Dual‑Route Design**: Clear north/south route separation with different enemy compositions and challenges
- **Resource Collection Emphasis**: Introduces steel and bauxite collection nodes with drum/landing craft bonuses
- **Advanced ASW Mechanics**: Features two distinct anti‑submarine nodes with different resource consumption patterns
- **Fleet Composition Testing**: Requires careful balancing of destroyer count for routing vs. combat effectiveness
- **7‑1 Prerequisite Gate**: First map requiring clearance of a World 7 map (7‑1), enforcing progression across different ocean areas
- **Transport Ship Farming**: Both G node and boss node feature transport ships for "ろ号" mission farming

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆ (6 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 50‑70 ships with good anti‑submarine and air superiority capabilities
- **Resource Cost**: Moderate‑high, with variable consumption at ASW nodes
- **Repair Cost**: High risk at nodes with リ級flagship (heavy cruiser) and ヌ級flagship (light carrier)
- **EXP Range**: 170‑420 (boss node 400‑420)
- **Enemy Threat Level**: High, with elite submarines, flagship heavy cruisers, and carrier task forces

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear 鎮守府海域 (World 1), 南西諸島海域 (World 2), 4‑1 (Jam Island), **and 7‑1 (Brunei Anchorage)**
- **Progression Gate**: First map requiring cross‑world progression (World 7 clearance for World 4 access)
- **Strategic Significance**: Represents coordinated operations across different ocean theaters

### Node Details

#### Resource Nodes
1. **Node J: アイテム獲得 (Item Acquisition)**

   - **Yield**: ボーキサイト+40～60 (increments of 5)
   - **Bonus**: Drum cans (+1.5×3), Daihatsu landing craft (+2×4)
   - **Strategic Value**: Provides bauxite recovery, useful for carrier‑heavy fleets

2. **Node K: アイテム獲得 (Item Acquisition)**
   - **Type**: Steel collection node
   - **Yield**: 鋼材+20～80 (increments of 5)
   - **Bonus**: Drum cans (+2), Daihatsu landing craft (+3×5)
   - **Strategic Value**: Provides steel recovery, valuable for repair resource

#### Anti‑Submarine Nodes (Special Mechanics)
- **Node B**: "対潜戦 (通常消費)" – ASW with normal consumption
  - **Submarine Effect**: None (no visual effect)
  - **Resource Consumption**: 20% fuel and ammunition
  - **Enemy**: 潜水ヨ級elite (Yo‑class elite) + 潜水カ級 (Ka‑class) formations
- **Node H**: "対潜戦" – ASW with reduced consumption
  - **Submarine Effect**: Present (visual effect)
  - **Resource Consumption**: 5‑8% fuel only, no ammunition consumption
  - **Enemy**: 潜水ヨ級elite (Yo‑class elite) heavy submarine formations

#### Non‑Combat Nodes
- **Node F**: "敵影を見ず。" – no combat (enemy not sighted)
- **Node I**: "敵影を見ず。" – no combat (enemy not sighted)

#### Combat Nodes (see Enemy Analysis for details)
- **Node A**: 敵東方前衛艦隊 – Enemy Eastern Vanguard Fleet (heavy/light cruisers)
- **Node C**: 敵東方艦隊　遊撃戦隊 – Enemy Eastern Fleet Guerrilla Squadron (mixed with light carriers)
- **Node D**: 敵空母機動部隊 – Enemy Carrier Task Force (carrier Wo‑class flagship + battleship Ru‑class)
- **Node E**: 敵東方艦隊　遊撃戦隊分遣隊 – Enemy Eastern Fleet Guerrilla Detachment (similar to C)
- **Node G**: 敵東方艦隊 上陸輸送船団 – Enemy Eastern Fleet Landing Transport Convoy (transport ships + escorts)
- **Node L (BOSS)**: 敵東方艦隊　強襲上陸主力艦隊 – Enemy Eastern Fleet Assault Landing Main Force

### Route Strategy Analysis (from Wiki Guide)
**Critical Requirements:**
- **Boss Access**: Requires 駆逐 ≥2 ships (≥3 ships guarantees boss on any route)
- **North Route (A) Advantage**: Fewer battles, lower air threat, easier air superiority
- **South Route (B) Disadvantage**: More battles, carrier task force at D (high air power), longer path

**Recommended Compositions:**
1. **North Route Optimal (ACL)**: (戦艦+空母)=2, (重巡級+雷巡)=1, 軽巡=1, 駆逐=2
2. **North Route with Transport Farming (ACGL)**: (戦艦+空母)=2, 軽巡=1, 駆逐=3 (includes G node for transport ships)
3. **South Route (BDCL)**: Similar composition but faces tougher D node

**Air Superiority Targets:**
- **Node D**: 52/78/156 (advantage/superiority/supremacy) - requires ~85 fighter power for superiority
- **Boss L**: 27‑28/41‑42/81‑84 - manageable with seaplane fighters
- **North Route**: Maximum air power <70 across all nodes, full air supremacy achievable

## Enemy Analysis

### Node A: 敵東方前衛艦隊 (Enemy Eastern Vanguard Fleet)

- **最高 Pattern (190 EXP)**: 重巡リ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級elite×2, 駆逐ロ級後期型×2
- **Threat Level**: High (リ級flagship artillery dangerous for destroyers/light cruisers)

- **Pattern 1‑3 (180‑190 EXP)**: 潜水ヨ級elite (1‑2), 潜水カ級 (2‑3), 軽巡ト級elite, destroyer escorts
- **最高 Pattern (190 EXP)**: 潜水ヨ級elite×2, 潜水カ級×3, 軽巡ト級elite
- **Threat Level**: Moderate (ヨ級elite torpedoes dangerous without proper ASW)

### Node C: 敵東方艦隊　遊撃戦隊 (Enemy Eastern Fleet Guerrilla Squadron)
- **Purpose**: Intermediate combat with light carrier support
- **Pattern 1 (200 EXP)**: 重巡リ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級elite, destroyers
- **Pattern 2‑3 (220‑240 EXP)**: 軽母ヌ級flagship, 重巡リ級flagship (1‑2), 軽巡ホ級flagship/elite, destroyers
- **Air Power**: 23/35/69 (advantage/superiority/supremacy)
- **Threat Level**: High (combined cruiser/carrier threat)

### Node D: 敵空母機動部隊 (Enemy Carrier Task Force)
- **Purpose**: Major air power challenge (south route only)
- **Pattern 1‑4 (220‑280 EXP)**: 空母ヲ級flagship, 軽母ヌ級elite, 戦艦ル級 (elite/flagship), 軽巡ヘ級flagship, destroyers

- **Threat Level**: Very High (carrier‑battleship combined arms)

### Node E: 敵東方艦隊　遊撃戦隊分遣隊 (Enemy Eastern Fleet Guerrilla Detachment)
- **Purpose**: Alternative north route combat node
- **Pattern 1‑3 (200‑220 EXP)**: Similar to node C but slightly weaker
- **最高 Pattern (220 EXP)**: 軽母ヌ級flagship, 重巡リ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級flagship, 軽巡ホ級elite, destroyers
- **Air Power**: 23/35/69 (same as node C)
- **Threat Level**: Moderate‑High

### Node G: 敵東方艦隊 上陸輸送船団 (Enemy Eastern Fleet Landing Transport Convoy)
- **Purpose**: Transport ship farming node (contains 3 transport ships)
- **Pattern 1‑3 (350‑380 EXP)**: 輸送ワ級 (flagship/elite)×3, 軽巡ホ級 (flagship/elite), destroyer escorts
- **最高 Pattern (380 EXP)**: 輸送ワ級flagship, 輸送ワ級elite×2, 軽巡ホ級flagship, 駆逐ロ級後期型×2

### Node H: 対潜戦 - 敵潜水哨戒部隊 (ASW - Enemy Submarine Patrol Unit)
- **Purpose**: Pure submarine combat with reduced resource consumption
- **Special**: Submarine visual effect present, 5‑8% fuel only consumption
- **Pattern 1‑3 (180‑220 EXP)**: 潜水ヨ級elite (1‑2), 潜水カ級 (3‑4) formations
- **最高 Pattern (220 EXP)**: 潜水ヨ級elite×2, 潜水カ級×4
- **Threat Level**: Moderate (pure submarine threat, dangerous without ASW)

- **Composition**: Combined arms with battleship, carrier, and transport ships

- **Strategic Importance**: Contains transport ships for mission farming

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. Cross‑World Progression Gating
- **7‑1 Prerequisite**: First map requiring clearance of a different world (World 7), teaching players to progress across multiple theaters
- **Strategic Coordination**: Represents multi‑front operations requiring fleet deployment across distant oceans
- **Progression Validation**: Ensures players have mastered basic mechanics before tackling complex Western Waters maps

### 2. Dual‑Route Strategic Choice
- **Clear North/South Dichotomy**: North route (A) easier with fewer battles, south route (B) harder with carrier task force
- **Risk‑Reward Balance**: South route offers resource nodes (J/K) but at higher combat cost
- **Player Agency**: Allows players to choose difficulty based on fleet capability and objectives

### 3. Advanced ASW Mechanics Differentiation
- **Two ASW Node Types**: B (normal consumption, no effect) vs H (reduced consumption, visual effect)
- **Resource Management**: Teaches players about different ASW node characteristics
- **Enemy Variety**: Introduces ヨ級elite (Yo‑class elite) submarines as new threat tier

### 4. Destroyer‑Count Dependency Design
- **Critical Routing Factor**: Destroyer/coastal defense ship count determines initial route and boss access
- **Composition Trade‑off**: More destroyers improve routing but reduce combat effectiveness
- **Balanced Fleet Building**: Forces players to optimize rather than maximize any single ship type

### 5. Transport Ship Integration for Mission Farming
- **Dual Farming Opportunities**: G node (3 transports) and boss node (2 transports) for "ろ号" mission progress
- **Strategic Choice**: Players can choose to farm transports or take shortest route
- **Mission‑Map Integration**: Encourages repeated play for mission completion

### 6. Air Power Progression
- **Graduated Air Threats**: North route (<70 air power) vs south route (D node 52‑56/78‑84/156‑168)
- **Seaplane Fighter Focus**: Encourages use of aviation cruisers/battleships with seaplanes
- **Air Superiority Planning**: Requires different approaches for north vs south route
