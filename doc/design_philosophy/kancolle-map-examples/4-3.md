# Kantai Collection Map 4-3 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 4‑3: リランカ島空襲 (Lilanka Island Air Raid)** – A 7‑star difficulty Western Waters air raid map emphasizing carrier‑based fleet composition, with complex branching based on carrier count, destroyer count, submarine presence, and fleet speed unification.

### Purpose
- **Carrier‑Centric Design**: Requires aircraft carriers for successful air raid operations against enemy home port
- **Complex Branching Logic**: Multiple branching points with layered conditions based on ship composition
- **Air Superiority Management**: Features enemy carrier nodes requiring air power control
- **Submarine Routing Influence**: Submarine presence significantly affects routing at multiple nodes
- **Speed‑Based Branching**: High‑speed unified fleets receive routing advantages
- **Resource Node Variety**: Includes fuel node with drum can bonuses and whirlpool node with ammunition loss mechanics
- **High Difficulty Challenge**: 7‑star difficulty provides significant challenge for mid‑game players

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (7 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 50‑70 ships with proper carrier air power and anti‑submarine equipment
- **Resource Cost**: High fuel/ammo consumption due to multiple combat nodes and potential whirlpool losses
- **Repair Cost**: High risk of damage from elite enemy carriers, battleships, and heavy cruisers
- **EXP Range**: 150‑310+ (varies by node, boss node 300‑310)
- **Enemy Threat Level**: High, with elite carrier groups, flagship battleships, and heavy cruiser formations

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear 西方海域4‑2 (Western Waters 4‑2)
- **Progression**: Third map of Western Waters, following the air raid operation theme
- **Strategic Requirement**: Carrier‑based fleet composition emphasized by mission objective

**Critical Notes:**
- Carrier count significantly affects routing: 空母系=0 forces certain paths, 空母系≥3 creates biases
- Submarine presence forces routing to K from H and L from N
- Destroyer count critical: 駆逐=0 forces K from H, (駆逐+海防)≤1 forces E from D and L from N
- High‑speed unified fleet (高速以上統一) provides routing advantages at D→G and H→H (self‑loop?)
- Exact carrier counts (1, 2, 3) create specific probability biases at N→L branching

### Route Strategy Analysis (from Routing Patterns)
The complex branching creates several viable strategies:

1. **Carrier‑Heavy Route (Recommended for Air Raid)**
   - Composition: 空母系=2‑3, balanced escort with destroyers
   - Path: Likely A/C → D → E → F → H → K → L → N (boss)
   - Advantages: Maximizes air power for carrier‑focused map theme
   - Risks: Faces enemy carrier task forces at nodes F and H

2. **Light Fleet Route (Destroyer‑Heavy)**
   - Composition: (駆逐+海防)≥4, minimal carriers
   - Path: Likely A/C → D → B/E → G → H → K → L → N (boss)
   - Advantages: Accesses resource node B, avoids heaviest carrier nodes
   - Risks: Vulnerable to submarine nodes and may face ammunition loss at E

3. **High‑Speed Unified Fleet**
   - Composition: All ships high‑speed (fast battleships, light cruisers, destroyers)
   - Path: Benefits from D→G and H→H routing advantages
   - Advantages: More predictable routing, avoids certain dead‑ends
   - Risks: Limits ship selection, may lack firepower

4. **Submarine‑Inclusive Fleet**
   - Composition: Includes 1‑2 submarines
   - Path: Forced routing to K from H and L from N
   - Advantages: Potentially shorter route? Unclear from rules
   - Risks: Limited combat effectiveness against surface fleets

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. Land‑Based Boss Introduction
- **Historical Significance**: 4‑3 is the first normal map to feature a land‑based installation boss (港湾棲姫)
- **Mechanical Teaching**: Introduces anti‑installation combat requirements
- **Progression Milestone**: Marks transition from ship‑only enemies to mixed enemy types
- **Air Power Emphasis**: Boss node requires significant air power (80/120/240)

### 2. Carrier‑Centric Design Philosophy
- **Mission Theme**: Air raid operation requiring carrier‑based fleets
- **Routing Incentives**: Carrier counts (0, 1, 2, 3+) create distinct routing probabilities
- **Enemy Mirroring**: Enemy features carrier task forces at nodes F and H
- **Resource Synergy**: Bauxite node (J) supports carrier operations

### 3. Complex Multi‑Layer Branching
- **Condition Stacking**: Multiple conditions per branching point with priority ordering
- **Ship‑Class Precision**: Exact counts (e.g., 軽巡=1 exactly) create specific routing
- **Speed‑Based Routing**: High‑speed unified fleet advantages at D and H
- **Submarine‑Specific Routing**: Submarines force specific paths (H→K, N→L)

### 4. Resource Management Depth
- **Dual Whirlpool Types**: Ammunition loss (E) and fuel loss (K) with radar mitigation
- **Dual Resource Nodes**: Fuel (B) and bauxite (J) with transport bonuses
- **Strategic Tradeoffs**: Route selection balances resource loss vs. combat risk
- **Equipment Interaction**: Radar equipment reduces whirlpool losses

### 5. Difficulty Progression in Western Waters
- **7‑Star Difficulty**: Significant jump from 4‑1 (5 stars) and 4‑2 (6 stars)
- **Enemy Escalation**: Introduces flagship battleships (タ級flagship), flagship carriers (ヲ級flagship), and land‑based installations
- **Routing Complexity**: Most complex branching in World 4 so far
- **Composition Requirements**: Forces players to master fleet building with multiple constraints

### 6. Optional Combat Nodes
- **Node I (Battleship Reinforcement)**: Heavy combat optional node for additional challenge/rewards
- **Multiple Paths**: Players can choose between carrier‑heavy (F), surface‑heavy (G), or mixed routes
- **Risk‑Reward Balance**: Longer routes with more combat vs. shorter routes with resource loss
