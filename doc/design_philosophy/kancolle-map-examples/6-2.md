# Kantai Collection Map 6-2 Design Philosophy
**MS Islands Defense Battle** (MS諸島防衛戦)

## Map Overview
- **Purpose**: Test advanced surface‑fleet composition and LoS management; introduces heavy carrier‑battleship enemy groups
- **Difficulty**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (9 stars), high
- **Unlock Requirements**: Clear 6‑1 (Submarine Operation) AND 5‑4 (Second Battle of the Solomon Sea)
- **HP Gauge**: Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by ⅓ (damage does not reduce gauge); 3 sinks required to clear
- **Unique Drop**: 酒匂 (Sakawa) – only obtainable here, not buildable
- **Historical Context**: Defense of Marcus Island (MS諸島) against enemy invasion force

## Node Layout
```
Start → B/C → C/D → A/D/E → F/H → F/I/J → G/K → K (Boss)
```
**Node Types**:
- **A, E, G**: No combat (“気のせいだった”)
- **B**: Surface combat – enemy support group A (heavy cruiser flagship + light carrier elite + light cruiser + destroyers)
- **C**: Surface combat – enemy support group B (battleship flagship + heavy cruiser elites + light carrier)
- **D**: Whirlpool (強うずしお) – consumes ammunition (40‑150% base loss, reduced by radar equipment)
- **F**: Carrier battle – enemy carrier task force (carrier flagship ×2 + light cruisers + destroyers)
- **H**: Surface combat – enemy high‑speed surface strike force (fast battleship flagship + heavy cruiser elite + light cruiser elite + destroyers)
- **I**: Surface combat – enemy mission group B (battleship flagship ×2 + light cruisers + destroyers)
- **J**: Surface combat – enemy mission group A (battleship‑class elite/改 flagship + carrier‑class elite/改 + heavy cruiser elite/改)
- **K**: Boss – enemy main invasion force (transport flagship + battleship‑class elite/改 flagship + light cruiser elites + destroyers)

## Routing Rules
**Key Branching Conditions**:

### Start → B/C
- **B** if:
  - (Light cruiser + destroyer) ≥4 ships
- **C** if:
  - (Aviation battleship + aviation cruiser + seaplane tender + landing ship) ≤1 AND submarines ≤4
- **Random 65% B / 35% C** if:
  - (Battleship‑class + carrier‑class) ≥4 ships
- **C** (default) otherwise

### C → B/D
- **D** if:
  - (Light cruiser + destroyer) ≥5 ships
- **Random 70% C / 30% ?** if:
  - Carrier‑class ≤2 AND battleship‑class = 0
- **C** (default) otherwise

### D → A/E
- **A** if:
  - Submarines = 6, OR
  - (Battleship‑class + carrier‑class) ≥5, OR
  - (Battleship‑class + carrier‑class + heavy cruiser‑class) = 6, OR
  - (Battleship‑class + carrier‑class + submarines) = 6
- **E** if:
  - (Battleship‑class + carrier‑class) ≤2
- **D** (default) otherwise

### F → F/H
- **F** if:
  - Destroyers ≤2, OR
  - Contains battleship‑class OR carrier‑class, OR
  - (Light cruiser + destroyer) ≤4
- **H** otherwise

### H → F/I/J
- **F** if ANY of:
  - Battleship‑class ≥2, OR
  - Carrier‑class ≥2, OR
  - Destroyers ≤1
- **I** if:
  - LoS score <43 (HQ 120, branch coefficient 3)
- **Random I/J** if:
  - LoS score ≥43 AND <50
- **J** if:
  - LoS score ≥50

### I → G/K
- **G** if:
  - LoS insufficient
- **K** if:
  - LoS score ≥32

### G → G/K
- **G** if:
  - Submarines ≥4, OR
  - LoS score <35?
- **Random G/K** if:
  - LoS score ≥35? AND <40
- **K** if:
  - LoS score ≥40

**LoS Notes**:
- Branch coefficient: **3** (high)
- LoS scores listed are for HQ level 120; lower HQ levels need +1 or more margin
- LoS calculation uses standard formula (索敵スコア計算式)

## Resource Nodes

## Design Patterns Observed
1. **LoS‑Heavy Routing**: Multiple branches depend on precise LoS scores (coefficient 3), teaching players to optimize reconnaissance equipment
2. **Fleet‑Composition Gates**: Clear thresholds for battleship/carrier counts (≥5 forces route A, ≤2 forces route E) creating strategic choices between heavy and light fleets
3. **Carrier‑Battleship Synergy Test**: Node F features extreme air power (168+) requiring strong carrier‑based air defense or battleship‑based air‑cut
4. **Whirlpool Management**: Introduces radar‑based resource‑loss reduction, encouraging equipment optimization beyond combat
5. **Progressive Difficulty**: Enemy quality escalates from support groups (B/C) to mission groups (I/J) to main force (K), with elite/改 variants appearing later
6. **Transport‑Focused Boss**: Boss node includes transport flagship, emphasizing fleet‑clearance over pure combat power
7. **Historical Fleet Routing**: References Marcus Island defense, using appropriate ship types for Pacific theater
