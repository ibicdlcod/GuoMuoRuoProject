# Kantai Collection Map 2-3 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 2-3: 東部オリョール海 (Eastern Orel Sea)** - A mid-difficulty patrol map focused on convoy defense and resource gathering, serving as a primary hub for quest completion.

### Purpose
- **Quest Hub**: Over 40 individual quests require sorties to 2-3, making it a primary task-completion海域
- **Resource Farming**: Steady fuel and ammunition income via resource nodes encourages regular patrols
- **Ship Collection**: Boss node offers wide variety of rare and limited-time ship drops
- **Progression Gate**: Moderate difficulty (5 stars) tests fleet composition and equipment before advancing to harder maps
- **Strategic Training**: Teaches advanced fleet-composition principles through complex routing rules

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ★★★★★ (5 stars, moderate difficulty)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 30-50 ships with decent equipment
- **Resource Cost**: Moderate fuel/ammo consumption, but offset by resource node gains
- **Repair Cost**: Moderate damage, especially at boss node
- **EXP Range**: 70-290 (boss node 220-290)

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear World 2-2
- **No special requirements**: Open after completing 2-2

## Route Branching Rules (ルート分岐法則)

### Key Class Definitions
- **戦艦級 (Battleship-class)**: Includes Fast Battleships, (Slow) Battleships, Aviation Battleships
- **空母系 (Carrier-type)**: Includes Standard Carriers, Armored Carriers, Light Carriers
- **軽巡級 (Light Cruiser-class)**: Includes Light Cruisers, Training Cruisers, **Torpedo Cruisers** (updated 2022/11/13)
- **海防艦 (Coastal Defense Ships)**: Includes Coastal Defense Ships and Coastal Defense Battleships
- **補給艦 (Replenishment Ships)**: Includes Fleet Oilers, Special Duty Ships, Icebreakers, etc.
- **潜水艦 (Submarines)**: Includes Submarines and Submarine Carriers
- **潜水母艦 (Submarine Tender)**: Separate from submarines

### Detailed Branching Conditions

**Start → A/B/C**
- **Submarine + Submarine Tender only fleet** → C (100%)
- **Otherwise** → Random A or B (50/50)

**C → D/F**
- Random with 60% bias toward D

**D → F/G**
- **Seaplane Tender + Replenishment ≥1 AND Destroyer + Coastal Defense ≥2** → G
- **Submarine ≥2 AND includes Submarine Tender** → G
- **Submarine-only fleet** → Random with 65% bias toward G
- **Submarine ≥1 AND (Battleship-class + Carrier-type) ≥1** → Random with 65% bias toward F
- **Otherwise** → Random (G bias increases with more Destroyer/Coastal Defense ships: 0–1:35%, 2:50%, 3:65%, 4–6:75%)

**F → G/H/J**
- **(Carrier-type + Light Cruiser + Seaplane Tender) ≥1** → Random G or J (90% bias toward J)
- **Submarine ≥2 AND includes Submarine Tender** → Random G or J (80% bias toward G?)
- **Otherwise** → Random (G:H:J = 25%:35%:40%)

**G → I/K**
- **Submarine ≥2 AND includes Submarine Tender** → Random with 60% bias toward I
- **Submarine-only fleet** → Random with 55% bias toward I
- **(Light Cruiser + Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≤1** → K
- **(Seaplane Tender + Replenishment) ≥1 AND (Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≥2** → Random with 65% bias toward I
- **(Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≥3** → Random with 55% bias toward K
- **(Destroyer + Coastal Defense) ≥1** → Random with 65% bias toward K
- **(Destroyer + Coastal Defense) = 0** → K

**J → L/M/N**
- **Light Cruiser ≥1 AND Destroyer ≥4** → N
- **Light Cruiser =1 AND Heavy Cruiser =5** → N (Aviation Cruisers **NOT** allowed)
- **Submarine-only fleet** → Random M or N (90% bias toward N)
- **Fleet includes Submarine** → Random (10% chance to M)
- **(Battleship-class + Carrier-type) =6** → L
- **(Battleship-class + Carrier-type) =5** → Random L or N (85% bias toward L)
- **(Battleship-class + Carrier-type) =4** → Random L or N (75% bias toward N)
- **(Battleship-class + Carrier-type) =3** → Random L or N (80% bias toward N)
- **(Battleship-class + Carrier-type) ≤2** → Random L or N (90% bias toward N)

### Strategic Implications
- **Submarine Routing**: Historically known as "Orel Cruising" (オリョールクルージング). Submarine-only fleets start at C, can collect resources at D/G/H/I, but routing is probabilistic and less efficient in current game version (intentionally depowered).
- **Light Fleet Routing**: Fleets with 1 Light Cruiser + 4+ Destroyers can reliably reach boss (N)
- **Heavy Fleet Routing**: Battleship/Carrier-heavy fleets (≥4) tend to route away from boss, encouraging balanced compositions
- **Resource Gathering**: Specific compositions (Seaplane Tender + Replenishment + Destroyers) can route to resource nodes

## Design Philosophy Analysis

### Role in Game Progression
**Quest-Centric Hub**: With over 40 individual quests requiring sorties to 2-3, this map serves as a primary task-completion海域. Players return repeatedly throughout the game's lifecycle for daily, weekly, and quarterly missions.

**Resource-Focused Design**: The map provides steady fuel/ammunition income via resource nodes (D, G, H, I), encouraging regular patrols and teaching resource management. Players must balance fleet cost against resource gains.

**Progression Gate**: Moderate difficulty (5 stars) with a strong boss (Ru‑class flagship) tests players' fleet composition and equipment before advancing to harder maps like 2-4 and 2-5.

**Flexible Routing**: Multiple viable compositions allow both lightweight (CL+DD) and heavyweight (BB+CV ≤4) strategies, accommodating different player resources and playstyles. The complex branching rules teach advanced fleet-composition principles.

**Historical Nod**: "Orel Patrol" references WWII Arctic convoy patrols, thematically fitting the resource-defense objective and tying gameplay to historical naval operations.

### Strategic Intent
**Training Ground**: Provides consistent combat experience (70–290 EXP) without extreme difficulty, making it ideal for leveling mid-tier ships.

**Resource Management Exercise**: Players learn to optimize fleet composition for both combat effectiveness and resource efficiency, especially when farming for quests.

**Team-Building Exercise**: Requires understanding of route-branching rules and class synergies to reach boss reliably. Different strategies (submarine cruising, light fleet, heavy fleet) teach meta-game concepts.

**Drop Variety**: Boss node offers a wide array of rare and limited-time ships, encouraging repeated runs for collection and providing long-term engagement.

### Notable Design Features
**Boss Lock Mechanism**: At high HQ level (120+), boss patterns are restricted to harder variations (patterns 4–8 only), maintaining challenge for veteran players and preventing trivial farming.

**Submarine Nerf Evolution**: Once-dominant submarine cruising ("Orel Cruising") was intentionally depowered in later game updates, shifting meta toward surface fleets and demonstrating game balance evolution.

**Mission Overlap Design**: Many quests (including quarterly and yearly missions) target this map, ensuring it remains actively played throughout the game's lifecycle and serves as a social hub for player discussions.

**Air Combat Introduction**: While only present at boss node, the moderate air power (18-54) introduces air superiority concepts without overwhelming new players.

### Map-Specific Mechanics
**Resource Node Scaling**: Fuel/ammunition gains scale with Drum Canisters and Landing Craft, encouraging equipment optimization.

**Transport Ship Focus**: Node M features transport Wa‑class ships, supporting "supply ship destruction" quests and teaching anti-transport combat.

**Formation Strategy**: Boss node uses Single formation (increasing damage dealt/received), teaching formation selection importance.

**Class Definition Precision**: The 2022 update clarifying that "Light Cruiser-class" includes Torpedo Cruisers demonstrates ongoing rule refinement and attention to detail.

### Overall Design Goal
A mid-difficulty, repeatable map that serves as a workhorse for quest completion, resource gathering, and ship collection, while teaching players advanced fleet-composition principles through its branching rules. It balances accessibility for mid-level players with long-term value for veterans through rare drops and quest integration.

## Implementation Considerations for FleetMemories

### Core Mechanics to Replicate
1. **Complex Routing System**: Implement the detailed branching rules with probability biases
2. **Resource Node Integration**: Fuel/ammunition nodes with scaling based on equipment
3. **Quest Integration**: Design multiple quests targeting this map for player engagement
4. **Boss Lock Mechanism**: Restrict boss patterns based on player level/equivalent
5. **Submarine Routing**: Include submarine-specific paths (though less efficient)

### Adaptation Suggestions
1. **Simplify Class Definitions**: Use FleetMemories' existing ship classifications while preserving strategic intent
2. **Adjust Resource Values**: Scale to FleetMemories' economy (fuel/ammunition values)
3. **Modify EXP Ranges**: Align with FleetMemories' leveling curve
4. **Adapt Enemy Patterns**: Use equivalent enemy types from FleetMemories' roster
5. **Maintain Strategic Depth**: Keep the light vs. heavy fleet trade-offs and resource management aspects

### Balance Considerations
- **Difficulty Curve**: Position as mid-difficulty map between 2-2 and 2-4
- **Resource Economy**: Ensure resource gains are meaningful but not exploitable
- **Quest Frequency**: Include regular quests targeting this map to maintain relevance
- **Drop Rarity**: Preserve rare ship incentives without frustrating drop rates
