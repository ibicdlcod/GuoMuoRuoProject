# Kantai Collection Map 5-3 Design Philosophy
## 第一次サーモン沖海戦 (First Battle off Salmon)

### Map Overview
**Purpose:** Introduction to night‑only combat maps, teaching players to build fleets for night battle dominance in Southern Waters.

**Difficulty:** ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (9 stars) – high difficulty, requires specialized night battle equipment and tactics.

**Unlock Requirements:**
- Clear map 5‑2 (珊瑚諸島沖海戦)
- Teaches progression from carrier‑focused combat to night‑focused combat

**Notable Features:**
- **HP Gauge:** Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by 20% (5 sinks required to clear)
- **Night‑only combat nodes:** Majority of combat nodes are 夜戦マス (night battle nodes)
- **Resource nodes:** Steel (H) and Ammunition (F) with transport equipment bonuses
- **Active branching:** Player‑choice branching at node O
- **Historical theme:** Based on the Naval Battle of Guadalcanal (Ironbottom Sound)

**BGM Changes:**
- Map: "艦隊の再集結"
- Route daytime: "二水戦の航跡"
- Route night: "次発装填、再突入!"
- Boss daytime: "海上輸送作戦"
- Boss night: "水雷戦隊の反撃"

### Node Layout
Map features **17 combat nodes** (A‑Q) plus start point, with complex night‑battle‑focused branching.

**Key Node Types:**
- **A, B, D, E, G** – No combat (戦闘回避)
- **C** – Surface combat (敵鉄底海峡任務部隊 哨戒隊) – light cruiser/destroyer force
- **F** – **Resource (Ammo)** +60 ammunition, bonus with drum cans/landing craft
- **H** – **Resource (Steel)** +50‑80 steel, bonus with drum cans/landing craft
- **I, J, K, N, P** – **夜戦** (Night Battle) – various enemy formations
- **L** – **Whirlpool** (うずしお) – fuel drain
- **M** – Carrier‑based enemy (敵任務部隊 増援部隊) – air power focused
- **O** – **Active Branching** (能動分岐) – player chooses route
- **Q** – **Boss** (敵南方艦隊 旗艦) – Southern War Demon (南方棲戦姫)

**Route Structure:**
Complex branching with multiple decision points:
- **Start → C/D:** Based on speed, battleship/carrier counts, submarine presence
- **C/D → B/Q:** Random with battleship/carrier influences
- **B → F/E:** Complex destroyer/light cruiser conditions
- **F/E → G/I:** Based on aviation battleship, regular carrier, submarine, destroyer counts
- **I → J/O:** Based on light carrier, battleship, destroyer, light cruiser counts
- **J → O:** Based on similar conditions
- **O → K/L/M/N:** Player choice (active branching)
- **K → P/H:** Based on destroyer counts and special conditions
- **P → Q:** Boss node

### Routing Rules (ルート分岐法則)
**Updated:** 2023‑02‑11

**Key Branching Conditions:**

1. **Start → C/D:**
   - **D if:** High‑speed+ unified
   - **C if:** (戦艦級 + 空母系) ≥3
   - **C if:** (戦艦級 + 空母系) =2 **AND** contains速力:低速
   - **D‑biased random (60%) if:** contains潜水艦
   - **Otherwise D**

2. **B → F/E:**
   - **F if:** contains潜水艦
   - **Random if:** (戦艦級 + 空母系?) ≥1 **AND** 駆逐 ≤1
   - **Q if:** contains軽巡 **OR** 重巡級 ≥4 **OR** 駆逐 ≥4
   - **Otherwise random**

3. **F/E → G/I:**
   - **J if:** contains航空戦艦 **OR** 正規空母 **OR** 潜水艦
   - **I if:** 駆逐 =0 **OR** 軽空母 ≥2 **OR** (軽空母 =1 **AND** 駆逐 =1)
   - **J if:** 軽空母 =1 **OR** (低速)戦艦 ≥2
   - **Otherwise I**

4. **I → J/O:**
   - **J if:** contains軽空母 **OR** 戦艦級 ≥3 **OR** 戦艦級 ≥2
   - **O if:** 駆逐 ≥3 **OR** (軽巡 ≥1 **AND** 駆逐 ≥2)
   - **O if:** (軽巡 ≥1 **AND** 重巡級 ≥4 **AND** (重巡級 + 軽巡 + 駆逐) =6)
   - **Random otherwise**

5. **J → O:**
   - Similar conditions as I→J branching

6. **K → P/H:**
   - **H if:** 駆逐 ≥4 **OR** (駆逐 =3 **AND** 軽巡 =1)
   - **H if:** 駆逐 =2 **AND** (高速+以上統一 **OR** contains航空戦艦 **OR** contains補給艦 **OR** contains潜水母艦 **OR** ドラム缶搭載艦 ≥2 **OR** 大発動艇系搭載艦 ≥2)
   - **Otherwise E**

7. **L → M/N:**
   - **Random if:** contains潜水艦
   - **M if:** (戦艦級 + 空母系) ≥4 **OR** contains正規空母 **OR** 軽空母 ≥2
   - **N if:** 軽空母 =1 **AND** (低速)戦艦 ≥2
   - **L/N random if:** 軽空母 =1 **AND** contains航空戦艦
   - **L if:** 軽空母 =1 **AND** (駆逐 ≥3 **OR** (軽巡 ≥1 **AND** 駆逐 ≥2) **OR** (重巡＋航巡) =3 **AND** 駆逐 =2)
   - **L/N random otherwise** (more destroyers increase L probability)

**Night Battle Node Consumption:**
- **I, J, K, N, P:** 10% fuel and ammunition consumption each

**Speed Notes:**
- **High‑speed+ unified:** All ships at high‑speed+ or faster
- Speed enhancements reference: [速力](/kancolle/%E9%80%9F%E5%8A%9B)

### Design Patterns Observed

#### 1. **Night‑Only Combat Focus**
- Majority of combat nodes are **夜戦マス** (night battle nodes)
- **No aircraft, ASW, or torpedo phases** – only night combat
- **Attack power = Firepower + Torpedo stat combined**
- Teaches importance of **night battle equipment** (night scouts, searchlights, starshells)
- **10% fuel/ammo consumption per night node** – resource management critical

#### 2. **HP Gauge with Higher Requirements**
- **Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by 20%** (5 sinks required to clear)
- Increased from 5‑2's 25% (4 sinks) – longer engagement required
- Encourages repeated engagement with night‑specialized fleets
- Prepares players for more demanding gauge mechanics

#### 3. **Active Branching (Player Choice)**
- **Node O offers player route selection** (能動分岐)
- First appearance of active branching in Southern Waters
- Allows players to choose between different challenge levels
- Teaches strategic decision‑making during sorties

#### 4. **Night Battle Equipment Requirements**
- **Night scouts** (九八式水上偵察機(夜偵)) – increase accuracy/attack power
- **Starshells** (照明弾) – increase accuracy/Cut‑In rate, decrease enemy CI rate
- **Searchlights** (探照灯) – increase accuracy/Cut‑In rate but increase targeting risk
- **Skilled lookouts** (熟練見張員) – increase attack power/night CI rate
- **Night‑specific attacks** (試製 夜間瑞雲) – special night attacks with starshell effect

#### 5. **Enemy Night Combat Specialization**
- **重巡リ級flagship/elite** – high night Cut‑In rate (fish/torpedo/main gun CI)
- **雷巡チ級flagship** – triple torpedo cruiser threat
- **戦艦タ級flagship** – night double‑attack capability
- **戦艦ル級flagship** – new enemy type introduced at node N
- Teaches players to prioritize high‑threat night combat enemies

#### 6. **Carrier Node as Mid‑Map Challenge**
- **Node M** provides carrier‑based challenge amidst night nodes
- **6‑pattern progression** with escalating air power (up to 756 air supremacy)
- Requires players to maintain some air power despite night focus
- Bridges 5‑2's carrier focus with 5‑3's night focus

#### 7. **Transport Ship Farming**
- **Node P** features 6 patterns of transport ships (輸送ワ級)
- **High EXP values** (700‑750) for relatively low threat
- Provides farming opportunity for resources and experience
- Encourages players to build transport‑hunting fleets

#### 8. **New Enemy Introductions**
- **南方棲戦姫** (Southern War Demon) – new boss enemy type
- **戦艦ル級flagship** – new battleship enemy at node N
- **雷巡チ級flagship** – triple torpedo cruiser threat
- Expands enemy roster for Southern Waters

#### 9. **Speed‑Based Routing**
- **High‑speed+ unified** requirement for optimal start routing
- **Speed influences multiple branching points**
- Requires careful speed tuning for desired routes

#### 10. **Resource Management Emphasis**
- **10% consumption per night node** – significant resource drain
- **Whirlpool fuel drain** on certain routes
- **Resource nodes** provide replenishment but require transport equipment
- Teaches players to balance combat effectiveness with resource efficiency

### Conclusion
Map 5‑3 serves as the definitive night‑combat map, introducing specialized night battle mechanics, equipment, and enemy capabilities. The focus on night‑only combat nodes teaches players to build fleets for night dominance, while the active branching provides strategic choice. The HP gauge with 5‑sink requirement encourages repeated engagement, and the new enemy types (南方棲戦姫, 戦艦ル級flagship) expand the Southern Waters threat roster.

For FleetMemories, this map provides a template for night‑focused combat, active branching mechanics, night battle equipment systems, and resource management with node‑based consumption. The transport farming node offers alternative gameplay, while the carrier node maintains connection to previous map mechanics.
