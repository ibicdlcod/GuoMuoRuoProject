# Kantai Collection Map 7-4 Design Philosophy
**Operation Hi Convoy Escort** (ヒ船団海上護衛作戦)

## Map Overview
- **Purpose**: **Escort‑focused map** requiring specialized anti‑submarine and anti‑air light fleets to protect a resource convoy (Hi‑gun) from submarine packs and carrier task forces. Introduces **Hi Convoy Princess** as a boss with escorting submarines, testing **percentage‑based damage mechanics** (海防艦特効) and **LBAS‑radius‑2 restriction**.
- **Difficulty**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (9 stars)
- **Unlock Requirements**: **7‑3 clear**
- **HP Gauge**: Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by **⅕ (20%)**; **5 sinks required to clear**
- **Monthly Reset**: **Gauge resets on the 1st of each month**, but **no Medal or Special War Results rewards** (unlike Extra Operations)
- **LBAS (Land‑Based Air Support)**:
  - Available after **mission achievement** (出撃可能航空隊数：1)
  - **No enemy air raids on friendly base**
  - **Radius 2 required to reach boss node P**
- **Special Mechanics**:
  - **Escort Ship Bonus Damage**: Escort ships (海防艦) deal **percentage‑based damage** to Hi Convoy Princess (effective against high‑armor boss)
  - **Anti‑PT Equipment**: Not required (no PT boats in this map)
  - **Submarine‑Intensive**: Multiple submarine nodes (B, C, G, J, K) with escalating difficulty
  - **Carrier‑Task‑Force Nodes**: H and D/E feature light carrier groups with black aircraft (high air power)
- **BGM**:
  - Map: 「南号作戦」
  - Route combat: 「第一護衛艦隊の戦い」
  - Boss battle: 「護衛船団の航跡」

## Node Layout
```
Start → A → (B/C) → (C→D/E) → (D→H/I) or (E→J) → (J→F→G/K/L/P) → (P: boss)
```
**Key Routes**:
- **Shortest route (A‑B‑E‑J‑P)**: Requires **Escort‑heavy fleet** (練巡1, 海防3, 大鷹型1 or 駆逐1)
- **Resource‑farming route**: A‑C‑D‑H (carrier node) → I (no combat) → O (resource node)
- **Boss‑access route**: Must pass **submarine node J** (mixed surface+sub) and **anti‑sub node K** or **surface node L**

**Node Types**:
- **A**: **No Combat** – “ヒ船団、抜錨ッ！ 対潜、対空警戒を厳とせよ！” (Hi Convoy, sortie! Maintain strict ASW/AA alert)
- **B**: **Anti‑Submarine** – Deep‑Sea Submarine Fleet Patrol Group B (潜水カ級elite/flagship, 3‑5 subs)
- **C**: **Anti‑Submarine** – Deep‑Sea Submarine Fleet Patrol Group C (潜水カ級elite/flagship, 4‑5 subs)
- **D**: **Air Raid** – Deep‑Sea Task Force Carrier Group (軽母ヌ級elite with black aircraft, 軽巡ツ級, 駆逐イ級後期型)
- **E**: **Air Raid** – Deep‑Sea Task Force Carrier Group (軽母ヌ級elite with black aircraft, variants up to 軽母ヌ級改elite)
- **F**: **Active Branching** (能動分岐) – no combat, routing decision point

- **H**: **Carrier Task Force** – Deep‑Sea Task Force Carrier Mobile Force (軽母ヌ級elite/改elite with black aircraft, 軽巡ツ級, 駆逐イ級後期型)

## Routing Rules
**Key Branching Conditions** (fleet composition, ship counts, LOS, speed):

### Sortie → A/C
- **→C if**: Contains any **Battleship, Regular Carrier, or Submarine**; OR **Heavy‑Cruiser‑class ≥2**; OR **Light‑Cruiser‑class ≥2**; OR **(Aviation‑Battleship + Light‑Carrier + Akitsu‑Maru) ≥3**
- **→A if**: **Akitsu‑Maru AND Escort‑ships ≥2 PLUS one Destroyer or Escort‑ship**; OR **(Destroyer + Escort) ≥3**; OR **Escort‑ships ≥2**
- **Otherwise →C**

### C → D/E
- **→D if**: Contains any **Battleship, Regular Carrier, or Submarine**; OR **(Light‑Carrier + Akitsu‑Maru) ≥3**
- **→E if**: **(Destroyer + Escort) ≥4**; OR **Training‑Cruiser ≥1 AND (Destroyer + Escort) ≥3**; OR **Escort‑ships ≥3**; OR **Fast+ Unified AND Destroyers ≥2**
- **Otherwise →D**

### E → G/J
- **→G if**: **Escort‑ships ≥4 PLUS one from (Supply, Landing) AND one from (Taiyou‑class, Supply, Landing, Destroyer, Escort)** (6‑ship fleet)
- **Otherwise →J**

### J → F (Active Branching)
- Always →F

### F → G/K/L/P
- **→K if**: **LOS score <33**
- **→K or L (random) if**: **LOS 33‑37**
- **→L if**: **LOS ≥37 AND** (contains ships other than Taiyou‑class, Training‑Cruiser, Destroyer, Escort) OR (6‑ship fleet contains non‑Escort) OR (Escort‑ships =3 AND Training‑Cruiser included AND no Taiyou‑class/Destroyer) OR (Escort‑ships =3 AND Destroyers ≥2) OR (Escort‑ships =3 AND Taiyou‑class ≥2) OR (Escort‑ships ≤2 AND fleet ≠ 1 Destroyer +2 Escort)
- **→P if**: **LOS ≥37** (and above L‑conditions not met)

### L → P
- Always →P

### K → P
- Always →P (K→P route exists but rare)

### P → M/N/O
- **→N if**: **LOS <45**
- **→N or O (random) if**: **LOS 45‑47 AND** contains (Slow Battleship, Regular Carrier) OR (Fast‑Battleship ≥2) OR (Aviation‑Battleship ≥2) OR (Light‑Carrier + Akitsu‑Maru ≥2) OR (Fast‑Battleship + Aviation‑Battleship + Light‑Carrier + Akitsu‑Maru ≥3) OR (Destroyer + Escort ≤1)
- **→O if**: **LOS ≥47**
- **Otherwise →P** (return to boss)

**LOS Notes**:
- **Branching coefficient**: 4
- **LOS scores above are for HQ Level 120**; for HQ <120, **add +1 margin for safety**
- LOS calculated via standard formula (索敵スコア計算式)

## Design Patterns Observed
1. **Escort‑Ship‑Centric Design**:
   - **海防艦 required for shortest route** (A‑B‑E‑J‑P) and **bonus damage vs boss**
   - Routing heavily favors **Escort‑count** (≥2 for A, ≥3 for E, ≥4 for G)
   - **Percentage‑based damage** allows low‑firepower Escorts to contribute against high‑armor boss

2. **Submarine‑Saturated Environment**:
   - **Five dedicated submarine nodes** (B, C, G, K, plus subs in J, L, P)
   - Requires **strong anti‑submarine gear** (Toukai, sonar+depth‑charge)
   - **Submarine princess (K)** as mini‑boss with escalating difficulty

3. **Carrier‑Task‑Force Threats**:
   - **Black‑aircraft carriers** (軽母ヌ級elite) in D, E, H, M deal high air damage
   - **Radius‑2 LBAS restriction** forces careful air‑power management

4. **LOS‑Based Branching Complexity**:
   - **Three LOS checkpoints** (F→K/L/P, P→N/O, historical‑ship dependency absent)
   - **HQ‑level dependent thresholds** (120‑based, margin needed for lower HQ)

5. **Monthly‑Reset Gauge without EO Rewards**:
   - **Gauge resets monthly** like Extra Operations, but **no Medal or Special War Results**
   - Encourages **repeated play for resource farming** (O node) and **Escort‑ship leveling**

6. **Resource‑Node as Strategic Objective**:
   - **O node** provides substantial fuel/bauxite with ship‑type bonuses
   - **Farming route** (A‑C‑D‑H‑I‑O) allows resource collection without boss engagement

## Implementation Notes for FleetMemories
- **Escort‑Ship Mechanics**: Implement **percentage‑based damage bonus** for Escort‑class ships against convoy‑type bosses
- **Submarine‑Heavy Maps**: Design maps with **multiple submarine nodes** requiring ASW specialization
- **LOS‑Dependent Branching**: Use **branching coefficient 4** and **HQ‑level scaling** for LOS checks
- **Monthly‑Reset Gauges**: Allow gauge reset without medal rewards for **replayable high‑difficulty content**
- **Carrier‑Task‑Force Nodes**: Include **black‑aircraft carriers** with high air power but limited radius
- **Resource‑Node Bonuses**: Apply **ship‑type‑specific bonuses** to resource collection (drum cans, landing craft, etc.)
- **Boss with Escort Submarines**: Design bosses accompanied by **submarine escorts** requiring combined anti‑surface and ASW tactics
