# Kantai Collection Map 3-2 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 3-2: キス島撤退作戦 (Kiska Island Evacuation Operation)** - A 5-star difficulty destroyer‑focused map that tests players' light ship capabilities, with strict composition restrictions, whirlpool mechanics, and emphasis on speed/radar management.

### Purpose
- **Destroyer‑Fleet Test**: Forces players to rely on destroyers and light cruisers, prohibiting battleships/carriers for boss access
- **Composition Discipline**: Teaches precise fleet building (exact ship counts and types)
- **Speed & Radar Mechanics**: Introduces speed‑based routing and radar utility for whirlpool loss reduction
- **Resource‑Risk Balance**: Whirlpool penalties encourage careful fuel management
- **Northern Waters Progression**: Second map of World 3, raising difficulty after 3‑1

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆ (5 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 50‑70 ships (destroyers/light cruisers)
- **Resource Cost**: Moderate fuel/ammo, but whirlpool can cause significant fuel loss
- **Repair Cost**: High risk of damage at combat nodes (especially H with battleships)
- **EXP Range**: 150‑390 (boss node 340‑390)
- **HQ Level Scaling**: Not explicitly mentioned

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear both 3‑1 ("モーレイ海哨戒") and 1‑5 ("鎮守府近海")
- **Boss Access Restriction**: Only fleets with exactly 6 ships, composed solely of destroyers, light cruisers, and/or a single auxiliary (補給艦) can reach the boss node. Specifically:
  - Must have 4‑6 destroyers
  - May include 0‑1 light cruiser (雷巡不可 – torpedo cruisers not allowed)
  - May include 0‑1 auxiliary (補給艦)
  - No battleships, carriers, heavy cruisers, submarines, etc.

## Node Layout

### Visual Structure
```
Start → [A or C based on composition]
   A → B (resource) → return
   C → [E/G based on speed/radar] → [F/H/I/J based on composition] → [L (boss) or K (carrier node) or I (resource)]
```
Detailed branching rules below.

### Node Details

#### Resource Nodes
1. **Node B: アイテム獲得 (Item Acquisition)**
   - **Type**: Ammunition resource node
   - **Yield**: 50‑150 ammunition (increments of 5)
   - **Equipment Bonus**:
     - Drum Canisters: +2 per canister
     - 大発系 Landing Craft: +3 per craft
   - **Strategic Value**: Primary ammunition farming spot; accessible via A→B route

2. **Node I: アイテム獲得 (Item Acquisition)**
   - **Type**: Furniture box node
   - **Yield**: 家具箱（小） (small furniture box)
   - **Strategic Value**: Side reward on certain routes

#### Whirlpool Node
1. **Node G: うずしお (Whirlpool)**
   - **Fuel Loss**: Base 35%, actual loss 28% (maximum loss 30%)
   - **Radar Reduction**: Each radar equipped reduces loss percentage (0 radars: ‑28%, 1 radar: ‑21%, 2 radars: ‑16.8%, etc.)
   - **Strategic Impact**: Forces players to equip radars or accept fuel penalty; influences routing decisions

#### Non‑Combat Nodes
- **Node D**: “帰ろう。帰れば、また来ることができる。” – safe retreat point (no combat)
- **Node E**: “突入海域の霧が……” – fog (no combat)
- **Node F**: “天祐は我に在り。艦隊泊地突入！救出作戦開始！” – successful insertion (no combat)

#### Combat Nodes (see Enemy Analysis for details)
- **Node A**: 敵北方遊撃任務部隊 – light cruiser/destroyer opposition
- **Node C**: 敵水雷戦隊 – destroyer/light cruiser/ torpedo cruiser groups
- **Node H**: 敵北方水上打撃艦隊 – contains battleship Ru‑class elite
- **Node J**: 敵北方攻撃任務群 先遣護衛戦隊 – heavier cruiser/destroyer groups
- **Node K**: 敵北方攻撃任務群 機動部隊 – carrier‑battleship combined fleet (dangerous)
- **Node L (BOSS)**: 敵キス島包囲艦隊 – transport‑escort fleet (primary target)

## Routing Rules

### Ship‑Class Definitions (as of 2022/11/13)
- **軽巡級**: 軽巡＋練巡＋**雷巡** (light cruisers, training cruisers, **torpedo cruisers**)
- **戦艦級**: 高速戦艦・(低速)戦艦・航空戦艦 (fast battleships, slow battleships, aviation battleships)
- **空母系**: 正規空母・装甲空母・軽空母 (standard carriers, armored carriers, light carriers)
- **重巡級**: 重巡・航空巡洋艦 (heavy cruisers, aviation cruisers)
- **補給艦**: 補給艦・揚陸艦・工作艦・潜水母艦など (supply ships, landing ships, repair ships, submarine tenders)
- **海防艦**: 海防艦 (coastal defense ships)

### Route Strategies (from Player Guides)
1. **Boss‑Accessible Routes**:
   - **CEFL** (2 combat nodes + whirlpool): Requires fast+ unified fleet with at least one radar.
   - **CGFL** (2 combat nodes + whirlpool): Similar but via G whirlpool.
   - **CGHFL** (3 combat nodes + whirlpool): Default route for non‑optimized fleets.
2. **H‑Node Avoidance**: Equip radars and achieve fast+ unification to reduce chance of facing battleship Ru‑class elite at node H.
3. **J/K Diversion**: Fleets with carriers or battleships are forced to nodes J/K, which are dead‑ends or extremely dangerous for destroyer fleets.

## Enemy Analysis

### Node A: 敵北方遊撃任務部隊 (Enemy Northern Raiding Task Force)
- **EXP**: 150‑190
- **Patterns**: 6 variations, escalating with more 重巡リ級elite and 軽母ヌ級elite
- **Typical Composition**: 2‑3 heavy cruisers, 1‑2 light cruisers, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Trapezoid (梯形)
- **Air Power**: Only pattern 5‑6 have light carrier (制空値 24/36/72)

### Node C: 敵水雷戦隊 (Enemy Torpedo Squadron)
- **EXP**: 160‑180
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, mostly destroyers and light cruisers
- **Typical Composition**: 1 light cruiser flagship, 3‑5 destroyers; pattern 2 has 3 torpedo cruisers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Double line (複縦)

### Node H: 敵北方水上打撃艦隊 (Enemy Northern Surface Strike Fleet)
- **EXP**: 200‑240
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, includes 戦艦ル級elite (battleship)
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 battleships, 1‑2 heavy cruisers, 1 light cruiser, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Trapezoid (梯形)
- **Threat Level**: High – battleship can cause major damage to destroyers

### Node J: 敵北方攻撃任務群 先遣護衛戦隊 (Enemy Northern Attack Group Vanguard Escort)
- **EXP**: 170‑220
- **Patterns**: 6 variations, escalating with more flagship‑class heavy cruisers
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 heavy cruiser flagships, 1‑2 light cruiser flagships, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦)

### Node K: 敵北方攻撃任務群 機動部隊 (Enemy Northern Attack Group Mobile Force)
- **EXP**: 220‑320
- **Patterns**: 6 variations, includes 空母ヲ級flagship/elite and 戦艦ル級flagship/elite
- **Typical Composition**: 2 carriers, 1‑2 battleships, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Diamond (輪形)
- **Air Power**: 54‑84 (enemy) / 116‑168 (required for air superiority)
- **Threat Level**: Extreme – not intended for destroyer fleets

### Node L (BOSS): 敵キス島包囲艦隊 (Enemy Kiska Island Blockade Fleet)
- **EXP**: 340‑390
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, focused on transport escorts
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 light cruiser flagships, 2‑4 destroyers, 1‑2 transport Wa‑class (pattern 3 has elite transport)
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Double line (複縦)
- **Objective**: Sink transports for higher rank; flagship is light cruiser

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. Composition as Gatekeeper
- **Strict Limits**: Boss access locked to destroyer‑light‑auxiliary combinations; any deviation routes away.
- **Educational Intent**: Forces players to invest in destroyer/light cruiser development and understand ship‑class roles.
- **Progression Check**: Ensures players have built a competent torpedo squadron before advancing in Northern Waters.

### 2. Speed & Equipment Matter
- **Speed‑Based Routing**: “Fast+” unification enables safer routes; slow ships guarantee tougher nodes.
- **Radar Utility**: Reduces whirlpool losses and influences routing – encourages equipment diversity.
- **Trade‑offs**: Radar slots compete with combat gear, creating meaningful choices.

### 3. Risk‑Reward Whirlpool
- **Fuel Penalty**: Whirlpool can cripple fuel reserves before boss, encouraging radar use or route selection.
- **Predictable Reduction**: Formulaic radar reduction rewards planning and equipment investment.

### 4. Enemy Escalation
- **Controlled Difficulty**: Early nodes (A, C) are destroyer‑friendly; later nodes (H, K) introduce battleships/carriers as punishment for composition mistakes.
- **Boss Design**: Boss node is transport‑escort, rewarding players who can sink weakly‑armed but high‑HP targets.

### 5. Farming & Side Activities
- **Ammunition Farming**: Node B provides substantial ammunition, making the map attractive for resource gathering.
- **Low‑Level Training**: Node C’s weak enemy composition allows safe leveling of new ships (historic “3‑2‑1” training ground).
- **Event Utility**: Nodes J/K often host event‑specific drops (e.g., autumn saury), giving the map recurring relevance.

### 6. Mission Integration
- Numerous one‑time and seasonal missions require 3‑2 boss clears, often with specific destroyer squadrons (e.g., “精鋭「第十九駆逐隊」、全力出撃！”).

## Implementation Considerations for FleetMemories

### Core Mechanics to Replicate
1. **Strict Composition Lock**: Boss reachable only with destroyer‑centric fleets; heavy ships automatically divert.
2. **Speed‑Based Routing**: Fast+ unification enables optimal routes; slow ships guarantee harder battles.
3. **Radar‑Dependent Whirlpool**: Radar equipment reduces fuel loss, giving utility to otherwise combat‑weak gear.
4. **Resource‑Node Farming**: Ammunition node with equipment‑based bonuses encourages bringing non‑combat gear.
5. **Enemy Progression**: Early nodes destroyer‑focused, later nodes introduce battleships/carriers as “punishment” routes.

### Adaptation Suggestions
1. **Simplify Branching Complexity**: Reduce number of conditional branches while preserving the “destroyers only for boss” core.
2. **Adjust Whirlpool Penalty**: Scale fuel loss to FleetMemories’ economy; keep radar reduction meaningful but not mandatory.
3. **Modify Enemy Types**: Use equivalent enemy classes from FleetMemories’ roster (e.g., replace 戦艦ル級 with a heavy battleship type).
4. **Preserve Educational Intent**: Keep the lesson that light ships have unique routing advantages and heavy fleets face extra challenges.
5. **Maintain Farming Viability**: Allow ammunition farming to remain a viable resource‑gathering activity.

### Balance Considerations
- **Difficulty Curve**: Position as a significant spike after 3‑1, requiring dedicated destroyer/light cruiser development.
- **Resource Economy**: Ammunition yields should be attractive but not game‑breaking.
- **Enemy Strength**: Ensure battleship nodes (H) are threatening but not impossible for a well‑prepared destroyer fleet.
- **Route Viability**: All boss‑accessible routes should be feasible with appropriate equipment and levels.

### Learning Objectives to Preserve
1. **Ship‑Class Roles**: Destroyers and light cruisers are essential for certain objectives.
2. **Equipment Utility**: Non‑combat equipment (radar, drums) provides tangible benefits.
3. **Speed Matters**: Fleet speed influences routing and safety.
4. **Composition Discipline**: Precise fleet building is required for success.
5. **Risk Management**: Whirlpool penalties encourage planning and equipment choices.
