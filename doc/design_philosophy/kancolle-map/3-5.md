# Kantai Collection Map 3-5 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 3‑5: 北方海域戦闘哨戒 (Northern Waters Combat Patrol)** – A 9‑star difficulty monthly map with HP‑gauge mechanics, introducing land‑based abyssal (北方棲姫) as a mid‑boss, and offering distinct upper (heavy fleet) and lower (light fleet) route strategies.

### Purpose
- **Monthly Challenge**: Designed as a recurring monthly map with resetting HP gauge and time‑limited rewards (勋章, special ranking points)
- **Route Specialization**: Clear separation between upper route (battleship/carrier heavy) and lower route (destroyer/light cruiser focused)
- **Land‑Based Boss Introduction**: First appearance of 北方棲姫 (Northern Princess) as a mandatory mid‑boss on upper route, teaching land‑based combat mechanics
- **HP Gauge Mechanics**: Introduces boss‑flagship‑sink‑based gauge reduction (25% per sink) rather than damage‑based
- **Time‑Limited Rewards**: Monthly reset with special rewards available only until month‑end deadlines
- **Northern Waters Finale**: Concludes World 3 with the most complex mechanics and highest difficulty

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (9 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 80‑100 ships (well‑equipped with high‑end gear)
- **Resource Cost**: Very high fuel/ammo consumption, especially on upper route with multiple heavy combat nodes
- **Repair Cost**: Extreme risk of damage at nodes D (carrier task force), H (Northern Princess), and K (boss)
- **EXP Range**: 300‑600 (Northern Princess node 600, boss node 500‑550)
- **HQ Level Scaling**: LoS requirements scale with HQ level (120 is baseline)

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear 3‑4 ("北方海域艦隊決戦")
- **Monthly Reset**: HP gauge resets at beginning of each month; map becomes available after clearing 3‑4 once

### Route Strategies (from Player Guides)

1. **Upper Route (BDHK) – Heavy Fleet**:
   - **Conditions**: Submarine ≥3 OR Battleship ≥2 OR (Battleship+Heavy Cruiser) ≥3 OR Carrier/Torpedo Cruiser present
   - **Combat Nodes**: B (vanguard), D (carrier task force), H (Northern Princess), J (ammo node), K (boss)
   - **Advantages**: Can bring powerful battleships/carriers; shorter route (4 combat nodes)
   - **Disadvantages**: Must face Northern Princess (land‑based boss) and carrier‑battleship task force

2. **Lower Route (FEHK) – Light Fleet**:
   - **Conditions**: Destroyer ≥5 (guarantees F), Destroyer =4 (75% F), then follow F→E conditions
   - **Combat Nodes**: F (torpedo cruiser), E (battleship‑submarine), H (Northern Princess), J (ammo), K (boss)
   - **Advantages**: Avoids heavy carrier‑battleship node D; destroyer‑focused fleets cheaper
   - **Disadvantages**: Torpedo cruiser threat at F; submarine threat at E; still must face Northern Princess

3. **Alternative Upper Route (ADHK) – Carrier‑Heavy**:
   - **Conditions**: Submarine ≥4 OR Carrier ≥4 OR (Battleship+Carrier) ≥5 etc.
   - **Combat Nodes**: A (non‑combat), D (carrier task force), H (Northern Princess), J, K
   - **Advantages**: Can bring maximum carrier power
   - **Disadvantages**: Must meet strict composition requirements

4. **Submarine‑Focused Route**:
   - **Conditions**: 3‑4 submarines
   - **Purpose**: Use submarines as tanks against Northern Princess (she prioritizes submarines in shelling)
   - **Strategy**: Submarines absorb Northern Princess's powerful attacks while carriers deal damage

### Enemy Design Philosophy

1. **Progressive Difficulty Ramp**:
   - **Node B** (300 EXP): Introductory combat – teaches players to handle elite battleships/heavy cruisers
   - **Nodes D/F/E/G** (310–450 EXP): Intermediate threats – specialize in carrier, torpedo, submarine, or light‑carrier warfare
   - **Node H** (600 EXP): **Land‑based boss** – massive difficulty spike requiring specialized equipment/strategy
   - **Node K** (500–550 EXP): Final boss – relatively easier than H but must be tackled after resource/ammo depletion

2. **Specialized Role Checks**:
   - **Anti‑Carrier**: Node D demands strong fighter coverage and anti‑carrier firepower
   - **ASW**: Node E forces at least 1–2 ships with depth‑charges/sonar
   - **Anti‑Torpedo**: Node F's triple‑torpedo‑cruiser setup punishes low‑evasion fleets
   - **Anti‑Land**: Node H requires **三式弾** (Type 3 Shell), **WG42**, or **特二式内火艇** (Amphibious Tank) for effective damage

3. **Land‑Based Boss Mechanics (北方棲姫)**:
   - **Immune to torpedoes**: Cannot be targeted by opening/closing torpedo attacks
   - **Ignores submarine targeting in shelling**: Submarines are prioritized by Northern Princess (useful for tanking)
   - **Requires special equipment**: Standard AP shells ineffective; needs anti‑land gear
   - **Progressive strengthening**: "Final形態" appears after 3 boss kills (HP gauge ≤25%) – air power jumps from ~130 to ~250+

4. **Air Power Escalation**:
   - **Node D**: 112–214 (requires ~300–400 fighter power for AS+)
   - **Node H**: 130–254 (requires ~400–500 fighter power for AS+)
   - **Post‑strengthening H**: 250+ (requires ~600+ fighter power for AS+)
   - Teaches importance of **熟練艦載機** (veteran aircraft) and high‑end fighters (烈風, 試製烈風)

5. **Formation Variety**:
   - **Single‑column (単縦)**: Node B, F – maximizes enemy firepower
   - **Double‑column (複縦)**: Node E, K – balanced offense/defense
   - **Circular (輪形)**: Node D, G, H – enhances anti‑air defense
   - Strategic implication: Player must consider how formation affects both enemy offense and their own accuracy

### Non‑Combat & Resource Nodes

1. **False‑Alarm Nodes (A, C, I)**:
   - "気のせいだった。" ("It was just my imagination.")
   - Provide psychological relief and random route variation
   - No combat, no resources

2. **Ammunition Node (J)**:
   - **弾薬+50** (Ammo +50), **ドラム缶+10** with drum cans
   - Critical resource recovery before boss on both upper/lower routes
   - Teaches importance of **resource‑node timing** in long sorties

### HP‑Gauge‑Dependent Enemy Strengthening

- **Gauge ≤25%** (after 3 boss kills): Nodes E, F, G, H switch to "最終形態" (final form)
  - Node E: Adds 軽母ヌ級flagship (light carrier) – introduces air threat
  - Node F: Upgrades 重巡リ級elite → flagship, improves escort
  - Node G: Adds 軽母ヌ級flagship, improves escort
  - Node H: Northern Princess upgrades to "最終形態 強/弱" – huge air‑power increase
- **Design intent**: Prevents brute‑force grinding; requires adaptation to strengthened enemies
- **Monthly reset**: Gauge resets on 1st of month – enemies revert to normal forms

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. **Monthly Challenge Structure**
   - **Reset‑Based Progression**: HP gauge resets monthly, creating recurring engagement
   - **Time‑Limited Rewards**: Special rewards (勲章, 特別戦果) available only until month‑end deadlines
   - **Progressive Difficulty**: Enemy strength scales with gauge depletion (25%, 50%, 75% thresholds)
   - **Player‑Paced Completion**: No rush – can be cleared over several days within the month

### 2. **Route Specialization & Fleet Diversity**
   - **Upper Route (BDHK)**: Battleship/carrier heavy – tests high‑end fleet construction and resource management
   - **Lower Route (FEHK)**: Destroyer/light‑cruiser focused – tests evasion, torpedo combat, and low‑cost strategies
   - **Submarine Route**: Niche strategy using subs as tanks – teaches advanced mechanics (land‑based boss targeting)
   - **Design Intent**: Forces players to maintain diverse ship rosters and adapt strategies to available resources

### 3. **Land‑Based Boss Tutorial**
   - **First Encounter**: Introduces 北方棲姫 (Northern Princess) as mandatory mid‑boss
   - **Mechanics Tutorial**: Teaches anti‑land equipment (三式弾, WG42, 特二式内火艇), immunity to torpedoes, submarine tanking
   - **Progressive Learning**: Initial weak form → final strong form after gauge depletion
   - **Air‑Power Escalation**: Teaches importance of veteran aircraft and high‑end fighters

### 4. **Resource‑Node Strategy**
   - **Ammo Recovery (Node J)**: Positioned before boss on both routes – teaches resource‑management timing
   - **False‑Alarm Nodes (A, C, I)**: Provide psychological breaks and route‑variation RNG
   - **Drum‑Can Bonus**: Encourages transport‑ship experimentation (揚陸艦)

### 5. **HP‑Gauge Mechanics Evolution**
   - **Boss‑Flagship‑Sink Based**: 25% reduction per boss‑flagship sink (not damage‑based)
   - **Enemy Strengthening**: Gauge depletion triggers "最終形態" upgrades – prevents brute‑force grinding
   - **Monthly Reset Psychology**: Creates "fresh start" feeling each month, encourages repeated engagement

### 6. **Northern Waters Theme Consistency**
   - **Enemy Naming**: All forces prefixed "北方" (Northern) – 前衛警戒部隊, 派遣任務部隊, 遊撃部隊, 増援部隊
   - **Environmental Storytelling**: "AL泊地" (AL Anchorage) references historical Aleutian Islands campaign
   - **Progression from 3‑1 to 3‑5**: Starts with simple patrol (3‑1) → evacuation operation (3‑2) → advance (3‑3) → decisive battle (3‑4) → monthly patrol (3‑5)

### 5. **Monthly Challenge System**
   - **HP Gauge**: Start at 100%, reduce 25% per boss‑flagship sink
