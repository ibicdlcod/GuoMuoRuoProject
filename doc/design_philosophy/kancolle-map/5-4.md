# Kantai Collection Map 5-4 Design Philosophy
## 東京急行 (Tokyo Express)

### Map Overview
**Purpose:** Final map of Southern Waters, combining carrier‑focused upper route with night‑battle‑focused central route, teaching players to choose between air superiority or night combat specialization.

**Difficulty:** ☆(☆×10) (10 stars) – high difficulty, requires either heavy carrier investment or specialized night battle equipment.

**Unlock Requirements:**
- Clear map 5‑3 (第一次サーモン沖海戦)
- Final map of Southern Waters, testing all skills learned in World 5

**Notable Features:**
- **HP Gauge:** Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by 20% (5 sinks required to clear)
- **Dual‑route design:** Upper route (carrier‑heavy) vs Central route (night‑battle‑heavy)
- **Resource nodes:** Ammunition (I) with transport equipment bonuses
- **Whirlpools:** Two whirlpool nodes (D, K) with fuel drain penalties
- **Night battle nodes:** F, H, J are night‑only combat nodes
- **Historical theme:** Based on the Tokyo Express (鼠輸送) supply runs during the Guadalcanal campaign

**BGM Changes:**
- Map: "艦隊集結"
- Route daytime: "二水戦の航跡"
- Route night: "次発装填、再突入!"
- Boss daytime: "決戦！鉄底海峡を抜けて"
- Boss night: "アイアンボトムサウンド"

### Node Layout
Map features **16 nodes** (A‑P) with complex branching that separates into two main routes early, then converges before boss.

**Key Node Types:**
- **A, B, M, N, O** – No combat (戦闘なし)
- **C** – Surface combat (敵南方増援部隊 前方警戒艦隊) – light cruiser/destroyer force with possible submarine
- **D, K** – **Whirlpool** (うずしお) – fuel drain (30% base at D, 40%/150% at K)
- **E** – Surface combat (敵鉄底海峡哨戒隊) – light cruiser/destroyer force
- **F, H, J** – **夜戦** (Night Battle) – heavy cruiser/battleship night combat
- **G** – Carrier‑based enemy (敵南方増援部隊 機動部隊) – 2×空母ヲ級flagship with high air power
- **I** – **Item (Ammo)** +60 ammunition, bonus with drum cans (+10) and landing craft types (+15)
- **L** – Surface combat (敵南方増援部隊 本隊) – battleship with cruiser/destroyer escort
- **P** – **Boss** (敵南方増援部隊 本隊) – 輸送ワ級flagship with 軽母ヌ級flagship and 戦艦ル級flagship

**Route Structure:**
Two primary route families with early branching:
- **Upper Route (Carrier‑focused):** A → B → C → G → (K) → L → P
  - Requires carriers, faces high‑air‑power node G and battleship node L
  - Boss relatively easy after difficult route
- **Central Route (Night‑battle‑focused):** A → B → E → H → I → J → M → P
  - Requires no carriers/submarines, emphasizes destroyer/light cruiser composition
  - Must pass through night battle nodes F/H/J, boss air power challenging without carriers
- **Variants:** Central route has sub‑variants with/without whirlpool (D) and different branching based on fleet composition

### Routing Rules (ルート分岐法則)
**Updated:** 2020‑06‑08

**Key Branching Conditions:**

1. **Start → A/B:**
   - **B if:** Contains空母系 **OR** ドラム缶搭載艦＋大発動艇系搭載艦合計≥5 **OR** 駆逐≥4 **OR** (軽巡1隻 **AND** 駆逐≥3)
   - **A otherwise** (default route)

2. **B → C/D:**
   - **D if:** Contains潜水艦 **OR** 戦艦級≥5 **OR** 駆逐≥2 **OR** 重巡級≥3
   - **F otherwise** (proceeds to next branching)

3. **C → G/K:**
   - **K if:** Contains潜水艦? **OR** 戦艦級≥4?
   - **L if:** 正規空母≤2
   - **Random K‑biased (70%) otherwise**

4. **F → E/H:**
   - **H if:** Contains潜水艦 **OR** (低速)戦艦≥2 **OR** 戦艦級≥3
   - **E if:** 駆逐≥2
   - **F otherwise**

5. **E → D/E (from C):**
   - **D if:** Contains空母系 **OR** 潜水艦 **OR** (戦艦級含む **AND** 速力:低速) **OR** (航空戦艦+(低速)戦艦)≥2
   - **E if:** 高速+以上統一 **OR** 軽巡1隻 **AND** 駆逐≥3 **OR** 駆逐≥4
   - **Random D/E otherwise**

6. **L → N/P (Upper Route):**
   - **P if:** 高速+以上統一 **OR** 索敵スコア≥60? (分岐点係数=2)
   - **N if:** 索敵スコア<56?
   - **Random if:** 56?≤索敵スコア<60? **OR** (戦艦級+正規空母)≥5

7. **M → O/P (Central Route):**
   - **P if:** 高速+以上統一 **OR** 索敵スコア≥45
   - **O if:** 索敵スコア<41?
   - **Random if:** 41?≤索敵スコア<45
   - **Note:** 潜水艦を含むとPの割合が0.7倍になる

**Reconnaissance (索敵) Notes:**
- **Branching coefficient:** 2 for this map
- **Recon scores:** Values shown for HQ level 120; lower HQ levels need +1 margin
- **Random ranges:** Each +1 recon score increases destination probability linearly (20% per +1 for L/M branching)

**Speed Notes:**
- **高速+以上統一:** All ships at high‑speed+ or faster
- Speed enhancements reference: [速力](/kancolle/%E9%80%9F%E5%8A%9B)

#### **Boss Node**
**P – "敵南方増援部隊 本隊"**

**Patterns 1‑6:**
- **Common:** 輸送ワ級flagship (HP130), 軽母ヌ級flagship(艦載機赤), battleship, cruiser, 2× destroyer
- **Battleship variants:** 戦艦ル級flagship (patterns 1‑4) or 戦艦タ級flagship (patterns 5‑6)
- **Cruiser variants:** 軽巡ツ級elite (patterns 1,3,5) or 重巡ネ級elite (patterns 2,4,6)
- **Destroyer variants:** 駆逐ロ級後期型 (patterns 1‑2) or 駆逐ハ級後期型 (patterns 3‑6)

**Air Power:**
- **Fighter power:** 93 (軽母ヌ級flagship)
- **Air supremacy thresholds:** 63 for parity, 140 for superiority, 279 for supremacy
- **Formation:** Circular (輪形) fixed

**EXP:** 510‑550

**Design Intent:** Boss features transport ship as flagship with strong air escort. Circular formation reduces enemy firepower but air power challenge is significant, especially for central route without carriers. Requires players to balance night combat capability with air power generation.

### Resource Nodes
**I – Ammunition Collection:**
- **Base:** +60 ammunition
- **Bonus equipment:** Drum cans (+10), Landing craft types (+15)
- **Maximum:** 180
- **Strategic value:** Provides ammunition replenishment during central route, encourages transport equipment usage

**D, K – Whirlpool Fuel Drain:**
- **D:** 30% base fuel loss, radar mitigation (0‑6 radar reduce loss)
- **K:** 40% or 150% base fuel loss, two different loss patterns with radar mitigation
- **Design intent:** Penalize radar‑poor fleets, add resource management challenge
- **Strategic consideration:** Encourages radar equipment on certain routes

### Design Patterns Observed

#### 1. **Dual‑Route Philosophy**
- **Upper Route (Carrier‑focused):** Requires carriers, faces extreme air power at node G (206 fighter power)
- **Central Route (Night‑battle‑focused):** Prohibits carriers/submarines, emphasizes destroyer/light cruiser composition
- **Player choice:** Allows selection based on fleet strengths – carriers vs night specialization
- **Convergence:** Both routes converge before boss, maintaining map cohesion

#### 2. **Air Power Extremes**
- **Node G:** Consistent 206 fighter power across 6 patterns (309/618 air supremacy)
- **Boss Node:** 93 fighter power (軽母ヌ級flagship with red aircraft)
- **Design intent:** Force players to build for air superiority or accept disadvantage
- **Central route challenge:** No carriers makes air power generation difficult – requires innovative solutions (伊勢改二, 日向改二, 強風改, 二式水戦改)

#### 3. **Night Battle Node Progression**
- **Central route:** Must pass through F → H → J night battle nodes
- **Escalating threat:** F (battleship) → H (heavy cruiser) → J (transport farming)
- **Resource consumption:** Night nodes consume fuel/ammunition (standard night battle rules)
- **Equipment requirements:** Night scouts, starshells, searchlights, skilled lookouts

#### 4. **Transport Equipment Bonuses**
- **Node I:** Drum cans (+10) and landing craft types (+15) increase ammunition yield
- **Branching conditions:** Drum cans/landing craft count affects initial routing (合計≥5 sends to B)
- **Design intent:** Encourage transport equipment usage beyond expedition fleets

#### 5. **Reconnaissance‑Based Branching**
- **Multiple recon checks:** L→N/P and M→O/P depend on recon score (分岐点係数=2)
- **Random ranges:** 41‑45 and 56‑60 recon score ranges produce random branching
- **Linear probability:** Each +1 recon score increases destination probability by 20%
- **Design intent:** Force players to optimize recon without guaranteeing success

#### 6. **Speed‑Based Routing**
- **高速+以上統一:** Required for optimal routing at multiple points
- **Speed categories:** 低速 vs 高速+ distinctions affect branching
- **Design intent:** Encourage fleet speed tuning and modernization

#### 7. **Whirlpool Resource Management**
- **Two whirlpools:** D (30% base) and K (40%/150% base)
- **Radar mitigation:** 0‑6 radar progressively reduce loss percentage
- **Strategic choice:** Some routes avoid whirlpools, others require passing through them
- **Design intent:** Add resource risk/reward decisions

#### 8. **Transport Ship Farming**
- **Node J:** 6 patterns of transport ships (輸送ワ級)
- **High EXP values:** 450‑490 for relatively low threat
- **Night battle only:** Requires night combat capability
- **Design intent:** Provide farming opportunity within challenging map

#### 9. **Historical Theme Integration**
- **"Tokyo Express":** References Japanese supply runs to Guadalcanal
- **Boss composition:** 輸送ワ級flagship as Tokyo Express representative
- **Enemy types:** Southern Waters enemy roster (ヌ級flagship, ル級flagship)
- **Design intent:** Connect gameplay to historical naval operations

#### 10. **Task‑Driven Fleet Building**
- **Multiple quarterly tasks:** Require specific fleet compositions (三一駆, 三川艦隊, 六水戦, 第三戦隊)
- **Route restrictions:** Tasks force use of specific routes based on composition
- **Design intent:** Encourage players to experiment with different fleet types
- **Reward structure:** Bonus ranking points for task completion

### Conclusion
Map 5‑4 serves as the culmination of Southern Waters, testing all skills learned in World 5 through a dual‑route design that forces players to choose between carrier‑focused air superiority or night‑battle‑focused specialization. The extreme air power at node G and recon‑based branching add strategic depth, while transport equipment bonuses and whirlpool resource management encourage diverse fleet building. Historical theming connects gameplay to the Tokyo Express supply runs, and multiple quarterly tasks provide long‑term engagement.

For FleetMemories, this map provides a template for dual‑route philosophy, recon‑based branching with linear probability, extreme air power challenges, transport equipment integration, and task‑driven fleet composition. The whirlpool mechanics offer resource risk/reward decisions, while the transport farming node maintains gameplay variety within a challenging final map.
