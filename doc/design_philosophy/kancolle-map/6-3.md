# Kantai Collection Map 6-3 Design Philosophy
**Operation K** (K作戦)

## Map Overview
- **Purpose**: Test specialized light‑fleet composition (seaplane tenders, light cruisers, destroyers only); introduces reconnaissance‑based resource mechanics
- **Difficulty**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (7 stars), moderate‑high
- **Unlock Requirements**: Likely requires 6‑2 clear (MS Islands Defense Battle)
- **HP Gauge**: Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by ¼ (25%); 4 sinks required to clear
- **Unique Drop**: 雲龍 (Unryuu) – only obtainable here, not buildable
- **Fleet Restrictions**: **Only Seaplane Tender (水母), Light Cruiser/Training Cruiser (軽巡/練巡), Destroyer (駆逐) allowed** (Torpedo Cruisers prohibited)
  - Must include at least 1 of each allowed type
  - Seaplane Tender: max 2 ships
  - Light Cruiser + Training Cruiser: max 3 ships
- **Boss Victory Bonus**: B victory or higher at boss node grants resources from reconnaissance nodes G/H based on success level (failure gives nothing)
- **Historical Context**: Simulates Operation K – long‑range reconnaissance of the Marshall Islands by flying boats (Kawanishi H8K)

## Node Layout
```
Start → A (active branching) → B/C → D → E → F → G/H/I → J (Boss)
```
**Node Types**:
- **A**: Active branching (能動分岐) – player chooses between northern (B) or southern (C) route
- **B**: Mixed submarine/surface – anchorage patrol line (泊地哨戒線): submarine fleet (カ級 elite + regular) OR surface fleet (light cruiser flagship + destroyers)
- **C**: Submarine battle (対潜戦) – deep‑sea submarine fleet (ヨ級 elite, ソ級 elite, カ級)
- **D**: Surface – deep‑sea picket fleet (深海ピケット艦隊): light cruiser flagship + destroyers
- **E**: Surface – deep‑sea patrol torpedo squadron (深海哨戒水雷戦隊): light cruiser flagship + torpedo cruiser elites + destroyers
- **F**: Surface – deep‑sea central surface strike group (深海中部水上打撃群): heavy cruiser elite + light cruiser elites + destroyers
- **G**: Reconnaissance (航空偵察) – no combat, resource gain based on success
- **H**: Reconnaissance (航空偵察) – no combat, resource gain based on success  
- **I**: No combat (戦闘なし) – mission failure (enemy not sighted)
- **J (Boss)**: Main force –留守泊地旗艦艦隊 (fast battleship flagship + heavy cruiser flagship + destroyer princess + destroyers)

## Routing Rules
**Key Branching Conditions**:

### A → B/C
- **Active branching (能動分岐)**: Player manually selects either:
  - **B** (northern route): 2 battles (B→D) – mixed submarine/surface threat
  - **C** (southern route): 1 submarine battle (C) – pure submarine threat

### F → G/H/I
- **G** if ALL of:
  - Seaplane Tender ≤1
  - Light Cruiser ≤1  
  - Destroyer ≥3
- **Random 60% F / 40% ?** if:
  - Light Cruiser ≤2
- **F** (default) otherwise

### I → J
- **I** (failure) if:
  - LoS score <36 (HQ 120, branch coefficient 3)
- **Random I/J** if:
  - LoS score ≥36 AND <38
- **J** (boss) if:
  - LoS score ≥38

**LoS Notes**:
- Branch coefficient: **3** (high)
- LoS scores listed are for HQ level 120; lower HQ levels need +1 or more margin
- LoS calculation uses standard formula (索敵スコア計算式)

## Enemy Analysis
**Node B – Anchorage Patrol Line**:
- **Submarine patterns** (90‑100 EXP):
  - Pattern 1: 潜水カ級elite ×1 + 潜水カ級 ×3 (梯形/単横陣)
  - Pattern 2: 潜水カ級elite ×1 + 潜水カ級 ×4
- **Surface patterns** (100‑110 EXP):
  - Pattern 3: 駆逐ロ級後期型 ×3 (単縦/複縦陣)
  - Pattern 4: 軽巡ヘ級flagship + 駆逐ロ級後期型 ×2

**Node C – Submarine Battle**:
- All patterns feature elite submarines (ヨ級elite, ソ級elite) with regular カ級 escorts
- Pattern 1 (90 EXP): 潜水ヨ級elite ×2 + 潜水カ級 ×3
- Pattern 2 (100 EXP): 潜水ヨ級elite ×1 + 潜水カ級elite ×2 + 潜水カ級 ×3
- Pattern 3 (110 EXP): 潜水ソ級elite ×1 + 潜水ヨ級elite ×1 + 潜水カ級elite ×1 + 潜水カ級 ×2
- **Formation**:梯形陣 appears 2‑3× more often than 単横陣

**Node D – Deep‑Sea Picket Fleet**:
- 6 patterns (100‑150 EXP) featuring 軽巡ヘ級flagship with varying destroyer escorts (ロ級後期型, イ級後期型)
- Formation alternates between 複縦 and 単縦 across patterns

**Node E – Deep‑Sea Patrol Torpedo Squadron**:
- 3 patterns (200‑220 EXP) featuring 軽巡ヘ級flagship or 軽巡ツ級elite + 雷巡チ級elite ×2 + destroyers
- High torpedo threat; formations include 単縦 and 複縦

**Node F – Deep‑Sea Central Surface Strike Group**:
- 6 patterns (200‑250 EXP) featuring 重巡ネ級elite as flagship with 軽巡ト級elite(A), 軽巡ホ級elite, and destroyer escorts
- Heavy cruiser elite presents significant firepower; formations alternate 複縦/単縦

**Node G/H – Reconnaissance**:
- No combat; success determined by boss victory (B+)
- Resource yields depend on success level (see Resource Nodes)

**Node I – No Combat**:
- “敵影を見ず。本作戦完遂失敗。” (No enemy sighted. Mission failure.)
- Occurs if LoS insufficient from F branch

**Node J (Boss) – Main Force**:
- **Pre‑clear patterns** (400 EXP each):
  - Patterns 1‑5: 戦艦タ級flagship (1‑2) + 重巡リ級flagship (1‑2) + 軽巡ヘ級flagship (pattern 1 only) + 駆逐棲姫(A) + destroyers (ロ級/ハ級/ニ級後期型)
  - **Pattern distribution**: 1:2:3:4:5 ≈ 6:7:11:15:61% (pattern 5 dominates at ~61%)
  - **Formation**: 単縦陣固定
- **Final form (pattern 6)**:
  - 駆逐棲姫(B) (flagship) + 戦艦タ級flagship ×2 + 重巡リ級flagship ×1 + 駆逐ニ級後期型 ×2
  - Destroyer Princess (B) has enhanced stats (190 HP, 115 armor)
  - Extreme difficulty with two fast battleship flagships

## Resource Nodes
**Reconnaissance Nodes G/H**:
- Resources granted only if boss node achieves B victory or higher
- **Success levels**:
  - **Success**: Ammo 50/60/80, Bauxite 30/40/50, Development Material 1
  - **Great Success**: Ammo 100/150/200, Bauxite 80/100/150, Development Material 2/3/4
- **Failure** (C defeat or lower at boss, or routing to I): No resources
- **Maximum gain**: Up to 8 development materials per sortie (both nodes great success)

## Design Patterns Observed
1. **Specialized Fleet Restriction**: Forces players to master seaplane tender + light cruiser + destroyer synergy, prohibiting heavy ships
2. **Active Branching**: Player choice between submarine‑heavy (C) or mixed (B) route teaches strategic route selection
3. **Reconnaissance‑Resource Link**: Boss victory gates reconnaissance rewards, creating risk‑reward tension
4. **LoS‑Gated Boss Access**: High LoS requirement (coefficient 3) ensures players optimize reconnaissance equipment
5. **Submarine‑Surface Mix**: Node B randomly presents submarine OR surface fleet, requiring flexible anti‑submarine capability
6. **Torpedo Squadron Emphasis**: Node E features torpedo cruiser elites, teaching players to prioritize torpedo threats
7. **Final‑Form Princess**: Destroyer Princess (B) as flagship with enhanced stats creates dramatic difficulty spike for gauge clear
8. **Pattern‑Weighted Boss**: Post‑clear boss heavily weighted toward pattern 5 (~61%), maintaining challenge after initial clear
9. **Resource‑Efficient Farming**: Low ship‑count restrictions enable resource‑positive farming with reconnaissance rewards
