# Kantai Collection Map 6-1 Design Philosophy
**Submarine Operation** (潜水艦作戦)

## Map Overview
- **Purpose**: Introduce submarine‑fleet gameplay; tests ability to build and command all‑submarine fleets
- **Difficulty**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (8 stars), moderate‑high
- **Unlock Requirements**: Clear World 5‑5 (Second Battle of the Salmon Sea)
- **HP Gauge**: None – cleared by achieving B victory or higher at boss node
- **Boss Reach Requirement**: At least 3 submarines (潜水艦 or 潜水空母) in fleet
- **Historical Context**: Simulates Central Pacific submarine patrol lines (哨戒線)

## Node Layout
```
Start → A/B/C → D/F → G → H/I → J/K (Boss)
```
**Node Types**:
- **A, B, G, E**: No combat (“気のせいだった”)
- **C**: Surface combat (battleship + light carrier + heavy cruiser + destroyers)
- **D**: Light surface combat (light cruiser flagship + destroyer elites)
- **F**: Light surface combat (light cruiser flagship + destroyer elites/transport ships)
- **H**: Heavy surface combat (heavy cruiser flagship + light cruiser + destroyer elites)
- **I**: Carrier battle (空母棲鬼 + standard carrier flagship + heavy cruiser)
- **J**: Transport convoy (輸送ワ級flagship ×2 + light cruiser + destroyers)
- **K**: Boss – “Enemy carrier in transit” (空母ヲ級flagship + heavy cruiser + light cruiser + destroyers)

## Routing Rules
**Key Branching Conditions**:

### Start → A/B/C
- **A** if:
  - ≥3 submarines AND no other ship types, OR
  - 1 submarine tender (exactly) + ≥3 submarines + no other types, OR
  - 1 submarine tender + 3 submarines + 2 destroyers, OR
  - 1 submarine tender + 4 submarines + 1 (light cruiser/destroyer)
- **B** if:
  - (Battleship‑class + carrier‑class + heavy cruiser‑class) ≥3, OR
  - Battleship‑class ≥2, OR
  - (Light cruiser + destroyer) = 0
- **C** otherwise (default route)

### A → D/F
- **F** if fleet contains submarine tender (潜水母艦)
- **D** otherwise

### G → H/I
- **I** if:
  - Submarines ≤2, OR
  - (Battleship‑class + carrier‑class + heavy cruiser‑class) = 2, OR
  - LoS <12 (HQ 120)
- **H** if:
  - Contains submarine tender AND LoS ≥16
- **Random** otherwise (85% H if no tender but LoS ≥16)

### E → J/K (boss‑approach branch)
- **E** if LoS <20
- **K** if:
  - Contains submarine tender AND LoS ≥25, OR
  - LoS ≥36
- **Random** between J/K for intermediate LoS values (20‑25, 25‑36)

**LoS Coefficient**: 4 (very high – requires substantial reconnaissance equipment)

## Enemy Analysis
### Surface Combat Nodes (C, D, F, H)
- **C**: Battleship flagship + light carrier flagship + heavy cruiser elite + light cruiser + destroyers (EXP 380, air power 23)
- **D**: Light cruiser flagship + destroyer elites (EXP 380)
- **F**: Light cruiser flagship + destroyer elites / transport elites (EXP 380)
- **H**: Heavy cruiser flagship + light cruiser + destroyer elites (EXP 380)

### Carrier Node (I)
- **Patterns**: 空母棲鬼 (Carrier Demon) + standard carrier flagship + heavy cruiser flagship
- **Air Power**: 96‑180 (up to 540 for air superiority)
- **EXP**: 380
- **Notable**: Carrier Demon is a high‑threat event‑style enemy with strong air power

### Transport Node (J)
- **Patterns**: Transport flagship ×2 + light cruiser flagship + destroyers
- **EXP**: 380
- **No air power**

### Boss Node (K)
- **Patterns**: Standard carrier flagship (空母ヲ級flagship) + heavy cruiser flagship + light cruiser (elite) + destroyers
- **Air Power**: 84 (252 for air superiority)
- **EXP**: 380
- **Clear Condition**: B victory or higher (no HP gauge)

## Resource Nodes
- None (no resource or whirlpool nodes)

## Design Patterns Observed
### 1. **Submarine‑Fleet Tutorial**
- Forces player to deploy at least 3 submarines to reach boss
- Teaches submarine‑specific mechanics: opening torpedo volley, immunity to normal shelling, vulnerability to ASW
- Encourages building a dedicated submarine squadron (伊168, 伊58, 伊19, 伊8)

### 2. **ASW‑Heavy Enemy Design**
- Every combat node contains 3+ destroyers/light cruisers with ASW equipment
- Enemies have sonar + depth charge combos, making them lethal to submarines even in line‑ahead formation
- Submarines are fragile (equivalent to 潜水カ級elite) – requires careful routing and equipment

### 3. **LoS‑Gated Routing**
- High LoS coefficient (4) demands significant reconnaissance gear
- Submarine tenders improve LoS and provide routing advantages
- Teaches importance of seaplane scouts/radar on submarines

### 4. **Multiple Fleet‑Building Options**
- **All‑submarine fleet** (6 subs): Lowest resource cost, but high retreat risk due to ASW
- **Submarine tender + subs + escort**: More stable, can use tender’s seaplanes for LoS
- **Carrier + surface escort + 3 subs**: Hybrid approach with air support and surface combat power

### 5. **No HP Gauge – Single‑Clear Objective**
- Map cleared on first boss B victory
- Allows players to focus on reaching boss once rather than grinding gauge
- Reduces frustration for difficult submarine‑only fleet

### 6. **Carrier Demon as “Trap” Node**
- Node I contains 空母棲鬼, a powerful event‑style enemy
- Random routing can send unprepared fleets into this node, causing heavy losses
- Encourages players to control routing via LoS and submarine tender

### 7. **Transport Node as Alternative Path**
- Node J provides transport ships – useful for transport‑related quests
- Diversifies map utility beyond just clearing

## Implementation Notes for FleetMemories
### Map Structure
- No HP gauge – clear on first boss B victory
- Require ≥3 submarines in fleet to reach boss node
- Implement LoS checks with coefficient 4

### Routing Logic
- Implement submarine‑only and submarine‑tender routing conditions
- Add LoS‑based branching with high thresholds (12, 16, 20, 25, 36)
- Include random routing with weighted probabilities (85% H)

### Enemy Design
- **ASW emphasis**: All surface enemies should have ASW equipment
- **Carrier Demon**: Implement as special enemy with high air power (180+)
- **Boss**: Moderate air power (84) – achievable air superiority with limited carriers

### Submarine Gameplay
- Submarines should be fragile but have opening torpedo strike
- ASW attacks should be lethal – encourage players to eliminate enemy destroyers in opening phase
- Provide submarine‑specific equipment slots (seaplane scouts, radar, improved torpedoes)

### Player Guidance
- Clearly communicate submarine requirement before sortie
- Show LoS requirements for each branch
- Warn about Carrier Demon node and suggest avoidance strategies

### Reward Structure
- Boss drops should include rare submarines (伊8, 伊19, 伊58, まるゆ, 伊401) to incentivize farming
- Transport node drops useful for transport quests
