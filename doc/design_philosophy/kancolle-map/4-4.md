# Kantai Collection Map 4-4 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 4‑4: カスガダマ沖海戦 (Kasugadama Offshore Naval Battle)** – An 8‑star difficulty Western Waters map featuring an HP gauge mechanic that requires four boss flagship sinks to clear, with complex routing based on destroyer/corvette counts, battleship/carrier counts, heavy cruiser inclusion, and corvette/supply ship combinations.

### Purpose
- **HP Gauge Mechanic**: Introduces progressive boss gauge that reduces by 25% per boss flagship sink (4 sinks required)
- **Complex Fleet Composition Routing**: Multiple branching conditions based on exact ship class counts and combinations
- **Armored Carrier Princess Possibility**: Boss node may feature 装甲空母姫 (Armored Carrier Princess) as possible boss
- **Resource Node Variety**: Includes fuel and steel collection nodes with drum/landing craft bonuses
- **Anti‑Submarine Node with Visual Effects**: Submarine node E has visual effects and consumes 8% fuel with no ammunition cost
- **High Difficulty Challenge**: 8‑star difficulty provides significant challenge for mid‑game players

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (8 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 60‑80 ships with balanced fleet composition
- **Resource Cost**: High fuel/ammo consumption due to multiple combat nodes and potential resource loss
- **Repair Cost**: High risk of damage from flagship battleships, heavy cruisers, and possible Armored Carrier Princess
- **EXP Range**: 160‑480+ (varies by node, boss node 440‑480)
- **Enemy Threat Level**: Very High, with flagship battleship pairs, carrier groups, and possible boss princess

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear 西方海域4‑3 (Western Waters 4‑3)
- **Progression**: Fourth map of Western Waters, following the air raid operation theme of 4‑3
- **Strategic Requirement**: Fleet composition planning for HP gauge clearing and routing control

## Routing Rules

### Ship‑Class Definitions (as of 2026/03/27)
- **戦艦級 (Battleship‑class)**: 戦艦 (battleships), 高速戦艦 (fast battleships), 航空戦艦 (aviation battleships)
  - Includes Гангут (Gangut) as fast battleship
  - Internal data separates "高速戦艦" (fast battleships) and "(低速)戦艦" (slow battleships) but both count as 戦艦級 for routing
- **空母系 (Carrier‑class)**: 正規空母 (standard carriers), 装甲空母 (armored carriers), 軽空母 (light carriers)
  - 特設護衛空母 (special escort carriers) treated as 補給艦 (supply ships)
- **重巡級 (Heavy Cruiser‑class)**: 重巡洋艦 (heavy cruisers), 航空巡洋艦 (aviation cruisers)
- **軽巡級 (Light Cruiser‑class)**: 軽巡洋艦 (light cruisers), 練習巡洋艦 (training cruisers), **重雷装巡洋艦 (torpedo cruisers)**
- **海防艦 (Coastal Defense Ships)**: Includes 海防戦艦 (coastal defense battleships)
- **潜水艦 (Submarines)**: 潜水艦, 潜水空母 (submarine aircraft carriers)
- **補給艦 (Supply Ships)**: 補給艦, 特務艦, 灯台補給船, 南極観測船, 特設護衛空母, 砕氷艦, 雑役艦, 給糧艦, etc.

**Critical Notes:**
- Destroyer/Corvette count critical: (駆逐+海防)≥2 required for central route A→E→I→K
- Battleship/Carrier counts affect multiple branches: (戦艦級+空母系)≥4 forces B→A and creates G‑bias at E

### Route Strategy Analysis (from Routing Patterns)
The branching creates several viable strategies for HP gauge clearing:

1. **Central Short Route (Recommended for HP Gauge Clearing)**
   - Composition: (駆逐+海防)≥2, balanced fleet with specific carrier/cruiser counts
   - Path: A → E → I → K (boss) – minimal combat nodes
   - Advantages: Shortest route to boss, efficient gauge clearing
   - Risks: Submarine node E with fuel consumption, heavy cruiser node I

2. **Battleship‑Carrier Heavy Route**
   - Composition: (戦艦級+空母系)≥4, may include heavy cruisers
   - Path: B → A → E → G/I? → ... → K – varies based on subsequent conditions
   - Advantages: Powerful combat fleet for node clearing
   - Risks: Potential routing to heavy combat nodes (G, H), resource intensive

3. **Corvette‑Supply Ship Route (Resource Farming)**
   - Composition: 海防艦≥3 OR (海防艦≥2 AND (補給艦+潜水母艦)≥1)
   - Path: A → E → C → J? → ... – accesses fuel and steel nodes
   - Advantages: Resource collection with transport bonuses
   - Risks: Weak combat capability, may face difficult combat nodes

4. **Heavy Cruiser‑Inclusive Route**
   - Composition: Includes 重巡級 ships
   - Path: B → D/F random → ... – variable routing
   - Advantages: Heavy firepower with cruiser support
   - Risks: Unpredictable routing, may face carrier task forces or battleship squadrons

5. **Submarine‑Heavy Route**
   - Composition: 潜水艦≥4? ships
   - Path: E → G → I → ... – forced to battleship squadron
   - Advantages: Potential routing advantages
   - Risks: Submarines vulnerable to ASW nodes, limited surface combat ability

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. HP Gauge Mechanic Introduction
- **Progressive Clearance**: Boss gauge reduces 25% per boss flagship sink (4 sinks required for clear)
- **Historical Significance**: First appearance of HP gauge in normal maps (excluding event maps)
- **Mechanical Teaching**: Introduces multi‑sortie progression requirement for map clearance
- **Player Planning**: Encourages fleet composition optimization for repeated boss engagements
- **Resource Management**: Players must budget resources for 4+ successful boss sorties

### 2. Complex Composition‑Based Routing
- **Multi‑Layer Conditions**: Routing depends on exact ship class counts and combinations
- **Destroyer/Corvette Focus**: (駆逐+海防)≥2 required for central short route
- **Battleship/Carrier Thresholds**: (戦艦級+空母系)≥4 creates distinct routing patterns
- **Heavy Cruiser Penalty**: 重巡級 inclusion forces random routing at B→D/F
- **Corvette‑Specific Routing**: 海防艦≥3 or 海防艦≥2 with supply ship forces resource node access
- **Exact Count Requirements**: 正規空母=2 exactly or 重巡級=2 exactly with destroyers forces boss routing

### 3. Armored Carrier Princess Introduction
- **Visual vs. Actual**: MAP effect always shows 装甲空母姫 but actual boss may be light carrier flagship
- **Mechanical Variety**: Two possible boss types with different air power and combat characteristics
- **Player Adaptation**: Requires flexibility to handle both armored carrier and light carrier bosses
- **Air Power Escalation**: Armored Carrier Princess provides significantly higher air power (48/72/144 vs 23/35/69)

### 4. Resource Node Integration with Routing
- **Fuel Node (C)**: Accessible via corvette‑heavy routing (海防艦≥3 or 海防艦≥2 with supply ship)
- **Steel Node (J)**: Accessible via random routing from I (C:I:J = 25%?:50%?:25%?)
- **Transport Bonuses**: Drum cans and Daihatsu landing craft increase resource yields
- **Strategic Trade‑off**: Resource collection requires specific fleet compositions that may reduce combat effectiveness

### 5. Anti‑Submarine Node with Resource Cost
- **Fixed Fuel Consumption**: Node E consumes 8% fuel regardless of combat outcome
- **Visual Effects**: Submarine node includes visual effects for immersion
- **No Ammunition Cost**: Unique mechanic where only fuel is consumed
- **ASW Emphasis**: Encourages efficient anti‑submarine warfare to minimize repair costs

### 6. Whirlpool with Radar Mitigation
- **Fuel Loss Mechanic**: Node D causes fuel loss with radar‑based reduction
- **Equipment Interaction**: Encourages radar equipment for resource conservation
- **Route Association**: Appears on routes with heavy cruiser inclusion (penalty for heavy cruiser use)

### 7. Difficulty Progression in Western Waters
- **8‑Star Difficulty**: Highest difficulty in World 4 so far (4‑1: 5 stars, 4‑2: 6 stars, 4‑3: 7 stars)
- **Enemy Escalation**: Features flagship battleship pairs, possible Armored Carrier Princess, and complex compositions
- **Routing Complexity**: Most intricate branching in World 4 with multiple probability biases
- **Multi‑Sortie Requirement**: HP gauge necessitates 4+ successful boss engagements
