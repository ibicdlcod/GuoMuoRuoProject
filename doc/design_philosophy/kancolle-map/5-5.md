# Kantai Collection Map 5-5 Design Philosophy
**Second Battle of the Salmon Sea** (第二次サーモン海戦)

## Map Overview
- **Purpose**: Monthly Extra Operation (EO) with highest difficulty (☆×12), tests end‑game fleet building and strategic routing
- **Difficulty**: Maximum (12 stars), considered the "most brutal" normal map
- **Unlock Requirements**: Clear World 5‑4 (Tokyo Express)
- **HP Gauge**: 20% reduction per boss flagship sink (5 sinks required for clear)
- **Time Limits**: Bonus战果 (Special Results) until 21:59 last day of month; 勲章 (Medal) until 23:59
- **Clear Rewards**: Medal ×1, Special Results +200
- **Historical Context**: Based on the Second Battle of the Solomon Sea (Eastern Solomons), a carrier‑focused engagement

## Node Layout
```
Start → A/B → C → D/E/F/G/H/I/J/K/L/M/N/O/P/Q/R → S (Boss)
```
**Node Types**:
- **A**: No combat (empty)
- **B**: Submarine battle (ASW required, fuel 5‑10% consumption)
- **C**: Light surface combat (destroyer/light cruiser enemies)
- **D**: Whirlpool (fuel loss, reduced by radar count)
- **E**: Resource node (Fuel +40, bonus for drum cans & landing craft)
- **F**: Active branching (player choice)
- **G**: Night battle (heavy cruiser flagship + escorts)
- **H**: Carrier battle (enemy carrier task force C group)
- **I**: No combat
- **J**: Carrier battle (enemy carrier task force B group)
- **K**: Carrier battle (enemy carrier task force A group)
- **L**: Strong whirlpool (heavy fuel loss, two loss patterns)
- **M**: Night battle (new‑model battleship squadron)
- **N**: Heavy torpedo squadron (雷巡チ級 flagship ×2‑3)
- **O**: No combat
- **P**: Surface strike force (戦艦レ級 elite + battleship flagship)
- **Q**: No combat
- **R**: No combat
- **S**: Boss – Southern War Princess (南方棲戦姫) with varying escort compositions

## Routing Rules
**Key Branching Conditions** (simplified):

### Start → A/B
- A if: ≥4 destroyers OR ≥4 drum‑can carriers OR ≥4 landing‑craft carriers
- B otherwise

### B → F/K
- K if: ≥3 standard carriers OR ≥4 (battleship‑class + torpedo cruisers) OR ≥3 torpedo cruisers OR ≤1 destroyer
- F otherwise

### F → G/H
- H if: all ships fastest speed OR fast+ unified with ≥2 destroyers
- G otherwise

### G → ?
(From map layout, G leads to further nodes; specific rules not fully detailed in extracted data)

### D (Active Branching)
Player chooses between J and H.

### L → N/P
- N if: all fastest speed
- P if: ≥4 (battleship‑class + carrier‑class)
- L if: ≤1 destroyer
- Otherwise N

### I → L/M
- L if: exactly 3 (battleship‑class + carrier‑class) AND ≥2 destroyers
- M otherwise

### M → L/N
- N if already visited N
- L if: ≥4 (battleship‑class + carrier‑class) OR ≤1 destroyer
- O otherwise

### O → M/O
- O if: already visited M OR fast+ unified OR contains supply ship
- M if: ≥1 standard carrier OR ≥3 (battleship‑class + light carrier) OR ≤1 destroyer
- O otherwise

### R → S
- S if: fast+ unified AND LoS ≥66 (HQ 120)
- Random if: LoS 63‑66 OR contains submarine
- R if: LoS <63

### Q → S
- S if: all fastest speed OR (fast unified AND ≤5 (battleship‑class + carrier‑class) AND no submarine)
- Random if: LoS 73‑80 OR contains submarine OR ≥5 (battleship‑class + carrier‑class)
- Q if: LoS <73

**LoS Coefficients**: Branching points R and Q use coefficient 2.

**Speed Terminology**:
- 最速統一: All ships fastest speed (≥10 knots faster than slowest)
- 高速+統一: Fast+ unified (all ships fast or faster)
- 高速+以上統一: Fast+ or faster unified

## Enemy Analysis
### Submarine Node (B)
- Patterns: 潜水ヨ級elite/flagship + 潜水カ級elite
- EXP: 200‑250
- Requires ASW gear; fuel consumption 5‑10%

### Surface Combat Nodes (C, G, M, N)
- **C**: Light cruiser/destroyer groups (EXP 130‑170)
- **G**: Night‑battle node with 重巡リ級flagship + escorts (EXP 180‑200)
- **M**: Night‑battle node with 戦艦タ級flagship ×2 (EXP 450‑480)
- **N**: Heavy torpedo squadron with 雷巡チ級flagship ×2‑3 (EXP 280‑350)

### Carrier Nodes (H, J, K)
- **H**: Enemy carrier task force C group – 軽母ヌ級flagship ×2 or 空母ヲ級改flagship (EXP 380‑420, air power 46‑204)
- **J**: Enemy carrier task force B group – 空母ヲ級改flagship ×1‑2 (EXP 400‑450, air power 125‑204)
- **K**: Enemy carrier task force A group – 空母ヲ級改flagship ×1‑2 + 重巡ネ級elite (EXP 400‑450, air power 125‑227)

### Surface Strike Node (P)
- 戦艦レ級elite + 戦艦ル級flagship ×2 (EXP 500‑550, air power 94‑107)
- Multiple patterns with varying escort compositions

### Boss Node (S)
**Pre‑clear Patterns** (55% chance of hardest patterns):
- **Pattern 1** (15%): 空母ヲ級改flagship + 南方棲戦姫 + 重巡ネ級elite + destroyers + submarine (air power 149)
- **Pattern 2** (30%): 空母ヲ級改flagship ×2 + 南方棲戦姫 + destroyers + submarine (air power 251)
- **Pattern 3** (15%): 南方棲戦姫 + 戦艦レ級elite ×2 + light cruiser + destroyers (air power **261**, **most dangerous**)
- **Pattern 4** (40%): 南方棲戦姫 + 戦艦レ級elite ×2 + destroyers + submarine (air power **261**)

**Post‑clear Patterns** (fixed, slightly easier):
- **Pattern 5**: 空母ヲ級改flagship + 空母ヲ級flagship + 戦艦レ級elite + destroyers + submarine elite (air power 237)
- **Pattern 6**: 戦艦レ級elite + 戦艦タ級flagship + 空母ヲ級flagship + destroyers + submarine elite (air power 191)

**Key Threats**:
- Southern War Princess participates in air, torpedo, and shelling phases (post‑2018 buff)
- Dual 戦艦レ級elite (patterns 3/4) have extremely high firepower and durability
- Submarine escorts complicate targeting

## Resource Nodes
- **E**: Fuel +40 (base); +15 per drum can, +10 per landing craft (max 180)
- **D, L**: Whirlpools with fuel loss (32‑150% base, reduced by radar count)

## Design Patterns Observed
### 1. **Ultimate Fleet‑Building Test**
- Requires balancing multiple constraints: ship‑type counts, speed, LoS, drum/landing‑craft carriers
- No single "perfect" fleet – players must choose between carrier‑heavy, battleship‑heavy, or balanced compositions
- Active branching at D allows strategic route selection based on fleet strengths

### 2. **Layered Difficulty Progression**
- **Early nodes**: Submarine and light surface combat (resource management)
- **Mid nodes**: Carrier battles (air superiority challenge)  
- **Late nodes**: Night battles + heavy surface strike (firepower test)
- **Boss**: Combination of all enemy types (carrier, battleship, submarine, special princess)

### 3. **Pre‑ vs Post‑Clear Differentiation**
- Boss patterns change dramatically after gauge destruction (easier for farming)
- Encourages players to clear with optimal fleet first, then farm with more flexible compositions
- Time‑limited rewards create monthly engagement cycle

### 4. **Historical Fleet Composition Lessons**
- Reflects actual carrier‑focused Solomon Sea battles
- Carrier‑heavy routes (H, J, K) simulate carrier task force engagements
- Surface‑heavy routes (P, M) simulate night surface actions

### 5. **Equipment Specialization**
- Drum cans and landing craft provide resource bonuses at E
- Radar reduces whirlpool losses (encourages equipment diversity)
- ASW gear required for submarine node
- Night battle equipment essential for G and M

### 6. **Speed‑Based Routing**
- Multiple branches require speed unification (最速統一 or 高速+統一)
- Forces choice between powerful slow ships (Yamato‑class) and faster but weaker alternatives

### 7. **LoS‑Gated Final Approach**
- Final branches (R→S, Q→S) require high LoS (63‑80 depending on route)
- Coefficient 2 makes LoS requirements stricter than normal maps
- Submarines in fleet can cause random routing even with sufficient LoS

## Implementation Notes for FleetMemories
### Map Structure
- Implement as extra‑operation‑style map with monthly reset
- Use HP gauge (20% per sink, 5 sinks total)
- Add time‑limited rewards (medal + special results)

### Routing Logic
- Implement all branching conditions with strict ship‑type counting
- Include active branching at node D (player choice)
- Add speed‑unification checks with proper classification (fastest/fast+/etc.)
- Implement LoS calculations with coefficient 2 for final branches

### Enemy Design
- **Boss variations**: Implement all 6 patterns with proper difficulty scaling
- **Southern War Princess**: Special enemy with multi‑phase attacks (air, torpedo, shelling)
- **Dual 戦艦レ級elite**: High‑threat combination requiring focused firepower
- **Submarine escorts**: Force players to bring ASW or accept random targeting

### Resource Management
- Whirlpool fuel loss scaled by radar count (implement reduction formula)
- Resource node bonuses for transport equipment (drum cans, landing craft)

### Difficulty Tuning
- This is the hardest normal map – balance accordingly
- Pre‑clear patterns should be extremely challenging for end‑game players
- Post‑clear patterns should be farmable but still require competent fleets

### Player Guidance
- Provide clear feedback on routing conditions (why fleet went to specific node)
- Indicate LoS requirements before sortie
- Warn about monthly time limits for bonus rewards
