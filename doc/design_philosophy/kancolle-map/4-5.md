# Kantai Collection Map 4-5 Design Philosophy
## 深海東洋艦隊漸減作戦 (Deep Sea Eastern Fleet Attrition Operation)

### Map Overview
**Purpose:** High‑difficulty monthly map for experienced admirals, introducing attrition‑style HP gauge mechanics with strategic routing choices.

**Difficulty:** ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (9 stars) – one of the hardest normal maps in World 4.

**Unlock Requirements:**
- Clear map 4‑4 (カスガダマ沖海戦)
- Clear map 5‑1 (南方海域前面)
- Teaches cross‑world progression: players must advance in both Western and Southern waters.

**HP Gauge Mechanics:**
- Boss flagship sink reduces gauge by **20%** (damage‑only victories do not count)
- **5 sinks required** to clear the map (100% ÷ 20% = 5)
- Gauge resets monthly; failure to clear within the month forfeits monthly bonus rewards
- **Bonus rewards:** Medal ×1, Special Combat Results +180
- **Time limits:** Bonus rewards available until month‑end 21:59; Medal until 23:59

**Boss Fleet After Gauge Destruction:**
- Post‑clear boss fleets are random among patterns 1‑3 (no final forms)
- Allows farming for rare drops without extreme difficulty

### Node Layout
Map features **16 combat nodes** (A–T) plus start point, with intricate branching.

**Key Node Types:**
- **Start → A** (random: C or A)
- **A → B/D** (active branching)
- **B, D, F, O** – **Anti‑Submarine Warfare (ASW)** nodes with submarine fleets
- **C, I** – Active branching (player choice)
- **E, G, H, J, K, S** – Surface combat nodes (enemy surface fleets)
- **K** – Carrier‑based enemy fleet (深海東洋艦隊 機動部隊)
- **L, M, P, Q** – No combat ("enemy not sighted")
- **N** – Transport fleet node (東洋方面艦隊 後方兵站部隊) – weak but high EXP
- **R** – **Whirlpool** (resource drain)
- **T** – **Boss node** (リランカ島港湾守備隊) featuring Harbor Princess

**Node Connectivity (Simplified):**
```
Start → A → (B/D) → ...
Complex branching based on fleet composition, speed, LOS, etc.
```

### Routing Rules (ルート分岐法則)
**Updated:** 2020‑03‑18

**Key Branching Conditions:**

1. **Start → A:** Random (no bias)

2. **A → B/D:** Active branching (player choice)

3. **D → H:** Fixed

4. **E → K/M:**
   - **M if:** High‑speed+ unified **OR** contains supply ship **OR** (battleship‑class + carrier‑type) ≥3
   - **K if:** Light cruiser ≥1 **AND** (destroyer + coastal defense) ≥2
   - **Otherwise K**

5. **G → D/H:**
   - **H? if:** Light cruiser ≥1 **AND** (destroyer + coastal defense) ≥2
   - **Otherwise random?**

6. **H → K/T:**
   - **T if:** High‑speed+ unified **AND** (battleship‑class + carrier‑type) ≤4
   - **T if:** Light cruiser =1 **AND** (destroyer + coastal defense) ≥3
   - **T if:** Node D **not visited** **AND** Light cruiser =1 **AND** (destroyer + coastal defense) ≥2
   - **Otherwise K**

7. **K → L/M/T:**
   - **M if:** (battleship‑class + regular carrier) ≥4 **OR** (battleship‑class + carrier‑type) ≥5 **OR** supply ship ≥1
   - **L if:** LOS score <63? (HQ level 120 reference)
   - **Random between L/T if:** LOS 63?‑70? **AND** contains submarine
   - **Random between M/T if:** LOS 63?‑70? **AND** no submarine
   - **T if:** LOS score ≥70?

8. **N → R/N:**
   - **N if:** Fastest unified **OR** high‑speed+ unified **OR** (battleship‑class + regular carrier) ≤1
   - **R if:** Destroyer ≤1 **OR** contains slow ship
   - **Otherwise R**

9. **N → T/O:**
   - **O if:** (battleship‑class + carrier‑type) ≥5 **OR** destroyer =0
   - **P if:** LOS score <55?
   - **Random O/P if:** LOS 55?‑59? **AND** (battleship‑class + carrier‑type) ≥5 **OR** destroyer =0
   - **Random N/P if:** LOS 55?‑59? otherwise
   - **N if:** LOS score ≥59?

10. **N → S/N:**
    - **N if:** High‑speed+ unified **OR** (high‑speed+ unified **AND** (light cruiser + aviation cruiser) ≥1 **AND** destroyer ≥2)
    - **S otherwise**

**LOS Notes:**
- Map branching coefficient: **2**
- LOS scores referenced for HQ level 120; lower HQ levels need +1 margin
- LOS calculation uses standard Kantai Collection formula

**Ship‑Class Definitions:**
- **Battleship‑class:** Includes fast battleships, slow battleships, aviation battleships
- **Carrier‑type:** Regular carriers, armored carriers, light carriers
- **Light cruiser‑class:** Light cruisers, training cruisers, torpedo cruisers
- **Destroyer + coastal defense:** Counted together for some conditions

### Resource Nodes
**R – Whirlpool (うずしお):**
- **Fuel drain:** Base loss 35%, after second battle 25.2% (max loss 100%)
- **Radar reduction:** Each radar reduces loss by multiplicative factor:
  - 0 radar: -25.2% (actual loss 25.2%)
  - 1 radar: -18.9%
  - 2 radar: -15.12%
  - 3 radar: -12.6%
  - 4 radar: -11.34%
  - 5 radar: -10.58%
  - 6 radar: -10.08%
- **Design intent:** Penalize radar‑poor fleets, encourage equipment optimization

**No resource‑collection nodes** (fuel/ammo/bauxite/steel) unlike earlier Western Waters maps.

### Design Patterns Observed

#### 1. **Attrition‑Style Monthly Map**
- 5‑kill requirement spreads effort across multiple sorties
- Encourages strategic planning: balance fleet strength vs. resource consumption
- Monthly reset teaches consistent engagement over time

#### 2. **Cross‑World Progression Gate**
- Requires 5‑1 clearance (Southern Waters)
- Teaches players to advance in multiple directions simultaneously
- Prevents "tunnel vision" on single world

#### 3. **Complex Multi‑Factor Routing**
- Combines ship composition, speed, LOS, and path history
- **Example:** Node H → T requires specific cruiser/destroyer counts **AND** speed **AND** battleship/carrier limits
- Encourages fleet‑building creativity rather than brute force

#### 4. **Submarine‑Heavy Early Route**
- 4 dedicated ASW nodes (B, D, F, O)
- Forces ASW‑capable fleet composition
- Tests player's anti‑submarine preparedness

#### 5. **Carrier‑Focused Mid‑Route (Node K)**
- Features軽巡棲鬼 (Light Cruiser Demon) – durable enemy with high AA
- 空母ヲ級flagship with white aircraft (enhanced planes)
- Extreme air power (up to 504) requires strong fighter coverage
- Teaches carrier‑vs‑carrier combat

#### 6. **Transport Node as "Breathing Room"**
- Node N provides high EXP with minimal threat
- Allows leveling ships while progressing monthly gauge
- Strategic choice: farm EXP or rush boss?

#### 7. **Boss with Escort Variety**
- 港湾棲姫 (Harbor Princess) – land‑based installation
- 護衛要塞 (Escort Fortress) – damage‑soaking auxiliaries
- Multiple escort types (battleships, heavy cruisers, transports)
- Final forms shift composition toward surface combat

#### 8. **Post‑Clear Farming Optimization**
- Random patterns 1‑3 after gauge destruction
- Reduced difficulty enables efficient farming for rare drops
- Rewards players who complete monthly clear

### Implementation Notes for FleetMemories

#### **Routing System**
- Implement all branching conditions with **weighted multi‑factor checks**
- Track **path history** (e.g., "D not visited" condition)
- Support **active branching** where player chooses direction
- Calculate **LOS scores** with map‑specific coefficient (2)

#### **HP Gauge Mechanics**
- **Monthly‑reset HP gauge** with 5‑kill requirement
- **Boss flagship sink only** – damage‑only victories don't count
- **Post‑clear pattern randomization** (patterns 1‑3, no final forms)
- **Time‑limited rewards** (bonus until 21:59, medal until 23:59)

#### **Enemy Fleet Design**
- **Submarine nodes:** Use梯形/単横 formations, require ASW equipment
- **Carrier node K:** Implement軽巡棲鬼 with high durability and AA
- **Boss node:** 港湾棲姫 as land‑based installation with escort variety
- **Final forms:** Reduced air power but stronger surface composition

#### **Resource Drain**
- **Whirlpool node R:** Implement fuel drain with radar mitigation
- Formula: `loss = base_loss × (1 - radar_reduction)^radar_count`
- Maximum loss cap at 100%

#### **Player Guidance**
- Clearly communicate **cross‑world requirements** (4‑4 + 5‑1)
- Explain **monthly reset mechanics** and time limits
- Provide **routing calculators** for complex branching
- Highlight **ASW requirements** for early route

#### **Difficulty Balancing**
- Maintain **9‑star difficulty** through complex routing and strong enemies
- Ensure **final forms** are challenging but not impossible
- Allow **post‑clear farming** with reduced difficulty
- Balance **resource costs** against monthly reward value

### Conclusion
Map 4‑5 represents the pinnacle of Western Waters design: a monthly attrition map that tests fleet‑building versatility, strategic planning, and combat proficiency across multiple enemy types. Its cross‑world unlock requirement encourages broader progression, while complex routing forces players to think beyond simple composition checks. The map successfully integrates submarine warfare, carrier combat, surface engagements, and installation‑type bosses into a cohesive challenge that rewards consistent monthly engagement.

For FleetMemories, this map provides a template for high‑difficulty monthly content with intricate branching, multi‑phase boss design, and meaningful progression gates.
