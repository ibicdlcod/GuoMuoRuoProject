# Kantai Collection Map 3-3 Design Philosophy

## Map Overview
**World 3‑3: アルフォンシーノ方面進出 (Alfonsino Area Advance)** – A 7‑star difficulty map that introduces mandatory whirlpool mechanics, carrier‑focused fleet requirements, and distinct north/south route choices with different risk‑reward tradeoffs.

### Purpose
- **Carrier‑Fleet Emphasis**: Encourages use of carrier‑heavy fleets (2+ carriers) while still allowing non‑carrier strategies
- **Whirlpool Management**: First normal map where a whirlpool node is **mandatory** on all boss routes, teaching radar‑based fuel‑loss reduction
- **Route‑Choice Depth**: Offers separate north (short, carrier‑friendly) and south (longer, resource‑gathering) paths with different enemy compositions
- **Furniture‑Box Farming**: Provides medium and large furniture boxes as side rewards, encouraging diversion routes
- **Northern Waters Progression**: Third map of World 3, raising difficulty with stronger enemy air groups and battleship/carrier combinations

### Difficulty
- **Rank**: ☆☆☆☆☆☆☆ (7 stars)
- **Recommended Level**: Level 60‑80 ships (carriers, battleships, heavy cruisers)
- **Resource Cost**: High fuel/ammo consumption due to multiple combat nodes and whirlpool penalties
- **Repair Cost**: Significant risk of damage at nodes G (carrier task force) and M (boss)
- **EXP Range**: 150‑390 (boss node 350‑390)
- **HQ Level Scaling**: Not explicitly mentioned

### Unlock Requirements
- **Prerequisite**: Clear both 2‑4 ("沖ノ島海域") and 3‑2 ("キス島撤退作戦")
- **Note**: Rare reports of the map not appearing even with both cleared; exact conditions unclear

## Node Layout

### Visual Structure
```
North Route (carrier‑focused):
Start → A → C (whirlpool) → G → M (boss)
   (variants: C→E→G→M, C→G→H/J→M)

South Route (non‑carrier):
Start → A → B → F (strong whirlpool) → J → M (boss)
   (variants: B→D, F→K→M, etc.)
```

### Node Details

#### Resource Nodes
1. **Node D: アイテム獲得 (Item Acquisition)**
   - **Type**: Furniture box node
   - **Yield**: 家具箱（中） (medium furniture box)
   - **Strategic Value**: Accessible via B→D diversion; provides coin income

2. **Node H: アイテム獲得 (Item Acquisition)**
   - **Type**: Furniture box node
   - **Yield**: 家具箱（大） (large furniture box)
   - **Strategic Value**: Side reward on north‑route diversions

#### Whirlpool Nodes
1. **Node C: うずしお (Whirlpool)**
   - **Fuel Loss**: Base 30%, actual loss 24% (maximum loss 40%)
   - **Radar Reduction**: Each radar reduces loss (0 radars: ‑24%, 1 radar: ‑18%, 2 radars: ‑14.4%, etc.)
   - **Strategic Impact**: **Mandatory** on all north‑route boss paths; forces radar consideration

2. **Node F: 強うずしお (Strong Whirlpool)**
   - **Fuel Loss**: Two patterns – 40% base (actual 24%) or **150%** base (actual 90%)
   - **Radar Reduction**: Same formulas as node C but applied to higher base values
   - **Strategic Impact**: Can drain fuel to near‑zero if strong whirlpool triggers with few radars; major risk on south route

#### Non‑Combat Nodes
- **Node I**: “敵影を見ず。” – no combat (fog)
- **Node J**: “敵影を見ず。” – no combat (fog)
- **Node L**: “敵影を見ず。” – no combat (fog)

#### Combat Nodes (see Enemy Analysis for details)
- **Node A**: 敵前衛艦隊 – light/heavy cruiser vanguard
- **Node B**: 敵北方巡洋艦戦隊 B群 – heavy cruiser‑led squadron
- **Node E**: 敵北方護衛空母群 – light carrier escort group
- **Node G**: 敵北方機動部隊 – carrier‑battleship task force (dangerous)
- **Node K**: 敵北方巡洋艦戦隊 A群 – heavy cruiser/torpedo cruiser group
- **Node M (BOSS)**: 敵深海北方泊地艦隊 – battleship‑carrier anchored fleet

### Route Strategies (from Player Guides)
1. **North Short Route (ACGM) – Carrier Recommended**:
   - **Conditions**: Exactly 1 standard carrier, (戦艦級+空母系) =2, ≥2 destroyers, no submarines.
   - **Combat Nodes**: A (light), C (whirlpool), G (carrier task force), M (boss).
   - **Advantages**: Short (3 combat nodes), predictable, avoids strong whirlpool.
   - **Disadvantages**: Requires carrier and precise composition; node G has dangerous carrier‑battleship enemy.

2. **North Long Route (ACEGM) – Heavy Fleet**:
   - **Conditions**: (戦艦級+空母系) =3.
   - **Combat Nodes**: A, C (whirlpool), E (light carrier group), G, M.
   - **Advantages**: Easier air superiority, more firepower.
   - **Disadvantages**: Extra combat node (E), higher resource consumption.

3. **South Route (ABFJM) – Non‑Carrier**:
   - **Conditions**: No standard carriers; light carriers allowed.
   - **Combat Nodes**: A, B, F (strong whirlpool), J (fog), M.
   - **Advantages**: No carrier requirement; can farm furniture boxes at D/H.
   - **Disadvantages**: Risk of strong whirlpool (150% fuel loss); longer route; boss air superiority harder to achieve.

4. **Furniture‑Box Farming Routes**:
   - **Medium Box (D)**: Reach node B with submarine or specific composition.
   - **Large Box (H)**: Reach node G with submarine or light cruiser/aviation cruiser/seaplane tender.
   - **Double‑Box (D+H)**: Specialized compositions with 2+ destroyers, limited battleships/light carriers, and submarines.

## Enemy Analysis

### Node A: 敵前衛艦隊 (Enemy Vanguard Fleet)
- **EXP**: 150‑170
- **Patterns**: 3 variations, escalating with more heavy cruisers
- **Typical Composition**: 1 light cruiser flagship, 1‑2 heavy cruisers, 1‑2 light cruisers, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦), Trapezoid (梯形)
- **Air Power**: None

### Node B: 敵北方巡洋艦戦隊 B群 (Enemy Northern Cruiser Squadron Group B)
- **EXP**: 180‑210
- **Patterns**: 4 variations, heavy‑cruiser‑centric
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 heavy cruiser flagships, 1‑2 light cruisers, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦)

### Node E: 敵北方護衛空母群 (Enemy Northern Escort Carrier Group)
- **EXP**: 200‑230
- **Patterns**: 4 variations, light‑carrier‑centric
- **Typical Composition**: 2‑3 light carrier Nu‑class (flagship/elite), 1‑2 heavy cruisers, 2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Diamond (輪形)
- **Air Power**: 70‑72 (enemy) / 105‑216 (required for air superiority)

### Node G: 敵北方機動部隊 (Enemy Northern Mobile Force)
- **EXP**: 290‑330
- **Patterns**: 5 variations, includes 空母ヲ級flagship and 戦艦ル級flagship/elite
- **Typical Composition**: 1 standard carrier Wo‑class flagship, 1 light carrier Nu‑class, 1‑2 battleships Ru‑class, 1 light cruiser, 2 destroyers
- **Formation**: Diamond (輪形)
- **Air Power**: 51‑52 (enemy) / 77‑156 (required for air superiority)
- **Threat Level**: High – combined carrier‑battleship force can cripple unprepared fleets

### Node K: 敵北方巡洋艦戦隊 A群 (Enemy Northern Cruiser Squadron Group A)
- **EXP**: 250‑290
- **Patterns**: 5 variations, includes torpedo cruiser Chi‑class flagship
- **Typical Composition**: 2 heavy cruiser flagships, 1 torpedo cruiser flagship, 1 light cruiser, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦)

### Node M (BOSS): 敵深海北方泊地艦隊 (Enemy Deep‑Sea Northern Anchored Fleet)
- **EXP**: 350‑390
- **Patterns**: 5 variations, battleship‑carrier anchored group
- **Typical Composition**: 1‑2 battleship Ru‑class flagships, 1 light carrier Nu‑class (flagship/elite), 1 light cruiser flagship, 2‑3 destroyers
- **Formation**: Single line (単縦)
- **Air Power**: 23‑24 (enemy) / 35‑72 (required for air superiority)
- **Objective**: Sink battleship(s) and carrier for higher rank; flagship is battleship Ru‑class

## Design Patterns Observed

### 1. Mandatory Whirlpool as Gatekeeper
- **First Normal Map** with unavoidable whirlpool; forces players to engage with radar mechanics.
- **Risk‑Reward**: North route (C) has predictable loss; south route (F) risks catastrophic 150% loss.
- **Equipment Planning**: Radar不再是 optional; optimal play requires 3‑6 radars to minimize penalties.

### 2. Carrier vs. Non‑Carrier Route Split
- **North Route**: Carrier‑friendly, shorter, more predictable – rewards players who have invested in carriers.
- **South Route**: Allows non‑carrier fleets, offers furniture‑box farming – provides alternative for players lacking strong carriers.
- **Strategic Choice**: Players can choose based on their fleet strengths and current goals (clearing vs. farming).

### 3. Furniture‑Box Farming Integration
- **Dedicated Nodes**: D (medium) and H (large) provide tangible coin income.
- **Specialized Compositions**: Specific fleet builds (e.g., submarine‑containing) can increase box‑drop rates.
- **Recurrent Value**: Makes the map economically relevant beyond initial clearance.

### 4. Air Power Escalation
- **Node E**: Introduces light‑carrier groups with moderate air power (70‑72).
- **Node G**: Features standard carrier + battleship combination with higher air power (51‑52).
- **Boss Node**: Moderate air power (23‑24) but combined with battleship firepower.
- **Progression**: Teaches players to maintain air superiority across multiple node types.

### 5. Composition Precision
- **Exact Counts Matter**: Many branching conditions require exact ship counts (e.g., “軽巡 =1”, “駆逐 =4”).
- **Educational Intent**: Reinforces careful fleet building and understanding of ship‑class categories.
- **Mission Adaptation**: Seasonal missions often require specific compositions that interact with these precise rules.

### 6. Mission Integration
- Numerous one‑time and seasonal missions require 3‑3 boss clears, including:
  - **冬季北方海域作戦** (Winter Northern Waters Operation) – requires S‑rank at 3‑1, 3‑3, 3‑4, 3‑5.
  - **AL作戦** (AL Operation) – yearly mission requiring S‑rank at 3‑1, 3‑3, 3‑4, 3‑5.
  - **日英米合同水上艦隊、抜錨せよ！** (Joint Japanese‑British‑American Surface Fleet, Sortie!) – requires non‑carrier fleets with specific nationality mixes.

## Implementation Considerations for FleetMemories

### Core Mechanics to Replicate
1. **Mandatory Whirlpool**: At least one whirlpool node must be traversed to reach boss; radar reduces loss.
2. **Carrier/Non‑Carrier Route Split**: Distinct north (carrier‑friendly) and south (non‑carrier) routes with different enemy types.
3. **Furniture‑Box Farming**: Side nodes providing currency/items accessible via specific compositions.
4. **Precise Composition Rules**: Exact ship counts matter for routing (e.g., “1 light cruiser, 4 destroyers”).
5. **Air Power Progression**: Enemy air power increases through route, requiring players to maintain air superiority.

### Adaptation Suggestions
1. **Simplify Whirlpool Mechanics**: Keep mandatory whirlpool but reduce penalty severity; maintain radar reduction.
2. **Adjust Route Requirements**: Make carrier‑route accessible with 1+ carriers (not exactly 1); relax exact count requirements where possible.
3. **Modify Furniture‑Box Rewards**: Convert to FleetMemories’ currency or item system; keep farming viable but not excessive.
4. **Preserve Strategic Choice**: Ensure both carrier and non‑carrier routes remain viable for different player resources.
5. **Maintain Air Power Checks**: Ensure enemy air power values encourage bringing aircraft but not mandating maxed‑out carriers.

### Balance Considerations
- **Difficulty Curve**: Position as moderate step up from 3‑2, introducing whirlpool mechanics while still allowing non‑carrier strategies.
- **Resource Economy**: Whirlpool penalties should be meaningful but not crippling; radar reduction should feel impactful.
- **Enemy Strength**: Node G (carrier‑battleship) should be threatening but beatable with appropriate preparation.
- **Route Viability**: Both north and south routes should offer reasonable clear chances for their intended fleet types.

### Learning Objectives to Preserve
1. **Whirlpool Management**: Players must consider fuel reserves and radar equipment.
2. **Carrier Utility**: Carriers provide route advantages and air superiority.
3. **Composition Precision**: Exact ship counts influence routing – encourages careful fleet building.
4. **Risk‑Reward Tradeoffs**: South route offers farming rewards but higher whirlpool risk.
5. **Air Superiority**: Maintaining air superiority across multiple node types becomes more important.
