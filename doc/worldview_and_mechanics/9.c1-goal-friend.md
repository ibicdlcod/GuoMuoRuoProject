# Tactical goal (for friend fleet)

Also called "Friend Fleet Priority", replaces Kantai Collection's formation chosen, this attribute affect your battle primarily through your own side's behaviour.

Beware your ships will lose the goal's effects and degenerate into Kantai Collection's random behaviour when the communication efficiency between the your flagship and your ship in question is low! (This also applies to goal against enemy fleet)
