# Gunshots

Workflow: when an gunshot is allowed to be made (any friend or enemy ship can independently make the process):

Reloading (see below)

[Target selection](9-battle.md) (Note: each squadron select target individually and sequentially, even in central phase)

Actual attack and damage (see below)

Any gunshot would force recalculation of the ship's concealment status (canceling existing concealment decider events scheduled for later time[Implmentation note: this logic is in Battle::forceVisible]) with the ship's concealment attribute temporarily reduced to 50%.

## Main gun shots

A ship can fire main gun shots if it equips main guns or its 1st Default Equip is a main gun. However, if it does not equip main guns, then reloading speed have a penalty of 0.5x.

### Reloading

Does not attack in air battle.

In approaching phase main gun will shoot $x$ seconds earlier than the end of approaching phase (that is clocktime = $20-x$)

Where

$$
x=min(10 \cdot \max_i r_i \cdot \frac{\sqrt{\text{los}_f^2+\text{los}_e^2}}{\text{los}_e\cdot \text{speed}_e}, 20)
$$

Where $r_i$ is firing range of $i$ th main gun, $\text{los}_f$ is attacker fleet los and $\text{los}_e$ is defender fleet los and $\text{speed}_e$ is the fastest ship speed in defender's fleet.

Then the main gun will reload for each $600z/y$ seconds throughout every phase where $y$ is maximum firing speed of any main gun mounted and $z=\frac{h+1}{2}$ where h is remaining health ratio.

For night-battle-start nodes, the main guns have a **chance** of initially unloaded and must go through the cooldown before first attack. The chance is

$$
p=\frac{\text{los}_e}{\sqrt{\text{los}_e^2+\text{los}_f^2}}
$$

otherwise it is loaded from the very beginning. Of course, these LOS is night mode LOS.

#### Base accuracy of ship

    double baseAcc = 500.0 * ((lv + 25.0) / 100.0)
                     / std::hypot(1.0, (lv + 25.0) / 100.0);

### Damage

#### Evasion chance:

$$
2^{-\frac{b}{c}}
$$

where $b$ is attacking ship's accuracy (taking equipment and visible bounses into account), $c$ is defender's evasion.

#### Damage chance:

processed after evasion decider.

$$
v\sim U(0,1), v<\frac{2\log (10AP/a)}{e\sqrt{1+\log^2 (10AP/a)}}+0.5
$$

 where $AP$ is highest armor penetration of the attacking ship's equip, $a$ is defender's armor, 

#### Overpenetration

processed simultaneously with damage chance

If $v<\frac{\log (10AP/a)}{\sqrt{1+\log^2 (10AP/a)}}-0.5$ then attack overpenetrates (override damage chance) and only deals $\text{HP}/64$ (HP is that of defender ship) damage. 

#### Damage value:

$$
x=\frac{w\max(u,0)z}{\sqrt{w^2u^2+z^2}}
$$

$u\sim N(10\text{DPM}/y, \text{DPM}/50)$ (DPM is that of attacker ship, taking equipment into account) where $y$ is the same as reloading section.

$z=\max(\text{HP}/16, 256)$ (HP is that of defender ship)

$w=1+0.25f$ where $f$ is [formation efficiency](9.c7-formation.md).

## Secondary gun shots

A ship can fire secondary gun shots if it equips secondary guns.

[Implemented in Battle::processSecondaryGunAttack, Battle::setupSecondaryGunshots, Battle::hasSecGun]

### Reloading

Only fires in central phase and night battle.

Then **each** secondary gun will reload for each $600z/y$ seconds throughout every phase where y is maximum firing speed of said secondary gun mounted and $z=\frac{h+1}{2}$ where h is remaining health ratio.

For night-battle-start nodes, the secondary gun is initially unloaded.

[Implemented in Battle::setupSecondaryGunshots]

#### Base accuracy of ship (for secondary gun)

```
double baseAccSec = 2000.0 * ((lv + 25.0) / 100.0)
                 / std::hypot(1.0, (lv + 25.0) / 100.0);
```

[Implemented in Battle::secGunBaseAccuracy]

## Damage

[Implemented in Battle::processSecondaryGunAttack]

#### Evasion chance:

$$
2^{-\frac{b}{c}}
$$

where b is attacking ship's secondary accuracy (base secondary gun accuracy of ship + equipment accuracy of **all secondary guns**), c is defender's evasion.

#### Damage chance:

Same formula as main guns (sigmoid-based on armor penetration vs armor), but no overpenetration case.

[Implemented in Battle::processSecondaryGunAttack#damage-chance]

#### Point-blank shot

processed simultaneously with evasion chance

Have a $e^{(b-4c)/1000}$ chace of point-blank shot which changes antagonist fleet's formation efficiency to $y=0.9375x-0.0625$ where $x$ is the original formation efficiency (this should be logged in battle report)

[Implemented in Battle::processSecondaryGunAttack#point-blank]

#### Damage value:

$$
x=\frac{wuz}{\sqrt{w^2u^2+z^2}}
$$

$u\sim N(10\text{DPM}/y, \text{DPM}/50)$ ($DPM$ is that of attacking secondary gun) where y is the same as reloading section.

$z=\max(\text{HP}/64, 64)$ (HP is that of defender ship)

$w=1+0.25f$ where f is [formation efficiency](9.c7-formation.md).

## Gunshot cut-in

### Plain Gunshot cut-in

| Cut-in type | Equipment 1          | Equipment 2          | Damage multiplier |
|:-----------:|:--------------------:|:--------------------:|:-----------------:|
| Gunshot     | Main gun             | Main gun             | 1.1               |
| Gunshot     | Main gun             | Secondary gun        | 1.05              |
| Gunshot     | Big-caliber main gun | Big-caliber main gun | 1.12              |
| Gunshot     | Big-caliber main gun | AP shell             | 1.17              |

#### Trigger mechanism

Have a chance to replace normal main gun attack (this is different from air attack cut-in). Chance is

$$
\prod_{i=1}^2a_i, a_i=\max(0, (f+1)/2)\cdot \frac{p}{\sqrt{1+p^2}}
$$

where $f$ is formation coefficient, $p$ is triggerer's primary stats divided by 100 (or 250 for big-caliber main gun, 50 for small-caliber main gun / secondary gun, $p=5$ for AP shell regardless of primary stats). Each combination of equips in ship's (equipslot plus defaultequip1) is considered; combinations that have higher damage multiplier takes precedence.

#### Damage and accuracy

Same formula as main guns (sigmoid-based on armor penetration vs armor) but damage is multiplied by damage multiplier in the table, and no overpenetration case.

### Spotting fire cut-in

| Cut-in type             | Equipment 1          | Equipment 2 | Damage multiplier | Accuracy multiplier |
|:-----------------------:|:--------------------:|:-----------:| ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Gunshot (spotting fire) | Main gun             | Recon plane | 1.2               | 1.5                 |
| Gunshot (spotting fire) | Big-caliber main gun | Recon plane | 1.25              | 1.5                 |

#### Trigger mechanism

Have a chance to replace normal main gun attack (if this is successful, then plain gunshot cut-in is not considered). Chance is

$$
p=\max(0, r) \cdot \frac{qs}{\sqrt{1+q^2s^2}}
$$

where $r$ is air superiority coefficient, $q$ is recon plane's primary stats divided by 400. $s$ is remaining recon planes in squadron. Each combination of equips in ship's (all equipslots plus defaultequip1) is considered; combinations that have higher damage multiplier takes precedence.

#### Damage and accuracy

Same formula as main guns (sigmoid-based on armor penetration vs armor) but damage is multiplied by damage multiplier in the table, evasion calculations have attacker accuracy mulitplied by accuracy multiplier in the table, and no overpenetration case.
