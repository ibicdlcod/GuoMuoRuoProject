# Gunshots

Workflow: when an gunshot is allowed to be made (any friend or enemy ship can independently make the process):

Reloading (see below)

[Target selection](9-battle.md) (Note: each squadron select target individually and sequentially, even in central phase)

Actual attack and damage (see below)

[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED] Any gunshot would force recalculation of the ship's concealment status (canceling existing concealment decider events scheduled for later time[Implmentation note: this logic is in Battle::forceVisible]) with the ship's concealment attribute temporarily reduced to 50%.

## Main gun shots

A ship can fire main gun shots if it equips main guns or its 1st Default Equip is a main gun.

### Reloading

Does not attack in air battle.

In approaching phase main gun will shoot $x$ seconds earlier than the end of approaching phase (that is clocktime = $20-x$)

Where

$$
x=min(10 \cdot \max_i r_i \cdot \frac{\sqrt{\text{los}_f^2+\text{los}_e^2}}{\text{los}_e\cdot \text{speed}_e}, 0)
$$

Where $r_i$ is firing range of $i$ th main gun, $\text{los}_f$ is attacker fleet los and $\text{los}_e$ is defender fleet los and $\text{speed}_e$ is the fastest ship speed in defender's fleet.

Then the main gun will reload for each $600z/y$ seconds throughout every phase where $y$ is maximum firing speed of any main gun mounted and $z=\frac{h+1}{2}$ where h is remaining health ratio.

For night-battle-start nodes, the main guns have a **chance** of initially unloaded and must go through the cooldown before first attack. The chance is

$$
p=\frac{\text{los}_e}{\sqrt{\text{los}_e^2+\text{los}_f^2}}
$$

otherwise it is loaded from the very beginning. Of course, these LOS is night mode LOS.

#### Base accuracy of ship

    double baseAcc = 1000.0 * ((lv + 25.0) / 100.0)
                     / std::hypot(1.0, (lv + 25.0) / 100.0);

### Damage

Damage chance:

$$
(\frac{\log (10AP/a)}{2\sqrt{1+\log^2 (10AP/a)}}+\frac{1}{2})e^{(b-c)/1000}
$$

where $AP$ is highest armor penetration of the attacking ship's equip, $a$ is defender's armor, $b$ is attacking ship's accuracy (base accuracy of ship + equipment accuracy), $c$ is defender's evasion.

Damage value: $x\sim N(10\text{DPM}/y, DPM/50)$

## Secondary gun shots

A ship can fire secondary gun shots if it equips secondary guns.
