### Highest speed factor

$b=log(x/y)$ where $x$ is friend fleet's speed (in knots) and $y$ is enemy fleet's.

### Consistent speed factor

$c=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}-|\log(x_i/v)|}{n}$ where $x_i$ is individual ship's speed and $v$ is the fleet's average speed. Note this factor is always negative.

### Total effects

The formula is

$$
a = \frac{b+c+d}{\sqrt{1+(b+c+d)^2}}
$$

Where $d\sim N(0,1)$ is a normally distributed random variable. 

The result range from 1 (Crossing the T (**Advantage**)) to -1 (Crossing the T (**Disadvantage**)).
