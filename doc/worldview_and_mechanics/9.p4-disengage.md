# Disengaging phase

Last for maximum 20 seconds. Refer to attack docs for attacks that can be made in this phase.

[Implemented in Battle::disengagingPhase]

## Friend and enemy night battle goal

The following process 

Our fleet either wants or does not want night battle. The enemy either wants or does not want night battle.

The enemy always have a 50/50 chance of choosing night battle or not; the player chooses via [night battle control in tactical goal](9.c2-goal-enemy.md)

| Our goal           | Enemy goal         | Result                                                                      |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Night battle       | Night battle       | Night battle is guaranteed to occur; phase have full 20 seconds             |
| Avoid night battle | Avoid night battle | Night battle is guaranteed to be avoided; phase have $y$ seconds            |
| Night battle       | Avoid night battle | phase have $(20-y)x + y$ seconds and $x$ chance to enter night battle       |
| Avoid night battle | Night battle       | phase have $(20-y)(1-x) + y$ seconds and $1-x$ chance to enter night battle |

$x$ is 

$$
\min(1, \frac{\text{los}_f\cdot\text{speed}_f}{\text{speed}_e\cdot\sqrt{\text{los}_f^2+\text{los}_e^2}})
$$

$y$ is

$$
\min(20, \frac{20\times100}{\sqrt{\text{speed}_e\text{speed}_f}})
$$

[Implemented in Battle::disengagingPhase#night-battle-decision]

## Battle assessment

Determines whether our fleet is winning or losing, used for night battle
auto-trigger rules (e.g. night battle when losing).

[Implemented in Battle::computePreliminaryAssessment]

### Night-battle-commence node

The above table is triggered at the end of night battle; but the "Night battle" in table is construed as "Day battle". No disengaing phase follows immediately after; the next phase is air battle.
