## Ranking and Industrial Plant

### Ranking points gain

[Implemented in Server::processExpGain#ranking_exp]

Any exp gained on battle (discounting flagship bonus) is added to current month's ranking exp. [MAY CHANGE IN A FUTURE RELEASE!]

### Industrial points gain

[Implemented in Server::minutePulse#award_industrial_points]

You gain industrial points every month by ranking. It is calculated as following:

$$
ln(a)+(x-1)ln(x-1)-xlnx+1
$$

$a$ is total number of players in server

$x$ is your ranking started from 1 (we regard $0ln0=0$).

This means all players gain on average 1.0 industrial points per month.

### Usage

[Implemented in Server::doBuy]

Industrial points can be used to purchase any equipment that you can develop, irrespective of its tech requirements (this means highly advanced equipment are desirable).

#### Industrial point requirements

[Implemented in Equipment::getPrice]

$$
\text{Price}=\frac{\sqrt{t+1}}{4}
$$
where $t$ is technology level.

#### Possessing limit

[Implemented in Server::doBuy#possess_limit]

If you try to gain equipment this way, maximum possessing limit in this case is global [rule/normalproductionstockpile](settings.md) rather than this equipment's specific attribute. This has the benefit of further increasing tech level and mitigate skill point loss from damaged equipment far more efficient than normal means can achieve.
