# Guided strikes

## Reconnaissance plane guided strikes

Reconnaissance planes (and to a lesser extent, other planes) can trigger guided strikes which amplifies air attack power in air battle. 

All the fleet's planes would be sorted by "Accuracy" attribute and from highest to lowest each have a chance to trigger (but once trigger is successful, the rest is not considered)

The chance is

$$
p=a\cdot\frac{b}{\sqrt{1+b^2}}
$$

where $a$ is air superiority coefficient and $b$ is accuracy value of the equipment divided by 100. If $a \leq 0$ then $p=0$.

Once successful, the amplifying effect is $b\%$.

## Midget sub guided strikes

Midget subs can trigger guided strikes which **allow for torpedo strikes in approaching phase where it's normally prohibited** (see [Approaching phase guided strikes (midget sub)](9.a3-torpedoattack.md))

All the fleet's midget subs would be sorted by "Torpedoaccuracy" attribute and from highest to lowest each have a chance to trigger (but once trigger is successful, the rest is not considered)

The chance is

$$
p=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}, x=\frac{ab}{10000}
$$

where $a$ is individual ship's "Torpedo" stats and b is "Torpedoaccuracy" value of the midget sub.
