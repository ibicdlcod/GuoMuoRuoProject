# Repair

Your ship have a repair cost (different from the construction cost, as that cost is very demanding for advanced ships), that will decide resource automatically consumed when ships are repaired by 1 HP.

[Implemented in Server::doRepair#stop_repair] Unlike in KC, repairing can be stopped even when not complete.

[Implemented in Server::doRepair#force_repair] You can also pay double resources to instantly complete repair. Resources that corresponds to time already spent is not doubled.

### Resource cost

[Implemented in Ship::repairRes]

$$
r = b\frac{t+1}{\sqrt{c^2+(t+1)^2}}h
$$

$r$: final resources

$b$: base resources [Implemented in ShipType::repairResBase]

$t$: tech level of ship

$c$: [rule/techfactorcontroller](settings.md)

$h$: difference of current hp to max hp

### Repair time

[Implemented in Ship::repairTimeInSecUnleveledPerhp]

$$
r = b\frac{t+1}{\sqrt{c^2+(t+1)^2}}h\frac{l}{\sqrt{1+(\frac{l}{25})^2}}
$$

$r$: final time

$b$: base time [Implemented in ShipType::repairTimeBase]

$l$: ship level
