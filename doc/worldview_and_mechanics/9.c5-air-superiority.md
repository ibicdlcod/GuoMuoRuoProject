# Air superiority coefficient

Air superiority coefficient affects battle in numerous ways. It replaces KC's air superiority status which can only take 5 states.

The formula is

$$
a = \frac{log(x/y)}{\sqrt{1+log(x/y)^2}}
$$

where $x$ is friend fleet's air superiority value and $y$ is enemy fleet's. Like LOS, non-night capable plane (or night carrier planes on non-night capable carrier, without night aviation personnel) is regarded non-existent in night battle.

The result range from 1 (air supremacy) to -1 (air incapability).
