# Air superiority coefficient

Air superiority coefficient affects battle in numerous ways. It replaces KC's air superiority status which can only take 5 states.

[Implemented in Battle::computeAirSuperiority, Battle::fleetAirSuperiority]

The formula is

$$
a = \frac{log(x/y)}{\sqrt{1+log(x/y)^2}}
$$

where $x$ is friend fleet's air superiority value and $y$ is enemy fleet's. Like LOS, non-night capable plane (or night carrier planes on non-night capable carrier, without night aviation personnel) is regarded non-existent in night battle.

The result range from 1 (air supremacy) to -1 (air incapability).

## Fleet air superiority value

**Equipment Air superiority** — For each equipment slot j, the equipment's LOS is
adjusted by skill-point effect and first-type visible bonus, then added:

$$
v_i \mathrel{+}= (\sqrt{a+n_j^2}-\sqrt{a}) \cdot \text{AA}_j \cdot \text{skillEff}_j \cdot \text{visBonus}_j
$$

where $n_j$ is remaining plane in the squadron. $a$ is maximum AA stats of the enemy fleet fighter equipment (if total lacking, then 0)

**Fleet Anti-air** — Collected v_i values are sorted descending. Fleet Air superiority is a weighted sum
with decay factor a (default 0.9, configurable via `rule/ascontrol`):

$$
L = \sum_{i=0}^{n}v_ia^i
$$

Ships that have fled are excluded.
