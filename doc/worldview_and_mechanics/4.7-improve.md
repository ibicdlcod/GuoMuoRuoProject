## Improvement

[Implemented in Server::modernizeEquip]

You can improve any equipment at the cost of 100% of [standard skill point](4.5-skillpoints.md) per star.

[Implemented in Server::getImprovementFactor] An equipment improved [Star] times would have its efficiency amplified by

$$
m=\frac{s}{\sqrt{s^2+1}}\cdot(\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}-\frac{1}{2}),s=\text{Star}/a
$$

times.

$a$: [rule/equipmentstandardstar](settings.md)

The maximum star for any equipment is unlimited (unlike 10 in KC).
