# Map star difficulty

The star difficulty is a concept separate from "map difficulty", with that one means three or four different tiers ("Early War", "Mid War", "Late War", "Historical"), and this one represents actual difficulty.

Normally a MapWithDiff have 0 to 16 stars (which does not always need to be an integer). In below [St] refers to star difficulty of a MapWithDiff.

In doc/Map_nodes.csv, column diffC/diffB/diffA means Star Difficulty of EarlyWar/MidWar/LateWar variant.

## Expected fleet composition tech level

This dictates ships and equipments used in testing. The builder of test fleet should choose the most powerful ships and equipment among tech 0 to [St].

## Expected pass rate

This dictates the expected pass rate (flagship sunk rate) in testing. Its value is

$$
e^{-\text{St}/10}
$$
