# ASW

## ASW detection vs concealment

Submarines have three states compared to surface ship's two: concealed, detected, and surfaced.

[Implemented in Battle::decideSubHidden, Battle::forceSurface, Battle::ConcealmentStatus]

Submarines are immune to all attacks when concealed, but enemies will try to detect her every 15 seconds.

The RNG check formula is

$$
\text{Non-detection Chance} = \frac{log(x)}{2\sqrt{1+log(x)^2}}+\frac{1}{2}, x=c/l
$$

where $c$ is concealment and $l$ is enemy fleet's (for enemy ship's hide-chance, friend fleet's) asw capability (defined in FleetInfo::asw(), which is $(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}x_i \cdot a^i)$, $x_0, ..., x_{n-1}$ is each ship's (raw asw value + any sonar's asw value) from highest to lowest, $a$ is configurable via `rule/aswcontrol` (default 0.5).

Submarines are not selectable as targets regarding gunshots (whether main, secondary, or any cut-in involving them) when not surfaced.

Submarines are not selectable as targets regarding torpedos (or any cut-in involving them) when not surfaced. This restriction is alleviated to not concealed if the attacker have a torpedo with "Asw" attribute > 0.

ASW attack, whether depth charge, aerial (or any cut-in involving them), if successful, make the submarine surface for 15 (not 30) seconds (cancelling any scheduled detection check), then detection check would run again. However, against non-ASW attack, submarines'  $\text{[concealment]}\cdot 16$ replace evasion's role.

[Implemented in Battle::forceSurface, Battle::subEffectiveEvasion]

[Implemented in Battle::forceSurface]

[NOTYETIMPLEMENTED] During night any submarine's concealment is 5x greater.

[Implemented in Battle::decideSubHidden]

## ASW depth charge attack

[Implemented in Battle::hasDepthCharge, Battle::setupDepthChargeAttacks, Battle::processDepthChargeAttack]

A ship can fire depth charge attack if it equips depth charges or its (OR ANY of its previous models) Default Equip contains depth charges. However, if it equip NEITHER sonars nor depth charges, then reloading speed have a penalty of 0.5x.

### Reloading

Does not attack in air battle.

Unless stated above, as long as any antagonist submarine is detected and the ship had not performed depth charge in last 14 seconds, she will select a detected antagonist submarine to attack.

[Implemented in Battle::setupDepthChargeAttacks]

### Damage

#### Evasion chance:

$$
a^{-\frac{b}{c}}
$$

where $b$ is attacking ship's asw (taking sonar asw into account, not other equipment), $c$ is defender's concealment.

$a=2$ in central phase/night, $a=4$ in approaching/disengaging phase.

[Implemented in Battle::processDepthChargeAttack]

#### Damage chance:

Guaranteed if not evaded.

### Damage value:

$$
\text{dmg} = r\ln(1+\frac{q}{r}), q=\frac{bc}{a}, r=\frac{h}{4}
$$

where $b$ is attacking ship's asw (taking all equipment and visible bonuses into account), $c$ is attacking ship's depth charges' asw (highest if multiple is present, 10 if none present), $h$ is antagonist sub hp. 

## ASW aerial attack

[Implemented in Battle::processAirAttack, Battle::hasPatrolPlane, Battle::collectAirSquadrons]

A ship can fire ASW aerial attack if it equips [patrol planes](4-equipment.md).

### Reloading

Same as [air attack](9.a1-airattack.md).

### Damage

#### Evasion chance:

$$
a^{-\frac{b}{c}}
$$

where b is attacking ship's asw (taking sonar asw and all patrol plane asw into account, not other equipment), c is defender's concealment.

a=3 in all phases.

#### Damage chance:

Guaranteed if not evaded.

### Damage value:

$$
\text{dmg} = r\ln(1+\frac{q}{r}), q=\frac{bc}{a}, r=\frac{h}{4}
$$

where b is attacking ship's asw (taking all equipment and visible bonuses into account), c is attacking ship's patrol planes' asw (highest if multiple is present, 10 if none present), h is antagonist sub hp.

## ASW cut-in

[Implemented in Battle::processASWCutIn (depth charge), Battle::processAirAttack (aerial)]

| Cut-in type      | Equipment 1                          | Equipment 2                                         | Equipment 3        | Damage multiplier | Accuracy multiplier |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| ASW aerial       | Autogyro ASW plane (patrol-autogyro) | Liaison ASW plane (patrol-liaison/patrol-liaison-f) |                    | 1.3               | 1.3                 |
| ASW depth charge | Sonar                                | Depth charge projector                              |                    | 1.2               | 1.2                 |
| ASW depth charge | Sonar                                | Depth charge racks                                  |                    | 1.1               | 1.1                 |
| ASW depth charge | Depth charge projector               | Depth charge racks                                  |                    | 1.12              | 1.12                |
| ASW              | Sonar                                | Depth charge projector                              | Depth charge racks | 1.43              | 1.43                |

#### Trigger mechanism

Have a chance to replace normal torpedo attack (this is different from air attack cut-in). Chance is

$$
\prod_{i=1}^2a_i, a_i=\max(0, (f+1)/2)\cdot \frac{p}{\sqrt{1+p^2}}
$$

where f is formation coefficient, p is triggerer's primary stats divided by 100. Each combination of equips in ship's equipment slots is considered; combinations that have higher damage multiplier takes precedence.

#### Damage and accuracy

Same formula as normal torpedo attacks but damage is multiplied by damage multiplier in the table, accuracy is multiplied by accuracy multiplier in the table.
