# LOS, undiscovered ships and surprise attacks

In each battle phase without clock or every 15 seconds in each battle phase with clock, each ship (whether friend or enemy) undergo an RNG check and when succeeded the ship becomes hidden and cannot be attacked upon. The next attack of it (does not apply to air attacks) become "surprise attack" and deals 1.5x more damage plus 1.5x more accurate.

The concealment check is performed by the event-driven system:

- Hidden: next check in 15 seconds, ship cannot be targeted
- Visible: next check in 30 seconds, ship can be targeted
- Force visible (after firing): next check in 30 seconds with 50% concealment

[Implemented in Battle::decideHidden, Battle::forceVisible]

The RNG check formula is

$$
\text{HideChance} = \frac{log(x)}{2\sqrt{1+log(x)^2}}+\frac{1}{2}, x=c/l
$$

where $c$ is concealment and $l$ is enemy fleet's (for enemy ship's hide-chance, friend fleet's) los (defined in FleetInfo::los, but in night battle this function have another mode which discounts ship's own LOS and non-night capable plane(`isNight() = false`) (or all planes on non-night capable carrier(`shipid & 0x000f8000 == 0x00060000`), without night aviation personnel(`equipid = 258 or 259`) on the same ship) is regarded non-existent).

## Fleet LOS value

Each ship $i$ contributes an individual LOS value $v_i$:

**Ship base LOS** — In day battle, the ship's own LOS attribute is included
(scaled by efficiency at current level/star via `FleetInfo::attrFromShip`).
In night battle, the ship's own LOS starts from 0.

**Equipment LOS** — For each equipment slot $j$, the equipment's LOS is
adjusted by skill-point effect and first-type visible bonus, then added:

$$
v_i \mathrel{+}= \text{Los}_j \cdot \text{skillEff}_j \cdot \text{visBonus}_j
$$

Day battle: equipment with special type 15 (searchlight) or 16
(starshell) are excluded.

Night battle: non-night capable planes (`isNight() = false`) are excluded.
Additionally, if a ship is a non-night capable carrier
(`shipid & 0x000f8000 == 0x00060000`) without night aviation personnel
(equip IDs 258 or 259) in any slot, **all** equipment LOS is skipped for
that ship.

**Virtual equipment bonus** — Second-type visible bonus LOS is added.

**Fleet LOS** — Collected $v_i$ values are sorted descending. Fleet LOS is a weighted sum
with decay factor $a$ (default 0.9, configurable via `rule/loscontrol`):

$$
L = \sum_{i=0}^{n}v_ia^i
$$

Ships that have fled are excluded.

[Implemented in FleetInfo::los]
