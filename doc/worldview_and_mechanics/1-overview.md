# Fleet Memories

This project is an free and open source game in the genre of ship collecting games, pioneered by Kantai Collection.

## Why we make it

We believe that Kantai Collection's event design philosophy has gone astray and becomes unsuitable for at least foreign audiences. We were attracted by KC's valuation of life, embodied by easily implemented battle decision of no ship losses. We thought this is opposite of WWII Japan's militarist spirit. But now KC's events consists of facing insurmountable odds, and (event after you have done everything right) you can only clear it through luck, sheer willpower, and help of gods (in term of repair goddesses), which is WWII militarist Japan minus actual kamikazeing. Your resources gained through hard work is spent in a heavily luck-determined manner and therefore not respected.

Another reason why we make this game is we believe Kantai Collection is service as a software substitute. We player's hard work is totally dependent on the whim of C2. Sure C2 makes this game, but we players invest heavily in it, the game is empty without the players. Therefore we intend to make this game to preserve our data in case C2 stops maintaining tKantai Collection one day.

## What this game is and is not

Existing Kantai Collection players can seamlessly import their data into this game and preserve much of their game experience. However, this is not a KC simulator, since all mechanics is redesigned and KC's ugly side is purged as much as possible. The worldview is also different (so not covered by C2's prohibition), as we battle not the Abyssal but the Amnesiac fleet. We intend to promote proper view of history and are against historical negationism.

## Migrate from Kantai Collection

[Implemented in Server::migrate]

Install poi-plugin-new-jerusalem and click import when the client is in login state. Click "export to new game" and wait for client to report "operation success".

You can neither abuse multiple imports nor lost progress. Multiple ships of the same name will be combined into one with all their experiences.
