# Decoration

[Implemented in Server::decorateShip]

Decorating a ship spends 1 medal and grants the ship one additional ring, raising her level cap by 100. Medals are purchased with ARD coupons — see [shop](10-shop.md).

## Level cap

[Implemented in Ship::expCap]

A ship with $n$ rings has a level cap of $100(n+1)$. An undecorated ship ($n=0$) is capped at level 100. Each decoration increments $n$ by one.

Higher levels increase ship efficiency — see [experience](5.7-experience.md).

## Cloning prerequisite

[Implemented in Server::decorateShip]

Reaching higher level caps is required to unlock additional cloning tiers. The $n$-th clone requires a ship that exceeds level $(n-1) \times 100$, which is only possible if the ship has been decorated at least $n-1$ times. See [cloning](5.9-cloning.md).
