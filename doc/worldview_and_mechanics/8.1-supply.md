# Supply

### Normal supply

[Implemented in Server::handleSupplyShip] Each ship has a maximum oil (fuel consumption) or explosives (ammo consumption) cost which will affect the below calculations.

[Implemented in Server::progressMap#fuel_ammo_consumption] Each battle node has a specific percentage of oil and explosives cost which will be imposed on your ships during sortie. Whirlpool may cause losses as well, but it is stochastic.

[Implemented in ShipModel::enactSupply, ShipModel::enactSupplyAll] You must resupply ships (ideally after each sortie) in order to avoid disadvantage. Unlike in KC, this process CAN be made automatic (you don't have to). Resupply is performed in the Anchorage panel, where individual ships can be selected per fuel and ammo columns, or all ships on the current page supplied at once.

[Implemented in Server::startSortie#supply_check] Ships with either 100% oil or explosives loss cannot be sortied.

### Supply chain and attrition

If your sortie map is far from any [home port](5.2-homeport.md) (any port designated as a home port that the user has unlocked, but does not include maps the user has yet to unlock) then upon end of sortie your oil and explosives will suffer further losses.

$$
\text{Attrition factor} = \prod_{i=1}^{n}s_i^{-1}-1
$$

where $s_i$ is (naval supremacy / expected supremacy, does not exceed 1) at any node on the route between any said home port and the sortie map. Expected supremacy is the same as the supremacy that [expediating](6.2-supremacy.md#Normal Expedition) the chosen difficulty will grant. The supply chain graph uses all edges from Map_relations.csv (excluding RS resource-map links); the minimum attrition across all valid home-port paths is used.

[Implemented in Sortie::recalculateAttrition, Client::loadSupplyChain] The attrition factor for the currently selected map and difficulty is computed client-side (using `mapSupremacies` and `supplyChainEdges` loaded from Map_relations.csv) and displayed in the sortie map-selection bar. If the supply line is broken (all paths pass through a node with zero supremacy) the display shows ∞; if no home port is connected to the map at all, it shows N/A.

[Implemented in Server::startSortie, Server::computeSupplyAttrition] At sortie start the server recomputes attrition from the database, rejects the sortie if the supply line is broken or infinite, checks that the player holds at least $a \times \sum_i C_i^{\text{fuel}}$ oil and $a \times \sum_i C_i^{\text{ammo}}$ explosives (where $C_i$ are the max consumption attributes of each ship in the fleet), and stores the attrition value in `UserAttr` (`Attribute = 'Attrition'`, `Realvalue`) for the duration of the sortie.

[Implemented in Server::progressMap] At each sortie node, after ship fuel/ammo fractions are deducted, the server computes the effective fleet supply cost for that node — $\sum_i \min(\text{fuel}_i,\, f_{\text{node}}) \cdot C_i^{\text{fuel}}$ in oil and $\sum_i \min(\text{ammo}_i,\, a_{\text{node}}) \cdot C_i^{\text{ammo}}$ in explosives — multiplies each by the stored attrition factor, and deducts the result (unrounded) from the player's Oil and Explosives.

### Maintenance of planes

[Implemented in PlaneReplenish::maintenanceCount()]  
Planes that survive a sortie have a chance to require maintenance.  
The number of planes needing maintenance is calculated as

$$
\text{maintenanceCount} = \operatorname{round}\!\left(
   \frac{\text{remaining}}{\sqrt{k \cdot x}}
\right)
$$
where  
– \(x = \max(1, \mathtt{equip\text{->}attr["Disallowmassproduction"]})\),  
– \(k\) is a random integer between 8 and 32 inclusive.  

Maintenance cost is rolled into the per‑100‑plane replenishment cost  
defined by `Equipment::replenishCostPer100Planes()`.
Resources are automatically deducted after each battle  
to cover both lost planes and maintenance.

### Automatic plane replenishment after battle

[Implemented in PlaneReplenish::replenishAfterBattle(), called from Server::server_battle.cpp line 1309] After each battle, lost planes are automatically replenished;
the resource cost is deducted (negative balances are allowed).
Cost is computed per equipment type, scaled from the per‑100‑plane cost and rounded up.

If this may cause your resources to drop below 0  
(calculated by 100% plane loss/maintenance,  
 no matter what actually will happen),  
you will not be allowed to sortie said map beforehand.
