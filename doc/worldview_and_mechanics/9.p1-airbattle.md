# Air battle

This phase of battle determines the [air superiority coefficient](9.c5-air-superiority.md); planes are shot down due to enemy air power (**S1**) and fleet anti-air (**S2**).

[Implemented in Battle::airBattle]

## S1 plane loss

Loss chance is $e^{-2(x+1)-y}$ where $x$ is air superiority coefficient and $y$ is the plane's "Evasion" attribute divided by 100. Each squadron calculates a binominal distribution (the loss of any plane is considered independent) rather than processing each plane separately (the same approach is used throughout the entire battle).

[Implemented in Battle::processS1PlaneLoss]

## S2 plane loss

Unlike KC, affects fighters as well.

Loss chance is $e^{4(x-1)-y+w}$ where x is enemy fleet's fleet anti-air and y is the plane's "Evasion" attribute divided by 100. $w$ is enemy fleet's total interception value divided by 100.

[Implemented in Battle::processS2PlaneLoss]

### Fleet anti-air

$$
x=\frac{y}{\sqrt{1+y^2}}, y=\frac{1}{100}\cdot\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}v_i(a-1)a^{-i}
$$

where $v_i$ is each ship's "fleet anti-air" in descending order. $a=0.8$.

[Implemented in Battle::fleetAntiAir]

#### Individual ship's fleet anti air

Mid-gun-flak, Second-gun-flak, Second-gun-flak-big, Small-gun-flak have their anti-air stats (taking visible bounses of first type into account) added to $v_i$, while AA-cannon also contribute but stats is multiplied by 0.5. Ship's bare AA stats also contribute, but stats is multiplied by 0.25.

[Implemented in Battle::individualShipFleetAA]

## Anti-air cut-in (S3)

See [equipment](4-equipment.md) for equipment that can trigger this type of cut-in.

Add an additional loss chance of enemy planes (value is $e^{8(x-1)}(1-x)^{-1}e^{-z}$) like S2 where $x$ is $\frac{y}{\sqrt{1+y^2}},y=\prod_{i=1}^{n}(1+v_n)$, where $v_i$ is anti-air stats of equipment particjipating in cut-in divided by 100 or anti-air stats of ship (taking equipment into account) divided by 400, $z$ is the plane's "Evasion" attribute divided by 100. Beware that this chance may exceed 100% (which means a guaranteed loss!)

Trigger chance is $x = \frac{y}{\sqrt{1+y^2}}$ where $y$ is anti-air stats of ship (taking equipment into account) divided by 400.

Trigger order is by anti-air stats of ship (taking equipment into account) descending.

[Implemented in Battle::processS3AACutIn]

## Long-range air attack

Each bomber squadron on any ship with remaining bombers will attempt to attack enemies once, for details see [Air attack](9.a1-airattack.md)
