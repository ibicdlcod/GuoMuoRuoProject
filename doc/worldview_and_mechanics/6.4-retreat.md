# Retreat

In this game the retreat rule is simple: you are simply not allowed to enter battle with critically damaged (<1/4 HP) ships.

However there are other case worth mentioning:

## Repair personnel/Goddess [NOTIMPLEMENTED]

In this game they are instantly consumed when your ship is below 1/4 HP, repair personnel will repair 1/4 HP (added to HP you already have) and Goddess will repair to full (and also restore condition to 480, although this is not an effective way to restore labor condition).

### Escorted retreat(Fleeing) from battlefield [NOTIMPLEMENTED]

The mobile strike force headquarters (equip 272, only when operated by a normal fleet), expeditioni force headquarters (equip 4098, only when operated by expedition fleets) and the combined fleet headquarters (equip 107, only when operated by a surface-strike/carrier-mobile/transport-escort fleet) have a common functionality: escort critically damaged ships from battle. Unlike in KC, a healthy(>=3/4HP) light cruiser may also fulfill the role of escorted ships (through destroyers will be preferred). Unlike in KC, two or more ships may be escorted out at once (but escorts must be sufficient). The headquarters must still be mounted on ship at position 1 to have an effect; she could not be escorted out nor escort either.

The Elite Torpedo Squadron Headquarters' (equip 413) single ship escape functionality is the same from Kantai Collection.

Fled ship have fuel/ammo empty and labor condition drop by 5.
