# Construction

[Implemented in Server::doConstruct]

Unlike in KC when you specify resources and may get various ship depending on many factors, in this game you must specify an ship definition and construct for that specifically.

Each ship, in addition to its blueprint[Implemented in Server::doConstruct#bp], have 1~5 initial equipment that must be provided when construct it[Implemented in Server::doConstruct#eat_default_equip]. Unlike other equipment, planes will not be consumed, only require you to possess it.

### Possess limit

[Implemented in Server::doConstruct#possess_limit]

Each ship (counting all remodel levels) can only have one present without using Cloning Vats. See [cloning](5.9-cloning.md).

### Success rate

100%

### Resource cost

[Implemented in Ship::consRes]
$$
\text{Cost} = a\text{Round}(c(t+1))
$$

$a$: base cost, varies by ship type [Implemented in Shiptype::consResBase()]

$c$: [rule/devresscale](settings.md)

$t$: ship's tech level

### Construct time

[Implemented in Ship::consTimeInSec]
$$
\text{Time(second)} = b\text{Round}(c(\frac{t^2}{\sqrt{25+t^2}}+0.1))
$$
$b$: [rule/devtimebase](settings.md)
