# Equipment skill points

Unlike in KC where only planes have skill levels, in this game all equipment have them.

Same model of equipment share skill points.

### Standard skill points

[Implemented in Equipment::skillPointsStd]

Each equipment have a standard skill point determined by its tech level:

$$
\text{Stand skill points}=ba^{t}
$$
$b$: [rule/skillpointbase](settings.md)

$a$: [rule/skillpointfactor](settings.md)

$t$: tech level

### Gain

[Implemented Server::processExpGain#equip] Base gain: equal to kanmusu that mounted this equipment (flagship bonus ignored). However actual gain is

$$
\frac{x^2}{\sqrt{a+x^2}}
$$
where $x$ is base gain and $a$ is existing skill points. Multiple equipment present in fleet will be counted multiple times.

This means early equipment is suited for early maps (no additional benefit for using it in advanced maps) and advanced equipment with high skill points is not suited for early maps (as $x$ is too low, further skill point gain is negligible, but the skill point loss risk does not change).

#### Skilled equipment

Applies to anyone whose "mother" is a real equipment, then it can transfer skill points from its "mother equipment" at a ratio of (standard skillpoints of son : standard skillpoints of mother)

#### Development fail

[Implemented in Server::doFetch#consolation_skill_points]

When failing to develop an equipment, you get

$$
\text{Consolation skill points}=\frac{s}{a}\cdot\frac{t_{current}+1}{\sqrt{(t_{equip}+1)^2+(t_{current}+1)^2}}
$$
$s$: equipment's standard skill points

$a$: [rule/penguinskillpointsdifficulty](settings.md)

$t_{equip}$: equip's tech level

$t_{current}$: your global tech level

### Loss

[Implemented in Server::calculateSkillPointDeduction, Server::shouldDamageEquipment, Server::processBattleCore, PlaneReplenish::calculateReplenishCost]

#### Non‑plane equipment damage during battle

When a ship takes HP damage (lossThisShip > 0), each non‑plane equipment mounted on that ship has a chance to be damaged. The chance depends on the ship's remaining HP ratio after the damage:

$$
\text{damage chance} = \text{base chance} \times (1 - \text{remaining HP ratio})
$$

where:

- $\text{base chance}$ = [rule/equipmentdamagebasechance](settings.md) (default 1.0 = 100%)
- $\text{remaining HP ratio}$ = $\frac{\text{new HP}}{\text{max HP}}$ clamped to [0, 1]

If the random check succeeds, one random non‑plane equipment slot on the ship is selected (including EX slot). The damaged equipment is immediately repaired, but its skill points are reduced by:

$$
\text{deduction} = \lceil \frac{\max(\text{current skill points}, 0) \times (1/a)}{100} \rceil
$$

where $a$ is the number of same‑type equipment (same definition ID) in the user's arsenal. The deduction is rounded up (minimum 1 SP if current SP > 0).

#### Plane equipment skill point loss from plane losses

When planes are lost in battle and later replenished, the corresponding plane equipment loses skill points per 100 planes shot down (regardless of whether the ship took HP damage). For each equipment definition, total plane losses are accumulated, and deductions are calculated in chunks of:

- $\text{threshold}$ = [rule/planelossdeductionthreshold](settings.md) (default 100)

The number of deduction chunks is:

$$
\text{deductions} = \lceil \frac{\text{total losses}}{\text{threshold}} \rceil
$$

For each chunk, the same deduction formula as above is applied (using the current skill points and same‑type count at the time of replenishment). The total deduction is $\text{deduction per 100} \times \text{deductions}$.

#### Skill point deduction formula common to both cases

[Implemented in Server::calculateSkillPointDeduction]

The deduction per damage event (or per 100‑plane chunk) is:

$$
\text{deduction} = \lceil \frac{\max(\text{current skill points}, 0) \times (1/a)}{100} \rceil
$$

where:

- $\text{current skill points}$ = skill points of this equipment definition for the user
- $a$ = count of same‑type equipment (same definition ID) in the user's arsenal (minimum 1)
- $\lceil \cdot \rceil$ = round up to nearest integer

The formula ensures that:

- A larger arsenal dilutes the loss (1/a term).
- Deduction is proportional to current skill points (percentage‑based).
- Zero or negative skill points yield zero deduction.
- At least 1 SP is deducted if current SP > 0 (due to ceiling).

### Effects

[Implemented in Server::getEquipSkillPointEffect]

At 0% stand skill points the equipment is 29.29% effective, at 100% points 100% effective. Maximum effectiveness (without improvement) is 129.29%. Therefore an inferior equipment with enough skill points may outperform a superior but unskilled one.

$$
\text{Efficiency} = 1-\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}+\frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\;+ m
$$

$x$: standard skill point for this equipment

$y$: actual skill point for your equipment

$m$: [improvement factor](4.7-improve.md)

total efficiency therefore cannot exceed 150%.
