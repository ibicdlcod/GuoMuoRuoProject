## Air attack damage calculation

Note that a squadron may simultaneously be a torpedo and dive bomber, during which both type of attack can occur.

### Individual anti-air

This phase is processed before any bomber attack.

Add an additional loss chance (for individual planes, the entire squadron follows binominal distribution) of enemy planes (value is $e^{16(x-1)}e^{-z}$) like S2 where $x$ is $\frac{y}{\sqrt{1+y^2}},y=\prod_{i=1}^{n}(1+v_n)$, where $v_i$ is anti-air stats of equipment or ship mentioned below divided by 100, $z$ is the plane's "Evasion" attribute divided by 100.

AA-gun have their anti-air stats (taking visible bounses of first type into account) added to v_i, while AA-cannon also contribute but stats is multiplied by 0.5. Ship's bare AA stats also contribute, but stats is multiplied by 0.25.

### Air torpedo attack (for a torpedo bomber squadron)

Damage chance (for individual planes, the entire squadron follows binominal distribution):

$$
p = \frac{e^{-x}}{\sqrt{1+e^{-2x}}}, x=\frac{v}{10a}
$$

where $v$ is evasion and $a$ is torpedoaccuracy attribute of the squadron.

Damage (for individual planes): $d=\frac{t}{\sqrt{t^2+b^2}}\cdot 16$ where $t$ is the squadron's torpedo attribute and $b$ is enemy armor.

### Air dive attack (for a dive bomber squadron)

Damage chance: $p=1$

Damage (for entire squadron): $d=max(n-\sqrt{a}, 0)\cdot 16\cdot \frac{b}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$ where $n$ is plane remaining in the squadron, $b$ is bombing attribute, $a$ is armor.

### Air attack cut-in

Unlike KC, These cut-ins does not deplete the normal attack order of bomber squadrons.

#### Trigger mechanism

Each combination of 2 squadrons is attempted to trigger, chance is $\prod_{i=1}^2a_i, a_i=\max(0, f)\cdot \frac{p}{\sqrt{1+p^2}}$ where $f$ is air superiority coefficient, $p$ is triggerer's primary stats divided by 100.

All the equipment in all of the successfully triggered cut-in is then combined into a single list for cut-in attack.
