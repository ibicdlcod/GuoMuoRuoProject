# Torpedo attacks

Workflow: when an torpedo attack is allowed to be made (any friend or enemy ship can independently make the process):

Reloading (see below)

[Target selection](9-battle.md) (Note: each squadron select target individually and sequentially, even in central phase)

Actual attack and damage (see below)

Any torpedo attack does not affect concealment.

## Attack timing

A ship is capable of torpedo attacks if it equips torpedo or any of its default equipment includes torpedo.

Normally torpedo attacks are made in disengaging phase and night battle.

### Torpedo reloading device

Torpedos that have "Firingspeed" attribute greater than 0 (meaning **battlefield reloading device**) may attack at most once in the central phase. The precise time of that timing in central phase follows $P(t>x)=e^{-x/a}$ where a is max "Torpedo" attribute of the equip in slots. (If $t>90$, then torpedo attack failed in central phase)

### Approaching phase guided strikes (midget sub)

If [Guided strikes (触接)](9.c6-guidedstrikes.md) (midget sub) is successful all ships (except submarines) that is capable of torpedo attacks may attack upon the start of the approaching phase; said attack have a chance to be evaded, calculated before the normal evasion:

$$
p=1-\frac{x}{\sqrt{4096^2+x^2}}
$$

Where $x$ is ship's torpedo stats, taking equipment into account.

### Submarine pre-emptive torpedo attack

Submarines will tirelessly reload their torpedos as long as she is concealed, not affected by what phase it is. If she is visible, the reload stops as she make emergency evasions. The reload continues as soon as she becomes concealed again.

## Attack details

### Base torpedo accuracy of ship

```
double baseTorpAcc = 250.0 * ((lv + 50.0) / 100.0)
                 / std::hypot(1.0, (lv + 50.0) / 100.0);
```

A different value is calculated for night:

```
double baseTorpAccNight = 1000.0 * ((lv + 25.0) / 100.0)
                 / std::hypot(1.0, (lv + 25.0) / 100.0);
```

### Damage

#### Evasion chance:

$$
e^{(c-b)/1000}
$$

where b is attacking ship's torpedo accuracy (base torpedo accuracy of ship + equipment accuracy), c is defender's evasion.

#### Damage value:

$$
x=\frac{wuz}{\sqrt{w^2u^2+z^2}}
$$

$u\sim N(r, r/2)$

$r=\frac{b^2}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$ where $b$ is attacking ship's torpedo stats (taking equipment into account), $a$ is defender's armor.

$z=\max(\text{HP}/8, 512)$ (HP is that of defender ship)

$w=1+0.5f$ where f is [formation efficiency](9.c7-formation.md).
