# Torpedo attacks

Workflow: when an torpedo attack is allowed to be made (any friend or enemy ship can independently make the process):

Reloading (see below)

[Target selection](9-battle.md) (Note: each squadron select target individually and sequentially, even in central phase). **Unlike other attacks, land structures and submarines are immune to torpedo attacks and cannot be selected.**

Actual attack and damage (see below)

Any torpedo attack does not affect concealment.

## Attack timing

A ship is capable of torpedo attacks if it equips torpedo or any of its default equipment includes torpedo.

Normally torpedo attacks are made in disengaging phase and night battle.

### Torpedo reloading device

Torpedos that have "Firingspeed" attribute greater than 0 (meaning **battlefield reloading device**) may attack at most once in the central phase. The precise time of that timing in central phase follows $P(t>x)=e^{-x/a}$ where $a$ is max "Torpedo" attribute of the equip in slots. (If $t>90$, then torpedo attack failed in central phase)

### Approaching phase guided strikes (midget sub)

If [Guided strikes (触接)](9.c6-guidedstrikes.md) (midget sub) is successful all ships (except submarines) that is capable of torpedo attacks may attack upon the start of the approaching phase; said attack have a chance to be evaded, calculated before the normal evasion:

$$
p=1-\frac{x}{\sqrt{4096^2+x^2}}
$$

Where $x$ is ship's torpedo stats, taking equipment into account.

(This evasion chance is intentionally very high to prevent midget subs being too powerful, both in the hands of player and enemy)

### Submarine pre-emptive torpedo attack

Submarines will tirelessly reload their torpedos as long as she is concealed, not affected by what phase it is. If she is visible, the reload stops as she make emergency evasions. The reload continues as soon as she becomes concealed again.

## Attack details

### Base torpedo accuracy of ship

```
double baseTorpAcc = 500.0 * ((lv + 25.0) / 250.0)
                 / std::hypot(1.0, (lv + 25.0) / 250.0);
```

A different value is calculated during the night:

```
double baseTorpAccNight = 1000.0 * ((lv + 25.0) / 100.0)
                 / std::hypot(1.0, (lv + 25.0) / 100.0);
```

### Damage

#### Evasion chance:

$$
e^{(c-b)/1000}
$$

where $b$ is attacking ship's torpedo accuracy (taking equipment into account), $c$ is defender's evasion.

#### Damage value:

$$
x=\frac{w\max(u,0)z}{\sqrt{w^2u^2+z^2}}
$$

$u\sim N(r, r/2)$

$r=\frac{b^2}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$ where $b$ is attacking ship's torpedo stats (taking equipment into account), $a$ is defender's armor.

$z=\max(\text{HP}/8, 512)$ (HP is that of defender ship)

$w=1+0.5f$ where f is [formation efficiency](9.c7-formation.md).

Gunshot cut-in

### Plain Torpedo cut-in

| Cut-in type | Equipment 1       | Equipment 2         | Damage multiplier | Accuracy multiplier |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Torpedo     | Torpedo           | Torpedo             | 1.1               | 1.2                 |
| Torpedo     | Submarine Torpedo | Submarine Equipment | 1.15              | 1.25                |

#### Trigger mechanism

Have a chance to replace normal torpedo attack (this is different from air attack cut-in). Chance is

$$
\prod_{i=1}^2a_i, a_i=\max(0, (f+1)/4)\cdot \frac{p}{\sqrt{1+p^2}}
$$

where f is formation coefficient, p is triggerer's primary stats divided by 100). Each combination of equips in ship's equipment slots is considered; combinations that have higher damage multiplier takes precedence.

#### Damage and accuracy

Same formula as normal torpedo attacks but damage is multiplied by damage multiplier in the table, accuracy is multiplied by accuracy multiplier in the table.

### Gun-Torpedo cut-in

| Cut-in type | Equipment 1   | Equipment 2 | Damage multiplier | Accuracy multiplier |
| ----------- | ------------- | ----------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Torpedo     | Main gun*     | Torpedo     | 1.07              | 1.2                 |
| Torpedo     | Secondary gun | Torpedo     | 1.02              | 1.2                 |

"Main gun" here don't include big-caliber main guns.

#### Trigger mechanism

Have a chance to replace normal gun or torpedo attack. Chance is

$$
\prod_{i=1}^2a_i, a_i=\max(0, (f+1)/2)\cdot \frac{p}{\sqrt{1+p^2}}
$$

where f is formation coefficient, p is triggerer's primary stats divided by 100 (or 50 for secondary gun). Each combination of equips in ship's equipment slots is considered; combinations that have higher damage multiplier takes precedence.

#### Damage and accuracy

Execute the effect of **a simultaneous gun+torpedo attack** (the gun attack type is that of the triggerer) but damage is multiplied by damage multiplier in the table, accuracy is multiplied by accuracy multiplier in the table.
